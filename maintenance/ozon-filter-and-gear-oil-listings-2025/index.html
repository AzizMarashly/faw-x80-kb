    Ozon filter and gear oil listings (2025)

    Tags: #air-filter · #cabin-filter · #oil-filter · #mazda-6-compat

    Prices and suppliers on this page are Russian-market (RUB, at the dates shown). Verify locally before relying on them in Syria.

    Air filter cross-references (Ozon, Sep 2025). Three air filters were listed for the FAW X80 and Mazda 6: RF4F13Z409A (the Mazda original number given in the listing), CAREADY C402011029 (X80 from 2016, 2.0 engine; Mazda 6 GH 2007-2013, GG and GY 2002-2008) and TSN 9.1.620PU (Mazda 6 GG from 2002, MPV II LW).123 Compare the other numbers on the filter part numbers card.

    ZEKKERT kit (Ozon, Oct 2025). A ZEKKERT filter set for the FAW Besturn X80 combines the air filter, oil filter and a carbon cabin filter in one kit.4 Earlier kit prices are on the filter part numbers card.

    RD5 oil filter (Ozon, Oct 2025). An RD5 oil filter was listed for Mazda 3 (BM/BP), Mazda 6 (GJ/GL), CX-5, CX-3, CX-30, MX-5 and Mazda 2; the listing does not confirm the fit for the X80, so verify before buying.5

    Scented cabin (salon, салонный) filter. A scented cabin filter listed on Ozon for FAW Bestune X80 1.8T B50, B70 and B90 and Mazda 6 I/II was reported by the buyer to fit exactly and work as intended, with the cabin smell gone; he ordered three and expected a delivery wait of a couple of weeks.67 Other cabin filter options are on the cabin air filter alternatives card.

    Gear oil listing (Ozon, Sep 2025). Petro-Ofisi MAXIGEAR EP 75W-80 transmission oil, 1 L, was listed on Ozon; the viscosity matches the 75W-80 GL4 spec described on the manual gearbox fluid card, but the listing gives no API class here, so check it before use.8
