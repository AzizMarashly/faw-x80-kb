    Scheduled maintenance pricing — dealer vs. chain

    Tags: #price · #dealer

    Prices and suppliers on this page are Russian-market (RUB, at the dates shown). Verify locally before relying on them in Syria.

    Autopilot service chain (Moscow/Moscow region), 2024-10-16 report: - Full oil change: free. - Air + cabin filter replacement: minimum 1,200 RUB (RU market, October 2024).12 - One owner's total for a complete service (all filters + oil) at a different, unnamed shop: 9,600–10,000 RUB (RU market, October 2024).3 - Another owner's total at yet another shop ("filter service"): ~6,200 RUB (RU market, October 2024).45

    Regional/shop variation is significant; Autopilot's advertised free oil change plus low-cost filters made it cheaper than some competitors for that specific owner, but total costs across the community range widely depending on shop and oil brand used.123456

    Official FAW dealer ТО-2 (2nd scheduled maintenance), Nikolaev, Ukraine, invoice dated 2024-10-31: - Total: ~8,000 UAH (Ukrainian dealer, October 2024; approximately 20,000 RUB equivalent at the time, RU market conversion).789 - Includes: engine oil change, filter change, belt inspection, and miscellaneous adjustments.8 - Owner's own estimate for doing the same work themselves (DIY labor + parts): 1,500–2,000 UAH (≈3,500–5,000 RUB, RU market conversion, October 2024) — i.e., roughly a quarter of the dealer price. The owner noted this was likely their last dealer-serviced visit, opting to do it themselves after the warranty period ends.1011

    Authorized dealer booking, AutoGermes (Nizhny Novgorod), Dec 2024 report. Authorized FAW dealer offers X80 service and parts sales. Initial diagnostic fee: 4,500 RUB (RU market, December 2024). Expect appointment delays during peak season. Dealer performs standard maintenance including oil changes and a technical wash.121314

    Dealer service quality issues — inadequate oil changes (Apr 2025 report). Community members report recurring quality problems at authorized dealer service: over-tightening drain plugs and filter housings, failing to remove undercar protection panels for proper servicing (later requiring welding/cutting when the owner attempts repairs), not actually changing the oil filter despite documented service claims, and questionable spark-plug replacement work. Doing an oil change through the dipstick access alone (without removing the drain plug/filter) is considered inadequate. Practical tip: photograph the filter/parts before and after a dealer visit as a check.15161718

    Official FAW dealer package, Sormovskaya location, Russia (2025-08-29). A single quoted service package — engine oil change, oil filter, air filter, and cabin air filter replacement — came to 20,000+ RUB.1920

    Typical non-premium shop pricing, Krasnodar region (2025-10). | Service | Cost | Notes | |---|---|---| | Oil change, no shield removal | 600–700 RUB | — | | Oil change, with shield removal | 800 RUB | Shop tried to upsell an unnecessary air filter replacement.21 | | Spark plug replacement | ~1,000 RUB | Per service appointment; separately, higher-end aftermarket plugs bought at ~1,000 RUB each were reported to fail within a year.222324 | | Antifreeze replacement | ~1,500 RUB | — | 2122

    60,000 km major service cost comparison (2026-04 report). At the dealership, a full 60,000 km service (oil, filter, antifreeze, spark plugs, air filter) runs about 27,000 RUB (RU market, April 2026); the same scope at a certified multi-brand (non-dealer) service center costs around 17,000 RUB (RU market, April 2026). Note: the dealer's oil-change labor alone is billed separately and was reported as approximately 2,700 RUB (RU market, April 2026) (a figure the owner initially mis-typed as "27 RUB"). Bringing your own oil and an original filter to the dealer can cut roughly 10,000 RUB (RU market, April 2026) off the dealer total.25262728

    Suspension-related repair labor costs, Saint Petersburg (2026-04 report). A Saint Petersburg service center quoted 83,040 RUB (RU market, April 2026) for a drive-shaft-related repair job, itemized as: 4× drive-shaft ("привод") repairs at 1,600 RUB each, plus 2× front control-arm bushing/cradle blocks (part MZAB-016) at 1,900 RUB each, with total materials around 43,000 RUB and the balance being labor/markup. For comparison, separately-reported rates in the same city for related jobs: rear hub removal/installation 7,920 RUB (RU market, April 2026) (vs. a typical local market rate closer to ~1,500 RUB (RU market, April 2026), described as a ~20-minute job); control-arm bushing press-out/press-in (перепрессовка сайлентблока) priced similarly high. Community consensus: these dealer/shop prices run roughly 30–40% inflated versus private mechanics — searching Avito for a well-reviewed garage-based mechanic was the suggested alternative for routine work. Note: warranty on shop-performed work only covers defective labor if the customer did not supply their own parts.2930313233343536
