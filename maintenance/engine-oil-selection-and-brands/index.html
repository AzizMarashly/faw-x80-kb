    Engine oil selection — grades and brands

    Tags: #engine-oil · #price

    Prices and suppliers on this page are Russian-market (RUB, at the dates shown). Verify locally before relying on them in Syria.

    Some advice here concerns sub-zero winters and is less relevant in a hot climate.

    Official dealer fill: LUKOIL GENESIS SPECIAL SPX 5W40.12345678910111213141516

    Grade recommendation. Multiple owners successfully use LUKOIL GENESIS ARMORTECH — both 5W-30 and 5W-40 (fully synthetic, API SP/CF, sold under the "SP-RC" classification in some listings). Common recommendation: 5W-40 for summer, 5W-30 for winter, to reduce engine noise and improve cold-start behavior. Several owners who switched from 5W-40 (red bottle) to 5W-30 (green bottle) for winter reported a noticeably quieter engine; one owner at 50,000 km made this switch and confirmed the quieter running.1718192021222324252627282930 The community notes some mixed results with the ARMORTECH CN 5W-40 (red) purchased from Wildberries, with concerns about counterfeits circulating on that channel — verify the API classification (SP/CF) printed on the canister and buy from authorized channels where possible.12345678910111213141516

    Base stock / Euro 5 note. Major lubricant manufacturers (Mobil, Shell, Lukoil, Idemitsu) supply base oils that get repackaged under multiple brands. Euro 5 standards require specific oil characteristics matched by the additive package per manufacturer specification — cheaper "synthetic" oils may lack a proper additive package for genuine Euro 5 compliance. The X80 may use oils built on Lukoil base stock formulations, the same base stock reportedly used in some Lexus/Mercedes applications. Always cross-check the owner's manual for the required viscosity and performance spec before buying on price alone.31323334

    Brand experience — Mobil vs. alternatives (conflict documented). One experienced owner (238,000 km) reports Mobil performs best on the X80: quieter engine, smoother cold start at 0°C, faster warm-up than Repsoil and Volvoil (both tried and found inferior). Oil darkening after ~1,000 km was observed and considered normal, showing good carbon-cleaning ability. On a previous Nissan, this owner got 8,000 km on Mobil without needing to top up, versus needing a top-up every 2,000 km on other oils tried. Conflict: other community members are satisfied using LUKOIL instead of Mobil — preference may be partly subjective, though Mobil is specifically praised here for cold-start behavior and reduced noise.353637

    KIXX 5W-40 consumption issue. One owner reports KIXX 5W-40 showing excessive oil consumption (noticeably dropping level) at high mileage (115,000 km+). Comma XFlow 5W-40 is recommended by the community as a superior alternative with lower consumption in the same conditions.383940

    Korson brand. One owner in Siberia recommends Korson motor oil, reporting acceptable oil-level retention between changes through winter driving, at a cost of roughly 40,000–50,000 RUB per oil-change interval (RU market, April 2024) (bulk purchase context unclear). The same oil was also tested by this owner on a Toyota Vitz (170,000 km) without smoking or burning issues.414243

    Takayama Adaptec — another commonly used brand (2025-03 report). Takayama Adaptec oils are commonly used by X80 owners: 5W-30 (API SP, ILSAC GF-6A) and 5W-40 (API SN/CF, ACEA A3/B4). Pricing: Takayama Adaptec 5W-40 synthetic, 5L, 2,536 RUB (4+1 promotion, originally 5,200 RUB, Russian market, March 2025). Syntek is another popular brand with owners reporting reliable performance at 5,000–6,000 km change intervals. Typical oil prices for a 4L set range 1,900–2,300 RUB (RU market, March 2025). Note: Rowe 9000 (multi-spec) exists on the market but may have compatibility issues with the AKPP automatic transmission fluid if cross-used — verify spec before substituting.44454647484950

    Oil condition at 6,000 km — Suntec 9000 5W30 (2025-07 report). One owner's dipstick check showed Suntec 9000 5W30 A3/B4 still dark/clean-looking after a 6,000 km interval, with the engine (53k total km) in good condition and normal wear patterns visible.51

    Consumption comparison across brands (2025-07 report). Sintec oil was reported to cause engine deposits after 4+ years of continuous use (considered detrimental long-term). VMP 10W-40 showed minimal consumption over 8,000 km change intervals. ELF and Motul (quality-tier brands) performed well. Lukoil, used for 6 years by one owner, left higher deposits but the engine still worked fine. Note: oil-level drop at sustained high speed (110+ km/h) and high sustained temperature is normal evaporation, not necessarily engine oil burn-down — cheaper oils tend to show worse consumption patterns overall.52535455565758596061626364656667686970

    More brand reports (2025-08, community poll). Recommended options mentioned in an August 2025 discussion: Genesis Special SPX 5W-40 (repeatedly recommended, matches the official dealer fill noted above), Idemitsu (~5,000 RUB/4L, RU market, August 2025), Lukoil, Gazprom, and ZIC X7 (one owner has run it continuously since the initial "0" service). Mirax 0W-30 was named as a very cheap option (1,750 RUB/5L, RU market, August 2025). A specific purchase example: one owner bought ZIC X7 in November 2023 for 1,200 RUB/liter (RU market, Ozon, seller UPEC) and reported excellent engine quietness after 7,700 km, at 55,000 km total engine mileage. Community advice repeated in this thread: verify the oil meets the FAW specification and suits the engine's mileage before buying.717273747576777879808182

    GENESIS SPECIAL SPX 5W-40 — separate OEM confirmation (2025-09 report). A community member reports that official Mazda dealerships in Moscow use the same GENESIS SPECIAL SPX 5W-40 oil noted above as the FAW dealer fill, reinforcing it as an OEM-level-approved choice; multiple owners report "all good" results with this viscosity grade.83

    Totachi — metal-canister recommendation (2025-09 report). Totachi oil in metal canisters is recommended by community members as a way to sidestep counterfeiting risk: metal containers are harder and more expensive to counterfeit than plastic ones. One owner notes Totachi is "significantly cheaper" than some premium brands (naming "Luckily" as one comparison) with comparable perceived durability; another recommends metal-can Totachi specifically for its 1,500–2,000 RUB price point.848586

    NGN — dual-use engine and gearbox oil (2025-09 report). NGN, a Russian-manufactured oil, is used by at least one owner in both the engine and the gearbox ("in the motor and the box"). Available on Ozon.ru.8788

    TEBOIL DIAMOND pricing (2025-09/20 report). TEBOIL DIAMOND 5W-30 synthetic (Finland), 4L: 821 RUB on Ozon using seller promotional points (250-point discount, Sept 20 2025) — versus a standard price of 1,670 RUB noted two weeks earlier. TEBOIL DIAMOND FS 5W-30 (A5/B5 spec) is also available for owners who prefer that specification. Sold by an official Ozon seller matching Teboil's own website store.899091

    Counterfeit risk and sourcing (2025-10 report). Counterfeit motor oil is common on marketplaces (Ozon, WildBerries) — reported fakes include Genesis, Lukoil, and Kixx. Symptoms of counterfeit oil: unusually thin viscosity, excessive consumption (one report of ~200 g per 3,000 km), and rough/hard engine running. Community recommendation: buy from authorized gas-station retailers or the official channels listed on the manufacturer's own website — cost is higher, but counterfeit risk is essentially eliminated.9293949596

    More brand/price data points (2025-10/11 reports). SINTEC ExtraLife 7000 5W-30 (SL/CF, synthetic, 4L) and the heavier 5W-40 (SN/CF) variant are both available on WildBerries with periodic manufacturer discounts. LUKOIL Armortech CN 5W-40 (synthetic, 4L) was seen at 1,199 RUB (RU market) planned for a November 2025 Ozon promotion. General advice: compare WildBerries/Ozon prices against gas-station prices before buying.979899100 Separately, in November 2025: Ozon pricing around ~4,000 RUB, Avtorusy around ~5,700 RUB (RU market); Belarusian Rosneft oil was recommended as a budget alternative (one owner runs it every 7–10 thousand km), and Obninsk-brand oil was cited as roughly 3× cheaper than metal-can premium oils. Reinforcing the counterfeit-risk note above: the community favors metal cans over plastic for lower counterfeiting risk.101102103104105106

    TOTACHI Hyper Ecodrive 5W-30 (API SP / ILSAC GF-6), conflict documented (2025-11 report). TOTACHI Hyper Ecodrive 5W-30 synthetic (4L) is recommended by multiple owners with good engine performance and no reported oil consumption.107108109110 Conflict: one owner (Сергей Надёжкин) tried it and reported a louder cold start and oil consumption, then switched to a different brand — contradicting the otherwise-positive community experience with this oil.111

    VMPAUTO modern SP 5W-30 — alternative brand (2025-11 report). VMPAUTO modern SP 5W-30 synthetic (5L) is available on Ozon and recommended by one community member as an alternative option.112113

    Kixx and Zic — two more owners confirm reliability (2026-02 report). One owner has used Kixx 5W-30 SN for about two years with no issues; Kixx G1 5W-30 was priced around 2,835 RUB on Ozon (RU market, Feb 2026). Zic was also mentioned as a working alternative in the same thread. Community view: with regular interval discipline, the specific reputable brand matters less than sticking to the schedule.114115116117

    MANNOL Energy Formula FR 5W-30 — 3-year track record (2026-02 report). A separate owner reports 3 years of continuous use of MANNOL Energy Formula FR 5W-30 fully-synthetic oil with no oil consumption or problems; multiple other community members confirmed similarly good long-term reliability with this oil on X80 engines.118119120121

    Reported oil spec — "Chery 5W-30", Mobil 3309 as an alternative (2026-03 report). One owner names "Chery 5W-30" as their recommended engine oil, with Mobil 3309 cited as an acceptable alternative. Note this "3309" reference overlaps with the ATF-spec figure used elsewhere in the KB for AKPP/power-steering fluid — treat this specific engine-oil mention with caution and verify against the owner's manual before buying.122

    Viscosity selection by mileage — community rule of thumb (2026-03 report). Recommended approach: 5W-30 for lower-mileage engines (under ~90,000 km) or an engine in good condition; switch to 5W-40 at higher mileage (over ~130,000 km) if the engine starts consuming oil slightly. Any synthetic meeting SN+ or SP is considered acceptable — Lukoil, VMP, and Polimerium were named as the most consistently reliable domestic options. Given the X80's naturally-aspirated, low-compression, older-design engine, the community view is that expensive premium branding matters less than matching the correct spec and viscosity to mileage. Conflict: one owner reported valve-tap noise on cold start after switching to 5W-40 at 90,000 km, suggesting the switch-over point may need to be later or more conservative for some engines.123124125126127128129130131132133134135

    FAW genuine 5W-40 (G0540SNA40C) — one reported issue (2026-03 report). FAW's own-brand synthetic 5W-40, OEM part number G0540SNA40C, sells for about 1,789 RUB on Ozon (RU market, March 2026). One owner reports this specific oil caused audible valve-tap noise at engine start on their car — consistent with the wider community debate above about the 5W-30-to-5W-40 switch-over point.136137138

    NGN 5W-30 — additional positive review (2026-03 report). A second, independent report on NGN 5W-30 synthetic (see also the dual-use engine/gearbox note above) describes quieter engine operation and an appropriate viscosity feel after switching to it; available on Ozon.139

    Budget synthetic sighting, repeat data point (May 2026). The Ozon-sourced Finnish synthetic oil noted above (TEBOIL DIAMOND, old pre-2022 stock) was again seen at 821 RUB (RU market) in May 2026, corroborating the September 2025 price point — the seller is described as an official store. A separate owner mentioned a rough per-liter price benchmark of roughly 1.5 RUB/ml (~1,500 RUB/L) (RU market, May 2026) for comparison shopping.140141

    Engine oil capacity and fill-quantity practice (2026-04 report). Spec capacity is 4.3 L. In practice, a 4.0–4.3 L range is considered acceptable — many owners run a single 4L canister with no top-up and report no issues or excess consumption. Regional guidance: in hotter climates, follow the full 4.3 L spec rather than the lower end of the range (an owner who under-filled and drove hard between changes pulled a dry-ish dipstick reading slightly below minimum, though nothing critical resulted). One owner successfully ran Totachi HYPER EcoDrive 5W-30 (API SP, ILSAC GF-6A, marketed as gasoline/diesel-compatible, 100% claimed original) with no issues after extended use.142143144145146147148149150151152153154155

    Market price snapshot for 5W-30 grades, X80 2.0 NA (2026-06 report). Recommended grade for the naturally-aspirated 2.0: 5W-30, SL or better. Market prices as of June 2026 (RU market): Gazprom Neft 5W-30 A5 (seasonal/winter option); Teboil 5W-30 SL (Finland) — previously ~820 RUB/4L on promotion (RU market; as of the June 2026 report) (corroborating the earlier TEBOIL DIAMOND price points above); Takayama Adaptec 5W-30 synthetic (metal canister, higher cost — corroborates the Takayama brand already documented above); Mirax MX7 (API SP, ACEA A5/B5) 5W-30 at 1,700 RUB/5L (a simpler/cheaper spec); Liqui Moly 5W-30 molybdenum at approximately 8,000 RUB total including oil + filter replacement labor. One owner (Victor) advises against GF-5-only oils, citing shorter service life (~5,000–7,000 km). Many owners in this thread mix brands experimentally rather than sticking to one.156157158159160161162163164165

    API/ACEA specification compliance — false-economy warning (2026-06 report). Using a motor oil that meets the X80 engine's actual API/ACEA specification matters — an oil that works fine in a different brand's engine is not automatically safe here if it doesn't meet the X80's requirements (Liqui Moly 5W-30 was cited as an example needing proper spec matching). Skipping spec compatibility to save money is considered false economy by the community — non-compliant oil risks engine damage. Typical all-in cost for a properly spec'd oil change (oil + filter + labor): ~7,000–8,000 RUB (RU market, June 2026).165

    Consumption comparison — VMP Avto, Tatneft, Taneco (2024-03 report). One owner reports VMP Avto premium synthetic (~2,800 RUB/5L, RU market, March 2024) shows essentially zero consumption over 6,000 km. By contrast, Tatneft conventional oil showed a level drop requiring top-up after only ~5,000 km on the same owner's car. Taneco Premium Ultra Eco Synth 5W-30 was separately rated positively in independent oil-club.ru forum testing. Community 4-liter price range at the time: 2,700–3,150 RUB (RU market, March 2024). General takeaway: premium synthetic is preferable for owners running longer change intervals, since different oils show meaningfully different burn/consumption rates even within a single ownership.166167168169170171172

    Lukoil Genesis ArmorTech 5W-40 — "for Chinese cars" formulation (2024-08 report). Lukoil Genesis ArmorTech 5W-40, marketed specifically as formulated for Chinese-brand vehicles (labeled "FOR CHINESE CARS"/CN designation on the bottle), meets API SP/CF and ACEA A3/B4. Multiple owners report satisfactory performance; Lukoil's published spec sheet cites a high TBN (total base number) of 9.94 mg KOH/g, supporting good anti-wear and detergency performance. Well-received by the community as a reliable OEM-equivalent choice — this is a separate product from the GENESIS SPECIAL SPX 5W-40 already documented above as the official dealer fill.173174175176177178179

    Additional recommended-brands roundup (2024-08 report). Further community-reported oils with good results, supplementing the brand list already on this card: Valvoline, Liqui Moly, Rowe, Rein Well; VMPAUTO 5W-30 (synthetic, reported zero sludge); Comma XFlow (corroborates the earlier Comma XFlow recommendation above); Zic X5 5W-40; Totachi EcoDrive 5W-40 (sealed tin, imported via Kazakhstan from Japan, higher cost, reported good engine performance — a separate product line from Totachi HYPER EcoDrive noted above). Previously popular but now harder to find: Shell Eco 5W-40; long-standing standard: Idemitsu 5W-30 in a tin container. Brands the community advises avoiding: Gazpromneft and Kixx, both cited for high oil burn (corroborating the earlier KIXX 5W-40 consumption complaint documented above). Typical seasonal range recommended: 5W-30 to 5W-40 depending on availability.180181182183184185186187

    Further brand corroboration and a 195,000 km reliability anecdote (2026-01 report). Lukoil Armortech JP 5W-30 synthetic was named directly by one owner as their go-to oil, reporting "no problems" with it;188189190 a separate owner at 195,000 km on regular oil changes reports having done no additional engine cleaning work in that entire distance beyond a throttle-body service, corroborating the "regular oil changes prevent major work" pattern documented elsewhere on this card;191 and two more owners name their own working choices in the same thread — any decent-brand 5W-40 works fine for one owner,192 while another runs Mobil 5W-30, noting 5W-40 is presumably thicker/heavier.193 Separately, Genesis 5W-30 for Ford was named by one owner as the best oil option they'd found for the X80, reporting excellent in-vehicle performance.194 Low-confidence, single-source anecdote on engine robustness: the same owner reports accidentally mixing roughly 15 liters of diesel into the gasoline tank and driving on the mixture with no engine problems — offered as informal evidence of engine tolerance, not a recommended practice.195
