    AKPP auxiliary cooling radiator installation

    Tags: #automatic-transmission · #cooling · #price

    Prices and suppliers on this page are Russian-market (RUB, at the dates shown). Verify locally before relying on them in Syria.

    AKPP auxiliary cooling upgrade, Crimea, April 2024: - Auxiliary radiator: 7,000 RUB (RU market, April 2024). - Additional hose: 1,500 RUB (RU market, April 2024). - Adapter/переходник to the heat exchanger: 4,500 RUB (RU market, April 2024). - Extra transmission fluid (~1.5 L) included in the cost. - Labor: 2,000 RUB (RU market, April 2024). - Total: approximately 16,000 RUB (RU market, April 2024), performed at a specialist AKPP repair shop.12

    Motivation — preventive fitment around 80,000 km (2024-08 report). Some owners add an auxiliary AKPP cooling radiator around 80,000 km as a preventive measure, rather than waiting for symptoms. The motivation cited: community reports of an AKPP failure at 165,000 km attributed to overheating on a car without an auxiliary cooler. This preventive-fitment reasoning is separate from (and complements) the cost breakdown above; see the ATF fluid and intervals card for the fuller partial-vs-full fluid-change context this recommendation appeared alongside.34567891011
