    AKPP fluid level check — plug, tube, temperature

    Tags: #automatic-transmission · #atf · #drain-plug · #temperature

    Prices and suppliers on this page are Russian-market (RUB, at the dates shown). Verify locally before relying on them in Syria.

    Setting and checking the ATF level: the thin-stream test at the drain plug, the measuring/overflow tube, working temperatures and part numbers.

    Fluid level check — from below only (2025-05 report). Check transmission fluid level only from underneath the car. With the engine running and fluid at operating temperature (35–45°C), unscrew the drain plug/pan: a thin steady drip means correct level; no fluid/only a trickle means underfilled; fluid pouring out like an open faucet means overfilled. The car must be level (not on a slope) before checking.1234

    Drain-plug + measuring-cup/tube sequence (2026-05 report). To drain: (1) unscrew the drain plug first — a small stream begins; (2) unscrew the measuring cup/tube located at the same point — a larger stream follows. The measuring tube may be plastic and wears with age; the metal, opaque cup portion serves as the oil-measurement indicator. One community member noted the tube may be integrated with the pan on some variants.5678

    Measuring/dipstick tube (мерная трубка) — sourcing, part numbers, and a contradiction worth flagging (2026-06 report). Despite the "X80 has no transmission dipstick" claim documented in the partial-change procedure card, later community threads describe and source a measuring/level tube for the 09G AISIN automatic by name — treat the two claims as reflecting different transmission variants/model years until reconciled. Sourcing options reported: (1) Avito marketplace, searching "09G AISIN measuring tube"; (2) abtomat.ru, part code 15765DT; (3) RuLi/AliExpress auto-parts listings. Tube length varies — three different lengths exist (41mm, 46mm, 50mm); the X80 is reported to need the smallest (~35–41mm depending on the specific report). Ozon pricing (RU market, June 2026): approximately 1,500 RUB. A partial-drain service that doesn't remove the pan cannot access this tube — the tube stays stuck/inaccessible from the top; a full pan removal is required for any service that involves replacing it.9101112131415161718192021222324

    Fluid check without pan removal — 3L capacity confirmation (2024-08 reports). For drain-and-fill without removing the pan, capacity is approximately 3 liters. Level check via the drain tube: fluid should flow slightly, not drip, when cold; excessive flow indicates overfill. One community member's clarifying note: the tube used for level-check/refill without pan removal is the drain/fill tube, not a true dipstick — draining and topping off by level is done through it.252627282930

    Two distinct level-checking methods — side plug vs. tube in pan (2026-08 report). Валерий raised a question in the community: he had learned there are two different ways owners check the АКПП fluid level on this Aisin-type automatic — via the side plug (боковая пробка) or via a tube inside the pan (трубочка в поддоне) — and asked which one is actually correct for the X80.31 Che Guevara replied that a service station ("сто") told him the tube-in-pan method is specifically what's used to set/establish the correct fluid level (i.e., it is the calibrated reference, not just an alternative check point).32 Konstantin pushed the reasoning further: if the side plug alone were the correct check, there would be no purpose in having a tube in the pan at all — implying the side plug is not a reliable stand-alone level check on this transmission.33 Caution: treat the side plug as a drain/access point only, not as the level-setting reference — using it to judge "full" without accounting for the tube-in-pan calibration risks overfilling the transmission. This exchange has not been independently corroborated beyond the three participants; treat as a documented community discussion rather than a settled procedure.
