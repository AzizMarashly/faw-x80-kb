    Transmission fluid change intervals and costs

    Tags: #maintenance-interval · #conflict · #automatic-transmission · #transmission-fluid · #price

    Prices and suppliers on this page are Russian-market (RUB, at the dates shown). Verify locally before relying on them in Syria.

    Community members disagree on this topic — both positions are reported.

    Official guidance vs dealer advice (AKPP). The factory service manual specifies only an oil-level check at 30,000 km intervals — no scheduled full oil change is mentioned 1. Dealership-recommended intervals vary but generally suggest a partial change at 60,000 km and a full change at 120,000 km 2. This is a discrepancy between OEM documentation (level checks only, no mandated change interval) and dealer service recommendations (60k/120k km) 12. Some owners prefer earlier maintenance, doing partial changes as soon as 50,000 km 2, so actual practice depends on each owner's reading of the manufacturer's guidance versus the dealer's. For fluid specification and procedure see the ATF fluid and intervals card.

    Community practice — 50,000 km interval. Multiple owners converge on a rule of thumb: change the automatic-transmission fluid roughly every 50,000 km and the AKPP holds up well with no reliability problems, including under highway driving with confident acceleration 345. One owner's detailed account: at 105,000 km all the bushings and bearings had worn out but the transmission itself continued to hold up fine, with no overheating even under aggressive and highway driving; a full bearing/bushing/brake-disc job at that mileage cost about 60,000 RUB (RU market, July 2025) 6. Community sentiment favors AKPP over manual for comfort when this maintenance interval is followed 3.

    AKPP oil filter — clean and reuse. The automatic-transmission oil filter is metal (not the paper type used in many other vehicles), so instead of buying a replacement at each service it can simply be washed and thoroughly cleaned and reused — a straightforward way to reduce recurring maintenance costs 7.

    ACVT (CVT-type) variant — service and cost. This applies to the ACVT (CVT-type) automatic transmission variant, distinct from the more commonly discussed Aisin AKPP torque-converter automatic above. At 200,000 km one owner had a filter change plus partial fluid replacement, with 3.5–4.5 liters of fluid extracted: labor 3,000 RUB, oil ~1,200 RUB/liter × 3 liters = 3,600 RUB, total ~6,600 RUB (RU market, Oct 2024) 89. A follow-up service at 240,000 km extracted approximately 3 liters 89. The fluid cited was Genesis Special SPX 5W-40 — note this is an engine-oil-branded product line being referenced for the CVT service; confirm the CVT-specific fluid coding with your supplier before use, since CVT fluids require a specific formulation distinct from engine oil or standard ATF 89.
