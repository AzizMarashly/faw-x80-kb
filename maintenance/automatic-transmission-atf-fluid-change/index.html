    Automatic transmission (AKPP) — ATF fluid & intervals

    Tags: #automatic-transmission · #atf · #aisin · #transmission-fluid · #engine-oil

    Prices and suppliers on this page are Russian-market (RUB, at the dates shown). Verify locally before relying on them in Syria.

    Overview. The Aisin G09 / 09G (TF60/TF80) automatic transmission (AKPP) of the X80 takes ATF to the JWS-3309 spec. The factory manual calls the fill lifetime, but owners overwhelmingly change it: usually a partial change (about 3–4 L drained) every 30,000–40,000 km, with a deeper pan-and-mesh service at the first major service.     Factory position vs. community practice. The factory manual states the transmission is "sealed for life" with no scheduled oil change. Community consensus strongly disagrees: because the Aisin transmission has an inherent overheating tendency and is sensitive to oil quality, dirty oil clogs the hydraulic block and its fine channels/solenoid pistons, reducing pressure on the friction-pack clutch cylinders and accelerating clutch degradation.12345

    Change interval — disagreement documented. There is real disagreement in the community between owners who prioritize the manufacturer's spec (60,000 km or "lifetime") and owners with empirical evidence favoring more frequent partial changes: - Олег Белоусов maintains the manufacturer specs (60k/lifetime) are the engineering baseline and should be trusted. - KASPER and others counter with real-world examples of extended transmission life (700,000+ km reported) achieved with frequent partial fluid changes every 20,000–40,000 km.6789101112131415161718192021222324252627 - The practical middle-ground that most experienced owners converge on: partial fluid change every 30,000–40,000 km, full/deeper service (sump removal, cleaning) only at first major service after 80,000–100,000 km or when shift quality degrades.1234528 - Other reported interval data points from individual owners: 70,000 km (city driving) is enough to notice improved shift smoothness after a change;29 ~80,000–82,000 km cited as a typical interval by some, though temperature and usage patterns affect frequency;30313233 one owner recommends a minimum of 30,000 km, others prefer 20,000–25,000 km.12345

    Repeated corroborating reports (Jan–May 2026) — same guidance, no new facts. Several later threads reconfirm points already established in the topic cards, with no substantive new information beyond what's documented in the topic cards: partial change every ~20,000 km starting around 100,000 km total mileage using MANNOL MULTIVEHICLE JWS 3309 (~3.5 L per change, no drain plug — pan removal required, sourced via Ozon/Yandex Market with filter);3435363738 the mandatory half-drain-only rule (never a full drain) restated in a March 2026 discussion, alongside a reminder to buy a new pan gasket;3940414243444546474849505152 the three documented AKPP service tiers (pan-plug-only partial, partial-with-pan-removal-and-filter, and full-equipment change) restated as a menu of options in April 2026;53545556 the half-drain step-by-step procedure (drain plug → dipstick tube → pan → filter → refill ~4 L → new ~800 RUB gasket, RU market) restated again in April 2026;57585960616263646566676869 the JWS-3309 spec with Idemitsu as an owner-confirmed acceptable brand, restated in an April 2026 thread;7071727374 and Dexron III (3309) named again as the AKPP fluid spec in a May 2026 exchange.7576

    Maintenance interval, another data point (2026-05 report). A separate service recommendation set the AKPP fluid change interval at every ~50,000 km, consistent with the 30,000–50,000 km range already documented above.77

    Auxiliary transmission cooler as a preventive measure around 80k km (2024-08 report). Owners who fit an auxiliary AKPP cooling radiator preventively (motivated by a reported overheating failure at 165,000 km) discussed it alongside this partial-vs-full fluid-change debate; the reasoning and the cost breakdown are in the auxiliary cooling radiator card.787980818283848586

    Change interval and spec — further corroborating reports (2026-01/02). Additional community data points reinforce the guidance already documented in the topic cards: one owner recommends changing every 60,000 km as a safe baseline "you won't go wrong with";87 another argues the change should really happen at 40,000–45,000 km, reporting they changed at 50,000 km and found the difference in transmission behavior before/after clearly noticeable;88 and a third owner, at 45,000 km, had not yet done their first AKPP fluid change, considering it "a bit early" still — consistent with the 40,000–50,000 km first-change window documented in the topic cards.89 On fluid spec, further owners confirm JWS 3309 (and any JWS-3309-approved analog/equivalent) as the correct spec when asked whether a specific canned fluid was suitable for the X80 automatic.909192
