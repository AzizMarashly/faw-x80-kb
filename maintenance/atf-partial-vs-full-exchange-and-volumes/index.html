    ATF partial vs full exchange and volumes

    Tags: #automatic-transmission · #atf

    Prices and suppliers on this page are Russian-market (RUB, at the dates shown). Verify locally before relying on them in Syria.

    How much fluid a partial or full change moves, and what owners report about machine (hydraulic) flushes versus repeated partial changes.

    Fill quantity confusion, resolved. Owners have reported conflicting numbers — some say 3 L is a full fill, others say 4–5 L. The dealer's own procedure clarifies the variance: drain fluid (~2.7–3 L emerges on a partial drain), add ~3 L back, then check/top up per the owner's manual procedure in 200 ml increments until correct (professional dealer tooling uses VAS 5051 / VAS 6262 / VAS 6262/2 adapters for precise top-fill). Total system capacity when genuinely full is ~4–5 L. After filling, run through all gears several times before final level check.123456789101112131415161718

    Safety warning — external flush machines. Do not use an external "аппарат" flushing device for an AKPP oil change once the transmission has 100,000+ km — it stirs up accumulated internal debris and risks damage. Manual half-drain of ~4 L at 30,000–40,000 km intervals is the safer approach. One owner reported oil condition was still excellent (raspberry-colored) at 80,000 km on this regime. Separately, one owner destroyed a Mazda transmission by overfilling during a half-drain procedure — a caution about being careful with quantities, not just method. Consensus: only intervene on a transmission with actual shift problems beyond the routine 30–40k interval; otherwise leave it alone.192021222324252627282930

    3 partial flushes vs. 1 full aerated flush. At 70,000 km, one owner chose 3 sequential partial fluid changes instead of a full aerated flush (the service shop offered both options). Result: noticeably improved shifting behavior, smoother downshifts, better acceleration — the factory fill was suspected to be poor-quality/degraded oil from assembly. Cost was also lower: 3 partial changes ≈ 18,000 RUB (RU market, November 2024) vs. a full aerated flush at 23,000–29,000 RUB (Moscow-area, RU market, November 2024), for comparable perceived improvement. One other community member disputed that 3 partials would typically be cheaper than one full flush, but the reporting owner's actual received quotes showed the gap.3132333435

    60,000 km first-change procedure and restyled-model top access (2024-06 report). Recommended first AKPP fluid change at 60,000 km, with two documented options: (1) full hardware/aerated flush — drain, refill with 12–14 L through the top, requires proper equipment and careful pressure management to avoid solenoid damage; (2) semi-drain (preferred under 60k km) — remove the pan, change filter and gasket, drain and refill with the same amount plus 50–70 g increments across multiple cycles, checking fluid level at a 35°C target temperature.36 Unlike pre-facelift cars, the restyled X80 has a top fill access port for transmission fluid — fill from above, drain from below — making the hardware-flush method impractical without proper ports, so the semi-pan-change method remains the practical option even for restyled owners.3738394041

    Fluid capacity — exact measured drain (2026-05 report). One partial-drain service measured exactly 4 liters drained from the automatic transmission, corroborating the ~4L figure used in the partial-change procedure card (actual amount can vary by procedure/temperature).4243

    Confusion over drained-vs-total volume, restated (2026-06 report, low confidence — single source). A viewer questioned a video's fluid-change procedure: draining via the drain plug removes only 1–1.2 liters, and the refill procedure calls for waiting for a 1-drop-per-second flow before closing the plug. This restates the fill-quantity confusion already documented above — actual total capacity requires calibration against a reference gauge, not just "replace what drained." Do not overfill; excess pressure damages seals. Some commenters suggested checking the service manual for exact specs rather than relying on informal videos.44
