    Emergency tool kit storage in the spare wheel well

    Tags: #tools

    Practical tip. Keep tools and spare parts in a bag/container stored inside the spare wheel well instead of loose in the trunk. This space-efficient approach allows carrying a full tool set, an air compressor, jump-start cables, a metal sheet (for jack stability on soft ground), rope, a folding shovel, and spare fluids — all without taking up regular cargo space.123456
