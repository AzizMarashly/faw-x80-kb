    Filter part numbers and kit pricing

    Tags: #part-number · #price · #cabin-filter · #air-filter · #mazda-6-compat · #engine-oil

    Prices and suppliers on this page are Russian-market (RUB, at the dates shown). Verify locally before relying on them in Syria.

    Part numbers. Cabin (salon) filter: 5DA061P11. Engine air filter: RF4F13Z40H or 5BA013Z40. Oil filter: 1012010-37K or 1012010-37K#P1. Fuel filter: FC0160950.1

    ZEKKERT filter kit pricing (2025-01, Ozon). A ZEKKERT filter kit (air + oil + cabin/coal filters) was available on Ozon at 1,583 RUB (35% discount from a listed 2,467 RUB) as of January 2025 (RU market).2

    Air filter material comparison (2025-04 report). Three air filter options were tested/measured by owners: Goodwill AG119 — filter element thickness 1mm, with a rare/tight rib pattern; the OEM original (unbranded) — 1.2mm thick; Purflux A 1157 — 1.5mm thick, the thickest of the three. One owner (Niko75) reports using similarly-specced filters successfully. Thicker filtration media and differing rib/spacing patterns may affect airflow and filtration efficiency, though no owner reported a functional problem from any of the three.345

    Air and cabin filter supplier quality notes (2025-09 report). Cabin (salon) filter brands are largely interchangeable and not critical to get exactly right. Engine air filters need more care: Miles-brand air filters reportedly have quality issues and a high defect rate per warehouse staff feedback; Sakura is marginally better with fewer returns. General recommendation: source air filters from verified suppliers rather than open marketplaces (Ozon/Wildberries) where counterfeit/defective stock is more likely. For cabin filters, canister-style activated-charcoal filters are preferred.6789

    Oil filter options and compatibility (2025-09 report). Tested oil filter options beyond the Mahle/Mann/Zekert/SCT/Hengst/LYNXauto list above: Lynx LC-1602 (Japanese Akika Kaihatsu brand — note this is the same base part as the LYNXauto LC1602 referenced elsewhere — Ozon price 373 RUB, RU market, 2025-09-20); Brave BR.F.2.20 (Korea Technology brand, rated 4.7/5 from 7 reviews, became a popular community choice); Goodwill GG 201 ua (rated 5.0/5; specs: height 120mm, diameter 76/71/62mm, thread M26x1.5; 2025-09-20). Goodwill is preferred for quality reputation, though Brave has also gained popularity on good reviews.101112131415

    Cabin filter alternative — Lada X-Ray part (2025-09 report). The factory cabin filter can be replaced with a Lada X-Ray filter, which requires minor cutting to fit properly. Brave and Goodwill brands were also tested as cabin-filter alternatives and are reported as reliable, good-quality options.12161314

    Air filter housing removal procedure (2025-10 report). The air filter housing assembly is held in place by rubber mounts on the bottom (similar to the engine cover mounting). Removal only requires careful pulling — no tools or bolts are involved. Pull gently to preserve the rubber mounts for reuse.17181920

    Air filter cross-compatibility — MARSHALL MA8100 (2025-11 report). The MARSHALL MA8100 air filter, designed for the Mazda 6, 626, and MPV, fits the FAW X80 perfectly. One owner reports excellent quality and proper fitment after installation.2122

    ZEKKERT kit — early corroborating report and charcoal-filter caveat (2024-06 report). An earlier community report already confirmed the ZEKKERT filter kit (air + oil + cabin filters) fits the FAW Besturn X80, corroborating the January 2025 pricing/availability note above from a full six months earlier. One community caveat on the charcoal cabin-filter variant specifically: charcoal (activated-carbon) cabin filters clog faster than standard non-charcoal filters, so some owners deliberately choose the standard variant for longer service intervals despite the general community preference for charcoal filtration noted elsewhere on this card.232425

    Oil filter cross-reference and "no meaningful difference" community view (2024-07 report). An additional confirmed-compatible oil filter option: Zekkert, cross-referenced for Ford/Mazda applications (Wildberries listing). Broader community sentiment from this thread: after cycling through many different oil filter brands, several owners report no noticeable performance difference between them — "give or take, they're all about the same" — reinforcing the "regular timely oil changes matter more than filter brand" conclusion already documented above regarding the back-pressure/anti-drainback valve question.26272829

    Air filter replacement interval and overdue symptoms (2025-11 report). The engine air filter should be changed around 100,000 km. Symptoms of an overdue filter: rough cold starts, poor acceleration, and an SVS warning light appearing on the dashboard — replacing the filter resolved all three symptoms for the reporting owner. Filters are available from Autodoc, with reliable delivery but variable pricing. Some filter designs include a washable mesh layer, which can be cleaned in place instead of doing a full replacement.30313233

    MANN air filter option, Ozon-sourced (2026-06 report). A MANN-brand air filter is confirmed compatible with the FAW X80, cross-referenced as also fitting Mazda vehicles. Available for purchase on Ozon.3435

    Additional cross-compatible filter options and suppliers (2026-09 report). Air filter: a Zekkert LF1444 air filter (cross-listed for the X80) is available on Ozon, though one owner (Prosto Zuev) notes it has gotten noticeably more expensive recently; a cheaper alternative is the RIGINAL RF2A13Z40 air filter (listed OEM cross-compatible for Mazda 6, 626, MPV, and FAW Bestune X80), untried by the reporting owner but priced more attractively. A further alternative confirmed by photo: the MARSHALL MA8100 air filter (Mazda 6/626/MPV/FAW Besturn X80 cross-reference, OEM RF4F13Z409A) at 416 RUB (down from 462 RUB) on Ozon (RU market, September 2026). Cabin filter: SUFIX SSC-1046 (activated-carbon/charcoal type), cross-listed for Mazda 6, Mazda 2, CX-7, and FAW Besturn X80, on Ozon. A further cabin-filter alternative confirmed by photo: SCT SA1215 (Mazda 2/6/CX-7 cross-reference) at 278 RUB (down from 309 RUB) on Ozon (RU market, September 2026). Oil filter: a listing for part 1012010-37KH (a variant of the 1012010-37K part number already documented above) was also sourced via Ozon; one owner reports buying 5 at once and verifying at least one as genuine, though cannot vouch for what's currently sold under that listing a year later. Hengst H90W27 is reiterated as the preferred oil filter choice — one owner specifically flags that Goodwill's X80 oil filter incorrectly includes an anti-drain-back rubber washer that should not be there, corroborating the earlier "no meaningful difference / Goodwill preferred" notes above with a concrete mechanical caveat against Goodwill. For sourcing beyond Ozon/Wildberries, established filter suppliers Emex, AutoDoc, Exist, and Vedro.pro were named; buying two Hengst H90W27 oil filters through a specific Ozon seller profile (which grants 250 bonus points) brought the pair down to about 600 RUB total (RU market, September 2026).363738394041

    Filter kit and oil price point, 2026-09 (Фаниль Фаритович report). A separate seller offered a complete filter kit for the FAW Besturn X80 at 1,500 RUB, plus motor oil at 5,000 RUB (RU market), as of 2026-09-04 — a different, more recent price point than the ZEKKERT kit pricing documented above (2025-01); both prices should be read as independent community-observed data points rather than a single official price.424344
