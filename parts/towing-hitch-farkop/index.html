    Towing hitch (farkop) — installation

    Tags: #installation · #availability

    Prices and suppliers on this page are Russian-market (RUB, at the dates shown). Verify locally before relying on them in Syria.

    Installation procedure. Fitting an aftermarket towing hitch requires removing the lower bumper skirt only — the upper bumper stays in place 123. Removal technique: pull the top part of the skirt toward you, then unscrew the self-tapping screws from the bottom 4. For the hitch's electrical wiring, there is enough space to route cables from the left side, near the rear light 56. The owner's manual does not recommend installing a tow hitch, but many owners do so anyway without reported issues 7.

    Availability. - As of 2024-10-19, one owner installed a hitch himself; sourcing required searching outside official channels 12. - By 2024-10-21, an aftermarket hitch became available for retail sale on Ozon — summer 2024 had been a shortage period before that 8. - A specific model, Sheriff 3951.12 (X80 fitment), is available through nakuzov.ru and other major Russian auto-parts retailers: https://nakuzov.ru/catalog/farkopy-i-komplektuyushchie/farkopy/farkop_sherif_3951_12_dlya_faw_besturn_x80/ 9.

    Towing hook (buksirovochnaya petlya/kryuk) — a different, factory recovery-loop part, not the aftermarket hitch above. OEM towing hook part 5DA050EJO (also cross-referenced from the FAW T77 platform) has thread spec M20×2.5 — confirmed by physical caliper measurement of the threads on a removed unit 1011121314151617181920212223242526. Original cost was 900 RUB in 2021 (RU market). A Lada Largus towing hook (~600 RUB, RU market, as of October 2025) has been used by one owner as a budget alternative — reportedly fitting a Besturn B50 and possibly (unconfirmed) the X80 too. Factory-price quotes were reported as fluctuating with the Yuan exchange rate, and members note that hooks matching this part number are either out of stock or priced very high through normal retail channels 27282930. Conflict: a VW Polo towing hook was also mentioned by community members as a possible fitment, but this was not confirmed within this discussion 26.

    Kit contents, Sheriff 3951.12 (Russian passport sheet). The kit with article 3951.12 consists of 19 numbered items: bar (shtanga) x1; hitch hook (kryuk) x1; junction-box cover (podrozetnik, the socket plate) x1; bolt M8x30 DIN 933 x10; bolt M10x30 DIN 933 x2; bolt M12x1.25x40 DIN 961 x4; bolt M12x1.25x70 DIN 960 x2; nut M12x1.25 DIN 934 x4; washer 12 DIN 125 A x10; spring washer 8 DIN 127 x10; mounting plate (planka zakladnaya) x2; spring washer 10 DIN 127 x2; spring washer 12 DIN 127 x6; washer 8 DIN 9021 x10; washer 10 DIN 9021 x2; brackets (three different, 1 + 1 + 2 pieces); ball cap (kolpachok na shar) x1 31.
