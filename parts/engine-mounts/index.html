    Engine mounts — part number and alternatives

    Tags: #engine-mount · #mazda-6-compat · #part-number · #patron

    Prices and suppliers on this page are Russian-market (RUB, at the dates shown). Verify locally before relying on them in Syria.

    Part number. OEM engine mount: 5CA139060 (also referenced as FA0139060). OEM cost is approximately 6,000 RUB (RU market, April 2025) from Chinese parts sites; a Mazda OEM hydro mount was separately reported at 10,000+ RUB (RU market, April 2025) 123.

    Quality issues. Mounts sold under the FAW code FA0139060 from supplier HUTY (via the Emex platform) were found defective — uneven rubber sealing and poorly formed metal components 45. Tested alternatives from one member: Patron and Tatsumi brands were acceptable; Miles 150 was rejected for poor quality; a fake "OEM" labeled part was also rejected. Chinese-manufactured parts in general show persistent QC issues in this category 4567.

    Right vs. left/lower mount, and Patron caveat. The right-side mount differs from the Mazda 6 GG equivalent and should use either the genuine OEM part or a pressed-in cartridge sourced from the GG mount — at 48,000 km no visible wear was found on a GG-sourced cartridge in one owner's report 128910. Patron should not be used for the lower engine mount — incompatibility/poor fit was confirmed when sourcing a Patron lower mount intended for a Mazda GG 1112. By contrast, the Patron right-side mount (hydraulic type) was tested on a restyled X80 and performed well after one day of use (though long-term data is still limited) 1112. At 48,000 km, a side-by-side comparison of the stock (worn) lower engine mount against a new Patron mount showed a visible difference in damping condition, favoring the fresh Patron part over the deteriorated stock one 1314.

    Left engine mount — inspection note. The same member found the left engine mount to be in quite normal condition on inspection; after removing it to check, they reinstalled the original rather than replacing it, planning to re-inspect around 20,000 km later. A Jikiu-brand cartridge was kept on hand as a spare in the meantime 12.

    Right engine mount alternative — Mazda 6/Atenza 2002–2007 (July 2026). Another cross-model alternative for the right-side engine mount: SAT brand part ST-GJ6G-39-060E (Mazda OEM equivalent GJ6A39060), sourced from the Mazda 6/Atenza (2002–2007). Available on Ozon for 4,141 RUB (RU market, July 2026) with next-day delivery 15. Vadim B asked how long members' mounts had lasted after buying via an Ozon link — his own lasted only 4 months before failing, which he suspected was a counterfeit or defective unit 1615. Fab, testing the same Mazda-sourced mount, reported 6 months of service with only partial reduction in vibration — better than Vadim's experience but still short of a durable fix 17.

    OEM engine mount identification — QR code and bilingual markings (July 2026). OEM part number FA0139060 (matches the 5CA139060/ FA0139060 numbering already noted above). Genuine Chinese-FAW-made mounts are marked with a laser-engraved QR code and bilingual Chinese/Russian text: "一汽零件" (yīqì língjiàn — "FAW part") and "纯正部件" (chúnzhèng bùjiàn — "genuine/original component"), alongside Russian translations "запчасть FAW" and "оригинальная деталь" 1819. Victor reported finding parts marked this way on AliExpress and called it "original from China" 1820. Vasily disputed the authenticity of the specific part shown, pointing out that genuine factory mounts do not carry white-paint lettering like the one pictured, and separately cautioned that QR codes are not proof of authenticity — Chinese counterfeiters also fake QR codes and link them to look-alike websites, so a scannable code alone does not confirm a genuine part 2122.

    More owner experience and fakes (September-October 2026). Lifetime reports on Patron and other aftermarket mounts, the GH/GG mount note and the Emex supplier codes to avoid are collected in engine mounts — owner experience and fakes 2324.
