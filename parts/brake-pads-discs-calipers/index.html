    Brake pads, discs & caliper parts

    Tags: #brake-pads · #brake-disc · #caliper · #part-number · #price

    Prices and suppliers on this page are Russian-market (RUB, at the dates shown). Verify locally before relying on them in Syria.

    Front brake pads. Original part number: B5CA03328ZA (also seen as B5CA03328ZA#P1), size designation 133 12. Guide pin/bushing hardware (направляющие колодок) is always included with OEM pad kits and generally not sold separately; kits are compatible with Mazda pad fitments where the pad shape matches 345678910111213.

    Front pad prices (RUB, RU market) reported over time: - 2024-04-12, Mytishchi: 1,276 RUB per set, size 133, designation P1 12 - Original ("оригинальные") pads: 7,000 RUB as of Feb 2025 1415 - Conflict: one member reported buying original pads for 980 RUB in 2023 from a factory pickup point in Kaliningrad; another disputed this, stating originals were never available at that price through KitParts inventory (typically 6,000–7,000 RUB at official dealers). Current market price remains unclear given the variance 161718. - A recommended Ozon.ru listing for front pads (reported as working well, no performance details given): https://ozon.ru/t/bpzFxL7 1920 - 2025-05: Comi One brand discs 7,680 RUB + matching pads 2,900 RUB per set; one buyer reported 500 km with no squeaking after install 2122

    Aftermarket/"kitzap" (китайские запчасти) pads are cheaper but have squeak issues reported by some owners 1415. Quality comparison from one member's experience with the Patron brand: Patron brake pads outperform Miles brand pads in durability and quietness 2324.

    Rear brake pads. Part number GDB3517, described by the community as "good" but "expensive"; exact pricing wasn't captured in the discussion, but it's noted as a premium option. Cheaper aftermarket alternatives start from about 650 RUB (RU market, February 2025) 252627.

    Front brake disc. Part number 5DA033251: 3,140 RUB as of 2025-04-30 (RU market), available at kitzapchasti.ru 28.

    Rear brake caliper repair kit. Part number B5CA02646Z: 2,080 RUB as of 2025-04-29 (RU market), sold at kitzapchasti.ru (Naberezhnye Chelny branch) 29.

    Front brake pad supplier — OOOVE.ru. Aftermarket supplier OOOVE.ru carries front brake pads, part number B5CA03328ZAP1, with an option to buy front and rear sets together. Pricing and availability are region-dependent 30.

    OEM pads from AliExpress (2024-03). Original factory front/rear brake pads sourced from AliExpress for 2,538 RUB (RU market, March 2024). One buyer installed them and reported 15,000 km with good results, keeping a spare set at home. Delivery to the buyer's apartment was available, but shipping took nearly 2 months 31323334.

    Local brake service pricing — Mytishchi, Moscow area (2024-03). Local prices (RU market, March 2024) for a full brake service: brake discs 4,200 RUB/pair (front), 3,700 RUB/pair (rear); brake pads 1,250 RUB for a full set. Same-day installation 35.
