    X80F catalog: chassis part codes

    Tags: #part-number · #catalog · #wheels · #drive-shaft

    How the catalog is organised. The FAW X80FC2 chassis catalog (top-level assembly X80FC2, "chassis - driving system") splits the car into groups coded NNNNA01X80F, for example 3400A01X80F for the front damper, spring and control arm. Each row has an item number, the Chinese and English part name, remarks and the spare-part code. Remarks flag the variant: MT model, natural aspiration (naturally aspirated) or turbocharged. Order by the part code, not by the name 1. The restyle (X80F) original catalogue repeats these groups and adds a stock lead time of 1.5 and ABC class B2 for most suspension items 2. Groups listed in the index but without detail: 2800A01X80F rear damper, 2510A02X80F drive shaft (MT), 4100A01X80F accelerator control mechanism 1. Clutch groups are in X80F clutch part codes; body panels in X80F body sheet-metal part numbers.
