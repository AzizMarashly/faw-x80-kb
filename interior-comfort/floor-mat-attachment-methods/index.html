    Floor mat attachment and securement methods

    Tags: #clips · #price

    Prices and suppliers on this page are Russian-market (RUB, at the dates shown). Verify locally before relying on them in Syria.

    Some advice here concerns sub-zero winters and is less relevant in a hot climate.

    Stock X80 floor mats come with adhesive tape strips and side clips. For enhanced securement, owners report: applying larger adhesive tape pieces and sewing the mats to the carpet/base along the edges with heavy-duty thread. The stock plastic clips need regular replacement — some owners report replacing them on a roughly monthly basis. Alternative: use two-sided automotive tape for full-perimeter adhesion instead of relying on the clips 1234567.

    Mat material by season — EVA vs. rubber: two main floor-mat types suit different seasons. EVA mats excel in winter — water-resistant, resist moisture, easy to shake out and dry. Rubber mats work better in summer — easy to wipe clean with a damp cloth, dry quickly. Combination approach used by some owners: factory rubber mats in summer, switched to EVA in winter. BigFoot brand (autokovrik-bigfoot.ru) is recommended for quality. Note: OEM factory mats deteriorate over time as their glue/backing fails 891011121314151617.

    EVO EVA floor mats — custom-cut option and pricing (May 2024): EVO EVA floor mats made specifically for the FAW X80 are available on Wildberries. Custom-fitted mats can also be ordered at local car accessory shops — professional shops cut and edge-seal EVO material to the exact floor contours, with a 2–3 hour turnaround, including anti-slip backing. Cost with professional installation: approximately 2,500 RUB (RU market, May 2024); pre-made standard mats are also sold directly on Ozon and Wildberries without custom cutting 18192021222324.

    Ozon floor mats — good price/quality report: separately, another Ozon listing for FAW Besturn X80 floor mats (sourced from China) was recommended by an owner for good price and quality, with a choice of colors available at checkout: https://ozon.ru/t/gaxnwfC 2526.

    EVA trunk mat. A black EVA trunk mat (коврик) for the FAW Besturn X80 (2013 onward) was listed by the Prime-avto shop in October 2025; no price was given.27
