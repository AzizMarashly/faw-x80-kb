    Water leaks — trunk and rear door

    Tags: #water-ingress · #trunk · #molding

    Prices and suppliers on this page are Russian-market (RUB, at the dates shown). Verify locally before relying on them in Syria.

    Water in the trunk lid through the clip holes: after repainting the trunk lid, one owner found water entering specifically through the holes where the chrome-trim retaining clips are inserted — a known issue on the X80. Pushsalo (rust-proofing wax) in hidden cavities helps as general protection 12.

    Rear door/trunk water pooling under the spare tire (2): water pools under the spare tire (10–20+ mL) even without opening the rear door, in heavy rain. Root causes include: a poor rear door/trunk seal, an incorrectly-seated door card trim, or possible leakage around the rear window molding / an incompletely sealed window. Diagnosis/fix: remove the door card, check the door seals for proper fit, check window-to-body panel gaps, and apply sealant where gaps are found. Test by pouring water with the door closed and the interior stripped 34567. ⚠️ Conflict: disagreement on the leak source — some owners report water entering only when the door is opened, others report leakage without opening the door at all (background infiltration through seals).

    Water under the spare tire — broader diagnosis and repair cost: water accumulating under the spare-tire location can have multiple sources: a degraded door/boot seal allowing ingress when the door opens, failed clips/seals on the door molding (especially on restyle models), or leaking rear-light seals. Diagnostic method: spray water on the sealed car while watching from inside. Common fixes include re-sealing with silicone, replacing window-molding clips with better seals, and checking rear-light gaskets. Repair cost at a Moscow shop (AutoGermes): ~18,600 RUB for diagnosis plus full repair (RU market, November 2025) 8910111213141516171819202122232425.

    Trunk and baggage-area water ingress — comprehensive diagnosis: common ingress sources in the trunk/baggage area: (1) loose or poorly-sealed rear window trim molding; (2) rear spoiler/trim clips where the original sealant has been removed; (3) door/frame gaps from sloppy reassembly. Puddles form in the spare-tire well and under door trim; water absorbs into the soundproofing material and slowly leaks internally. Fixes: reseal the molding clips, reapply gaskets, and check panel alignment. Rust can appear under the molding, but the body interior stays dry if properly sealed 2627282930313233343536.

    Water pooling in the spare-tire well — multiple candidate entry points (December 2025 report): water was entering the spare-tire well; one owner sealed the clip joints in the trunk lid with sealant, which helped but only partially — root cause remained unclear. Recommended diagnostic tests: (1) spray water from a hose and watch for entry points; (2) check the rear-door drain (visible on the garage floor); (3) inspect the rear-window latch seal; (4) check for entry between the rear side panels. A separate owner used wide vinyl tape as a temporary fix for gaps in the chrome trim — this sealed the symptom (reported dry for 2 months afterward) but not the confirmed root cause 37383940414243444546.

    Water in trunk side pockets and under the spare tire — 5th-door molding and ventilation: water accumulates in the side trunk pockets (карманы багажника) and under the spare-tire area after rain or washing (one report of ~5 liters). Primary cause: water enters under the rubber molding on the 5th door (trunk lid/liftgate). Fixes: (1) remove and re-seal the molding with silicone hermetic sealant; (2) improve ventilation by keeping the sunroof shutter cracked open; (3) remove the drain plugs from the underbody rails (корыта) to allow drainage and prevent pooling 47484950.

    Water leak from rear door/5th-door molding clips after reinstallation: if the rear-door (5th door) molding is removed and reinstalled — for example after accident repair — water can leak into the trunk because the clips lose their tight fit once disturbed. Solution: remove the molding, apply hermetic sealant around it, and reinstall to restore a tight seal; consulting an experienced service is recommended for this repair 515253.

    Water pooling in the rear cargo area under door seals: water accumulates in the rear cargo area, under the door seals and in the cup-holder recesses, during rain. Causes: poor condition of the rear door seal, and roof water drainage entering the interior. Fixes: (1) inspect and clean the rear door seal — soak it in kerosene if it has hardened, to restore elasticity; (2) drill drainage holes in the cargo floor pan with an angled outlet pipe; (3) maintain the roof drainage properly. Monitor nearby electrical boxes for corrosion as a consequence 54555657.

    Trunk water leak from the rear-quarter-glass seal: water entering the trunk from the upper rear corner area (near the rear-quarter window) has been traced to a failed seal between the rear-quarter glass panel and its molding. Fix: apply black sealant/silicone caulk along the gap between the glass and the mounting frame. Some owners additionally apply weatherstripping tape (sourced from AliExpress) for extra protection 5859.

    See also: Water leaks — fender and cabin ingress; Water leaks — door seals and body sealing; Water leaks — sunroof and roof drains.
