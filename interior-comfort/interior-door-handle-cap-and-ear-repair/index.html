    Interior door handle cap and ear repair

    Tags: #aliexpress · #conflict

    Community members disagree on this topic — both positions are reported.

    A replacement interior door-handle cap/plug (заглушка, sold on AliExpress) can be installed without disassembling the whole door. Installation notes: (1) the cap must be glued, not clipped — it does not snap into place on its own; (2) glue with Cosmofen adhesive mixed with a small amount of baking soda for a strong hold; (3) apply the adhesive with an insulin syringe for precision — this avoids getting glue on the paint, which hardens almost instantly on contact with air/ paint; (4) the cap needs minor filing on its interior-facing surfaces to fit properly; (5) the interior door handle must be unscrewed/ removed first to access the mounting area; (6) apply the glue to seat the cap, then run the syringe tip around the perimeter on top as a second pass for reinforcement. A Drive2 (дром) article on the X80 was consulted as an alternate reference, but it did not cover the adhesive step — the community verified the Cosmofen+soda method independently 1234567891011121314151617.

    ⚠️ Conflict / caveat: some users report the handle/cap detaching again 2–3 years after installation, even when glued. The reporting author still recommends the Cosmofen+soda adhesive method over any purely mechanical (clip-only) retention approach, since it has proven to be the community's most reliable fix despite the eventual-failure reports 1310111617.

    Related repair — broken handle "ear" (ushko), epoxy method: a broken plastic mounting ear ("ушко") on a door handle — the small tab that anchors the handle at its attachment point — can be repaired with the same family of adhesive rather than replaced outright: (1) file the broken edge smooth; (2) apply Cosmofen epoxy adhesive (cyanoacrylate + baking-soda mix, same recipe as above) to the broken area; (3) reshape the ear while the adhesive is workable; (4) apply glue around the perimeter for reinforcement; (5) install. One owner confirmed the repaired handle held securely in testing after curing. This method is suitable for plastic handle breakage specifically at the attachment/ear point, as a cheaper alternative to sourcing a replacement handle 1819.
