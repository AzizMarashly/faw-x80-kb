    Water leaks — sunroof and roof drains

    Tags: #water-ingress · #sunroof · #cleaning

    Prices and suppliers on this page are Russian-market (RUB, at the dates shown). Verify locally before relying on them in Syria.

    Some advice here concerns sub-zero winters and is less relevant in a hot climate.

    Sunroof/roof drain channel clog — repair: water leaking into the cabin via roof/sunroof vents despite dry weather can be caused by (1) clogged roof drain channels that let water back-flow into the interior, or (2) plastic fittings/connectors (white tubes) pulling loose from porous rubber gaskets, letting water bypass the drain. Fix: insert a brush (1.5 m is sufficient) through the roof drain openings to clear debris; for loose fittings, remove the connector, clean the porous rubber gasket, apply silicone sealant, refit firmly, and seal the perimeter with additional silicone. Also periodically spray the sunroof seal with silicone lubricant for waterproofing 12345678910.

    Roof-drain connection seal failure (passenger-side prone): a primary cause of cabin flooding — drain connections at the roof/windshield interface on both sides (passenger side particularly prone) become disconnected or loose, routing water into the interior. Located at the strut area below the windshield. Preventive fix: extend the drain hoses with additional tubing before leakage occurs; check both sides, since each has its own drain connection 1112131415.

    Dashboard rattle near the defroster — loose sunroof drain cover clip: intermittent whistling/rattling from the defroster area while driving (not present with the fan alone) is often caused by a loose trim/drain-cover clip on the roof panel near the windshield — specifically the "жабо" (roof rain pan) cover clip at the windshield strut. It appears/disappears with vibration; a similar issue is reported at the right corner of the жабо panel 161718.

    Sunroof drain — location and cleaning: the sunroof drain is located higher up, closer to the fender; use a flexible brush to clean the drain openings (available on AliExpress) 1920. If the sealant on the drain "mushroom" fitting looks intact but water still gets in, try: gently warm the area with a hair dryer (no overheating), then apply liquid rubber sealant; verify by pouring water on the sunroof or testing at a car wash 212223242526. If removing interior trim doesn't fix a leak, check the sunroof specifically and clean its drain holes with a flexible brush 272820.

    Water under the driver's floor mat: often tied to a clogged sunroof drain — clean the drain tubes 2930. Separate cause: the sunroof drain fitting sits directly above the BSM (body security module) block — the fitting loosens slightly and water starts leaking inside. Fix: apply sealant to the fitting, and thoroughly soak the sunroof gasket with silicone. The BSM block itself can be opened and its terminals treated with contact cleaner for a water-repelling effect. Desiccants used to dry the cabin: feminine hygiene pads, cat-litter gel crystals, adult incontinence pads (dog-bed absorbent pads) — plus a powerful vacuum at a self-service car wash 313233343536.

    Winter sunroof-drain-hose leak at wheel-well connections: in one case, sunroof drain hose connections at the wheel-well area leaked during winter — enough water accumulated to soak the carpets completely. Consequence: moisture reached the RKE (remote key entry) module's connectors, which corroded and later caused separate electrical issues (AKPP/automatic- transmission control errors). Fix: inspect and reseal the drain hose connections, verify correct drain routing 3738.

    Leak through the sunroof drain-hole seal (degraded sealant): rain-time cabin leak from the area above the glass corner (where the drain tube opens). Fix: gently heat the area with a hair dryer (no overheating), apply liquid rubber sealant to the joint between the drain and the panel, verify with a hose/car-wash spray test 3919212223242526.

    Sunroof-drain fitting misalignment — front passenger-floor leak: recurring water leaks under the front passenger floor mat traced to an improperly fitted sunroof drain-line connector (фитинг) on the driver side — the fitting holes were misaligned by ~0.5mm from the body attachment points, causing water to flow directly onto the mounting point instead of into the drain tube. Diagnosis: the sunroof drain line was leaking from under the weatherstrip seal despite previous sealing attempts. Fix: disassemble the drain-fitting area completely and re-seal with proper adhesive. Additionally, the BSM (body security module) block showed oxidation from water exposure — clean with contact cleaner to remove oxidation, then protect the contacts 404142.

    Water pooling under floor carpets — roof/sunroof drain and dome-light gasket checklist: water accumulates under floor carpets and interior trim after rain/snow even with a functional engine and heater. Suspected sources: (1) blocked panoramic-roof/sunroof drain channels or plugged side-window drains; (2) sunroof seal degradation; (3) failed dome-light (interior light fixture) gasket; (4) clogged windshield-wiper channel drains. A secondary symptom is corrosion (green patina) on electrical connectors once water reaches them. Diagnostic steps: inspect sunroof/skylight seals and drain tubes, check the interior-light gaskets, clear all drains, and dry the interior thoroughly with heat; a professional inspection may be needed if a structural leak is suspected. ⚠️ Conflict: several owners report the same symptoms but disagree on root cause — some suspect sunroof drainage specifically, others point to general drain blockage or seal failure more broadly 434445464748495051525354.

    DIY drain-cleaning tool: for clearing a blocked roof/sunroof drain (люк) or door-sill drainage holes, use a flexible drain brush from AliExpress (PlumHOUSE brand, ~112 RUB (RU market, April 2026), link: https://ali.click/fvvz51i). Similar cleaning procedures are demonstrated in YouTube videos for other vehicles (e.g. Mercedes, Volkswagen Teramont) and apply the same way to the X80's drains 5556.

    Water leakage into cabin (2019–2020 models) — sunroof drain / sealing / ventilation-intake theories: a commonly reported issue on 2019–2020 model years: water accumulates and drains into the cabin rather than outside. Candidate root causes debated in the community: (1) sunroof frame drainage blockage near the perimeter; (2) poor sealant at the roofline-to-body junction; (3) rain being drawn in via the ventilation system during heavy downpours. Workaround: seal the roof edge with a waterproof paint tracer to help locate the entry point on follow-up inspection. Note: even a stationary, unused car can accumulate cabin water after just 3 days of rain 57585960616263646566. ⚠️ Conflict: no consensus was reached — sealant failure, intake ventilation design flaw, and structural gap were all proposed, with the group agreeing the fault is difficult to diagnose without a camera/tracer technique. Some attribute it specifically to a factory defect in model-year 2019.

    Sunroof drain cleaning — brake-cable method: sunroof drain tubes can be cleaned from the interior using a brake cable (or similarly stiff wire) fed through the drain channels from the top opening, with the sunroof open. Work carefully — the drain tubes are loosely fitted and can disconnect if forced. This prevents water pooling in the roof area 6768.

    Sunroof drain tubes not fully seated — hermetic sealant blocking insertion: a common defect where the drainage tubes from the sunroof/ hatch aren't pushed in all the way, letting water drip under the driver's and passenger's seats. Root cause: hermetic sealant applied on a nearby seam blocks the tubes from being fully inserted. Fix requires removing the fender for proper external access to reseat and reseal the tubes; sealing from inside the cabin is possible but difficult due to poor visibility and access 6970.

    Roof-drain seal cracking at A-pillar plus A/C drain-tube extension — combined leak source: water pooling under the driver/passenger floor mats can stem from two independent sources found together: (1) the roof's water-drainage outlets at the A-pillar (front fender edge) use rubber seals that dry out and crack with age, letting water past the plastic interior drainage tubes; (2) the A/C drain tube under the hood needs to be extended for proper drainage away from the cabin. A dedicated drain-cleaning kit (soft brush + flexible tubing) is available for maintaining these drains, and owners can service both points themselves 717273747576.
