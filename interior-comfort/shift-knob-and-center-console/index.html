    Shift knob and center console

    Tags: #manual-transmission · #automatic-transmission · #price

    Prices and suppliers on this page are Russian-market (RUB, at the dates shown). Verify locally before relying on them in Syria.

    Manual transmission (МКПП) shift-knob removal: the gear-shift knob on manual-transmission X80s is fixed and cannot be easily hand-unscrewed — it rotates freely in place but does not thread off. Removal requires tools; one owner's attempt to DIY-remove it without proper tools was unsuccessful, and the installation method for the replacement was not documented in detail (low-confidence, incomplete report) 123.

    Center console removal and shifter access: to access or remove components in the center console area, remove the shifter knob first — it rotates off the shift lever. The top piece of the center console then slides out upward once its retaining clips are released. The flat panel with the cupholder is fully removable 456.

    Shifter knob leather/vinyl coating wear and restoration: the leather/vinyl coating on the shift knob wears through at the top surface and corners over time. Full removal procedure: (1) peel off the damaged coating; (2) clean the residual adhesive with solvent and a cloth, working carefully so as not to remove the underlying paint/finish. Once bare, the plastic underneath is black, matte, and smooth — cosmetically acceptable on its own. Alternative: remove the old coating and re-wrap the knob with new leather/vinyl material. If you choose not to re-wrap, the bare plastic finish is a reasonable permanent option and avoids future peeling 7891011121314151617.

    Cover peeling on the shift knob (reports and causes): on one FAW the gear-knob cover (наболдашник) was almost fully peeled at about 20,000 km 18. Victor's knob at 70,000 km peeled only slightly, and he attributes it to contact with aggressive substances such as oily hands 19. No Comments reports no sign of peeling at 220,000 km 20, while Андрей Б. says the hand wears the cover like sandpaper 21. Wear is therefore inconsistent between cars (low confidence); see the removal and re-wrap options above.

    Automatic transmission (AKPP) gear-shift handle — replacement, shaft diameter, aftermarket options: the AKPP shift handle is secured with bolts, not simply threaded onto the lever 222324. The synthetic-leather/dermantin covering can be removed and rewrapped 22. Shaft diameter is ~12mm 2526; some third-party handles (e.g. Mitsubishi Outlander/ASX alternatives) have different hole sizes and need modification (tapping/drilling) to fit properly 2527282629. Replacement handles are sold on Wildberries/OZON with various materials and designs 303132. Separately, cross-brand compatible aftermarket AT gear-selector knobs (fits Suzuki Grand Vitara/SX4/Swift, Mazda, and Mitsubishi models) are available thread-mounted, no tools needed, on OZON/AliExpress: premium version ~1,600+ RUB (RU market, January 2026), budget version ~450 RUB (RU market, January 2026) (metal + faux-leather construction) 333435363738.

    Manual transmission (МКПП) gear-lever boot (chekhol/dermantin) tearing — DIY fix: the leather/dermantin covering on the МКПП gear-lever boot wears and tears with use, the same failure mode as the shift-knob coating above. DIY fix: carefully remove the worn covering, clean off old adhesive residue, and leave the surface bare (looks acceptable, clean finish) — a 5-minute repair, no special tools. Alternative: a universal 10mm-diameter rubber boot is sold as a replacement 3940414243.

    Professional leather-wrap labor pricing (January 2026): a shop-installed leather/faux-leather (kozzam) wrap for the shift knob costs 1,500 RUB (RU market, January 2026) labor — the same shop that does steering-wheel wraps, see the steering wheel cover card for the matching steering-wheel price 444546474849.

    MKPP shift-knob rewrap — DIY paracord option: the OEM manual- transmission shift knob can become worn and slippery over time. Solutions: (1) a professional car upholsterer can completely rewrap it (see the professional leather-wrap pricing above); (2) DIY wrapping with paracord or similar material is possible without removing the knob; one user successfully wrapped their own knob this way. Multiple users report this as a common maintenance task 50515253.

    USB port recovery from the center console: if the USB port falls into the center console, the same shift-knob-first disassembly above gives access to retrieve it: (1) remove the gear-shift knob by unscrewing it; (2) carefully pry the console edge up — a wire for the handle button is attached, so don't yank it; (3) disconnect the handbrake-button connector; (4) access the port from underneath to reinstall it 545556.
