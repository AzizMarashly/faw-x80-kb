    Windshield interior sun shade — model and dimensions

    Tags: #windshield

    Prices and suppliers on this page are Russian-market (RUB, at the dates shown). Verify locally before relying on them in Syria.

    An interior windshield sun shade model FW-X80-VCS fits the X80 windshield precisely. Dimensions: inner working area 1350 × 965 mm; outer base width 1406 mm 1.

    Reinforcing measurement — height and part-designation variant: a second measurement confirms width 1406 mm (≈1.4 m) and gives height as approximately 980 mm (≈1 m) — close to, but not identical with, the 965 mm figure above (measurement method/reference point likely differs). For sun-shade fit, approximate usable dimensions are 1.3 m × 0.75 m. Part designation recorded as FW-X80-VCS LFW/X 23.

    Independent dimension report — ~120 × 75 cm: a separate report gives windshield dimensions of roughly 120 cm × 75 cm for sun-shade selection purposes — in the same range as the figures above. Custom sun-shade "umbrellas" can be ordered to these dimensions from retailers such as Wildberries 45.

    Dashboard sun shade (separate product) — AliExpress pricing: a dashboard sun shade (heat/UV insulation mat, decorative — distinct from the windshield-exterior shade above) is available on AliExpress for 2,710 RUB (RU market, June 2024), sold with a custom ornament pattern 67.
