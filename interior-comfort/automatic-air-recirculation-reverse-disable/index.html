    Automatic air recirculation — disabling

    Tags: #hvac · #part-number · #mazda-6-compat

    Prices and suppliers on this page are Russian-market (RUB, at the dates shown). Verify locally before relying on them in Syria.

    The X80 automatically switches the air intake to recirculation mode when reversing (to prevent exhaust-fume inhalation) and when the washer fluid is activated (to prevent odor). This causes window fogging during washing. Fix: disconnect the air-intake damper connector, located under the dashboard on the left side of the heater unit (requires removing the instrument-cluster binnacle). Manual control via the washer button's high-temp sensor partially manages activation; an alternative is toggling recirculation manually as needed 1234567891011.

    Alternate/simpler method — pull the flap connector, ignore the dash light: a simpler variant of the disconnect fix: remove the connector/ plug on the air-intake damper flap itself (located under the dashboard, near the glove-box area) so the flap stays fixed permanently in the non-recirculating (fresh-air) position and no longer switches automatically. The dashboard indicator light for recirculation stays lit afterward, but this is cosmetic only — it doesn't indicate a fault. One owner has run the car this way for 1.5 years without issues; a second owner independently confirmed the same approach works 1213141516.

    Part number and clicking noise — normal operation: the intake air-switch flap described above (street air vs. cabin recirculation) has part number GV7D61A70. The flap clicks audibly when it activates automatically during reverse gear or windshield-washer operation, and can also be triggered manually — this clicking is normal operation. If the clicking becomes excessive/frequent beyond the normal reverse/washer triggers, the actuator itself may need replacement 171819.

    Recirculation-flap motor mechanical failure — stripped gear, Mazda 6 replacement: separately from the disconnect/disable workarounds above, the flap actuator motor itself can fail mechanically. Symptom: the motor makes a straining/buzzing sound but the flap doesn't move. Root cause: the factory FAW motor has a fragile plastic intermediate gear inside the gearbox that strips its teeth (one owner's motor lost 3 teeth from the intermediate gear). Fix: replace with a Mazda 6 recirculation-flap motor — physically the same part with the same 3-pin connector; a common source is a used unit from Avito 20212223.

    Restyle (2019+) intake damper motor — non-response symptom and alternate part number: on a 2019 restyle model, the intake-damper motor stopped actuating entirely when the button was pressed — it makes an effort sound ("пыхтит") but the flap doesn't move, leaving the car stuck in one position (worked around by manually forcing the flap open to fresh air to stop fogging). A part number GV7D61A60 was given for this motor — close to, but not identical to, the GV7D61A70 actuator part number reported elsewhere in this card; it's unclear whether this reflects a different generation/variant or a typo, so treat both numbers as candidates to verify against your VIN/parts catalog before ordering 242526.
