    TPMS sensors — 5GA/5BA reports, programming, IDs

    Tags: #tpms · #sensor · #part-number · #tire-pressure

    Prices and suppliers on this page are Russian-market (RUB, at the dates shown). Verify locally before relying on them in Syria.

    Some advice here concerns sub-zero winters and is less relevant in a hot climate.

    Supplement to TPMS tire pressure sensors: part-number reports, programming pitfalls and ID-reading limits from the 2025 threads. Related cards: TPMS sensors — part number and pricing, TPMS sensor cloning when installing aftermarket wheels, TPMS sensor battery type and design; the sensor's controller wiring is in Wiring diagram (EWD) circuits and connectors.

    Part numbers: 5GA037973 vs 5BA037973. The X80FC2 parts catalog lists the TPMS sensor as part code 5GA037973 in the wheel-and-tyre group 3700A01X80F; the same group lists tyres 215/60R17 96V (Hankook, FQ9062016717) and 185/70R17 (Wanli, FQ9062017687). The catalog gives no frequency, battery or price 1. One member fitted 5BA037973 bought on Ozon and reports it working through a winter, about a year on. A member says 5BA037973 is for the pre-restyle X80 and the X40 and that the X80 sensor starts with 5GA, whereas one supplier told another owner that 5BA037973 is the restyle number; the two statements conflict. A set of 4 sensors listed on AliExpress as 5GA037973C for FAW Bestune 2018-2024 (T77) was priced ~7,459 RUB (RU market, Nov 2025) 23456.

    A new sensor must be programmed. A replacement 5GA037973 has to be programmed after fitting: either the old sensor's ID is read and cloned into the new one, or the ID is read from the car over OBD and written into the sensor 78. A Launch TPMS LTR-03 tool (433 + 315 MHz) was listed for this in Nov 2025 9.

    New sensor not accepted / dead after a battery swap. One owner's new 5GA037973 was not accepted by the car and the supplier said it belongs to a different system; the workaround described is to leave the old battery in place with its tabs cut and fit a new cell outside 1011. In another case a sensor did not work after a battery swap; the replies blamed wrong polarity or failing to disconnect the old cell, and the sensor eventually proved dead 1213.

    Reading and cloning IDs. One owner pulled the sensor IDs from the ECU via OBD-II with an Autel scanner; another says Diagzone Pro reads the IDs virtually from the ECU. A member states a sensor ID cannot be cloned onto a second wheel set because the sensors are not programmable 141516.

    Aftermarket FS43X0L5 and the OEM label. Aftermarket sensors for the X80 are sold as FS43X0L5; one owner bought an Autel-branded 4-pack, other options mentioned are a metal-bodied 4-pack and an NFC-type set. The OEM sensor label reads FS43X0L5-181218-050549BD with a DataMatrix code 17181920.
