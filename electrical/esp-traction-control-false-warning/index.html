    ESP/traction-control "drunk road" warning

    Tags: #esp · #abs

    Prices and suppliers on this page are Russian-market (RUB, at the dates shown). Verify locally before relying on them in Syria.

    Some advice here concerns sub-zero winters and is less relevant in a hot climate.

    Additional causes and fixes (ESC light specifically). Beyond the wheel-spin trigger and firmware fixes above, the ESC warning can also be caused by: (1) dirt/mud contamination on the ABS sensors from off-road driving or poor road conditions; (2) loose wiring shaken by suspension vibration; (3) in some cases, an aftermarket radio installation with improper grounding affecting the ESC module. Suggested checks in order: visually inspect all four ABS sensors for dirt/damage; check all connections; if the light appeared right after a radio replacement, revisit that installation's grounding. A diagnostic scanner is recommended; without one, a professional diagnostic visit is the fallback 12345678 910.

    Multiple owners report an ESP/traction-control warning light — nicknamed "drunk road" (пьяная дорога) after the on-dash slippery-road icon — firing after wheel-spin events, sometimes together with an automatic parking-brake engagement. Reported triggers: backing up with the wheel at full lock while spinning the wheels 111213; accelerating hard from a standstill in 1st–3rd gear on snow/gravel causing wheel slip 14151617; and sliding/skidding at speed 18. This is largely normal ABS/ESP operation on low-traction surfaces — the "drunk road" indicator lights up as part of the stability system reacting to detected slip, and the felt handbrake intervention is usually less prominent than expected 1619. Disabling ESP via its dashboard button reduces false activations and lets the wheels spin more freely, which some owners deliberately do on inclines or in sand/snow 2021. Tire tread/compound appears to affect sensitivity — some owners report no issue at all under similar conditions 22 2324.

    Light can persist after triggering until cleared. Fixes reported: a firmware update flashed via USB resolves it permanently on some units, with firmware files circulated in the community 25262728 29; disconnecting the negative battery terminal overnight also resolved it for some 3031; a full ECU reflash at a service center is another documented fix, at roughly 1500 RUB just to read the error code (RU market, December 2024) 323334. Alternative causes raised for a stuck/lit indicator specifically (as opposed to the transient wheel-spin trigger): incorrect brake-pad installation affecting the hill-hold function, or a malfunctioning tire-pressure sensor 29.

    Conflict: the exact mechanical trigger and the most reliable fix are both disputed — reported fixes (battery reset vs. rewiring by an electrician vs. full ECU reflash) worked for different owners, suggesting more than one underlying cause depending on trim/configuration 3536 37.

    ESP error after steering-wheel replacement — clearable with a Launch tool. A Launch X431 diagnostic tool can read X80 parameters and clear ESP error codes that appear on base-model vehicles after a steering-wheel replacement. This is a practical workaround where local service centers lack full dealer-level diagnostic capability 3839.

    Steering-angle-sensor false error at certain steering angles — likely a self-check quirk, not a real fault. A warning light/error code can appear at certain steering angles (especially mid-turn), which looks like sensor self-check behavior specific to early ownership: it beeps continuously on the very first startup, then behaves differently on subsequent startups. One way to sanity-check whether it's a real fault: drive slowly next to a wall/straight reference and see whether the error clears on its own. One experienced member's advice: avoid buying replacement steering-angle sensors from Ozon, as they're considered unreliable 404142434445.

    ESP light after multimedia/head-unit replacement — steering-angle calibration loss. A distinct trigger from the "drunk road" cases documented elsewhere in this card: the ESP warning light often comes on after a multimedia (head) unit replacement on 2018+ models. Root cause: the ECU loses its steering-angle-sensor calibration during the swap. This is not a mechanical failure — the fix is to have a certified technician run a steering-angle-sensor calibration through diagnostic software, or update the comfort-module firmware 46474849505152.

    "Slippery road" warning — unequal tire pressure or ESP miscalibration. Another ESP-adjacent warning, "slippery road" (скользкая дорога), has two leading causes: (1) unequal tire pressure between wheels — the most common cause, check all four pressures first; (2) ESP sensor miscalibration. Troubleshooting: turn the steering wheel hard right then hard left (a basic recalibration procedure) and verify all four tire pressures are equal. If the warning persists after both checks, official service is needed for a proper ESP recalibration 53545556.
