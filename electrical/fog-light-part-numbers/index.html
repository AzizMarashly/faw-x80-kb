    Fog light part numbers (front and rear)

    Tags: #fog-light · #part-number · #led

    Prices and suppliers on this page are Russian-market (RUB, at the dates shown). Verify locally before relying on them in Syria.

    Front fog lights (shared with FAW Besturn B50): FC025189X (right), FC025169X (left). For the X80 New (2018+): frame brackets 5CA250C1102 (right), 5CA250C2102 (left). LED lamp alternatives available from 460–580 RUB per lamp (RU market, April 2024), readily available from online retailers 123.

    Fog light bezel pricing confirmation and light/dark variants: separately confirms the facelift (2018+) frame bracket/bezel part 5CA250C1102 noted above — available in both light and dark finish versions, priced around 1,200 RUB, from COOVE.ru and similar retailers. A comparable pre-facelift bezel is also available at similar pricing 45678.

    Rear fog lamp: right-side part number 5CA0515L5A (red housing), used for an affected 2018 restyle model. Available through Russian auto-parts retailers such as kitzapchasti.ru 910.

    Auto Mento H11 LED retrofit: aftermarket LED fog lights (Auto Mento H11 spec) sourced from Ozon (link: https://ozon.ru/t/vdK1bKB) work as a drop-in replacement for the stock halogen fog lights. Fitment requires 4 self-tapping screws (vs. 3 stock) and minor mounting-bracket adjustment. The LED version shows significantly improved brightness over stock halogen 1112131415.

    Fog light fastener count differs stock vs. aftermarket: the stock fog light mounts with 3 self-tapping screws; aftermarket units (e.g. Auto Mento H11) need 4 screws, since mounting-hole positions differ between OEM and universal aftermarket housings. Fitment works with adjustment — using 4 screws holds better than 3, and the resulting gap/misalignment is minimal ("small displacement" after adjustment). Shims on the horizontal axis may be needed to center the beam properly 16171819202122232425.

    Fog light physical dimensions (X80 vs. aftermarket): stock X80 fog light mounting dimensions — 8.5 cm inner lens diameter, ~10.5 cm outer height, 11.5 cm outer width at full assembly width, 15.5 mm cross-section/bracket depth. AliExpress-sourced aftermarket fog lights show a similar overall envelope but minor mounting-hole position differences (4-screw vs. stock 3-screw). Universal fog lights sold for Lada Priora/Gazelle do NOT fit the X80 due to different mounting-bracket geometry 26272829.

    Front fog lamp bulb replacement without full bumper removal (restyle): on the restyle version, the bulb can be accessed without removing the whole bumper — left side: remove the air-filter housing; right side: pull back the fender liner or remove a trim panel. Takes minimal time compared to full bumper removal 3031.

    Left fog light reliability issue (7-year ownership): one owner experienced repeated failures of the left fog light only over 7 years of ownership — the left side kept overheating and bulbs burned out, while the right factory light never needed replacement. Suggests either a heat/ ventilation problem or a wiring/connector issue specific to that side's circuit 3233343536.

    Brightness comparison — LED retrofit vs. stock halogen: side-by-side testing of Auto Mento H11 LED fog lights against 7-year-old stock halogen units shows the LED version gives significantly brighter, whiter illumination and a more uniform beam, eliminating the yellowish halogen tint. A near/far beam-pattern video comparison after retrofitting both sides showed roughly equal intensity between them 14373839.

    Cracked/chipped lens — temporary and permanent repair: a fog-light lens cracked/chipped by a road stone can be temporarily sealed with aquarium silicone sealant plus waterproof tape to keep water out until a proper replacement is sourced. Do not use a car wash/high-pressure spray until sealed — water entering the lamp housing through the crack causes a short circuit and an expensive lamp failure (700+ RUB); avoid turning the fog light on at all if the lens is compromised, since running it while cracked risks the lens shattering if water contacts the hot glass. Apply the sealant carefully and let it fully dry, then order a replacement lens as the permanent fix — some owners report success sealing small cracks with epoxy as well 40414243444546.

    Rear fog lights — factory part number and sourcing: FAW factory part number for the rear fog light is 5CA1515M5 (restyle, 2018+) — separate from the right-side part 5CA0515L5A noted above; the two numbers likely correspond to different sides/positions of the housing. Non-OEM sources on Ozon are often cheaper than Yandex Market for the same part number; some vendors also offer circular (round-lens) fog-light variants. Price varies significantly for the same nominal part number across platforms — 1,200–2,700 RUB (RU market, June 2025) — so it's worth comparison-shopping. Pre-restyle and restyle parts differ in appearance despite similar part numbering 4748495051525354555657585960.

    Rear fog light removal procedure (no bumper removal needed): (1) disconnect the two bolts at the fog-light housing; (2) detach the clips accessible from the inner panel; (3) remove the two bolts at the top of the assembly; (4) slide the fog light out. Replacement units (left rear: part 5CA1515M5) are available on Wildberries and on Avito as used parts. The lens cover is not sold separately — a cracked/damaged lens requires replacing the full assembly 61626364656667686970.

    Pre-facelift fog light diameter (measured): standard pre-facelift X80 fog lights measure approximately 70 mm in diameter, as measured with a caliper. Useful for sourcing replacement fog lights or matching optics 7172.

    Rear fog-lamp trim bezels — pre-restyled only, extremely rare (January 2026): rear fog-lamp trim rings/bezels (окантовка задних ПТФ) for the pre-restyled (first-generation) FAW X80 are an extremely rare OEM part. As of January 2026, one seller reported having the last unit available in Russia. Originally sourced from Taobao ~4 years prior for 3,800 RUB (RU market, January 2026); asking price for the piece: 3,000 RUB (RU market, January 2026) 73.
