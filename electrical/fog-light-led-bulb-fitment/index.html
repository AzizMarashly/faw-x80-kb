    Fog light LED bulb fitment (H11 socket)

    Tags: #fog-light · #fitment

    Prices and suppliers on this page are Russian-market (RUB, at the dates shown). Verify locally before relying on them in Syria.

    Fog-light switch: Chinese-restyle vs Russian-domestic wiring difference. Separate from bulb fitment, the fog-light switch differs by market variant: the Chinese-restyle X80 has front AND rear fog-light control on the steering-column switch stalk, with a separate connector plug for it; the Russian-domestic version's stalk only controls the front fogs. This is a wiring/harness difference at the switch, not a difference in the fog-light motor/hardware itself — front-wheel-drive models use the same physical fog-light motor regardless of switch variant. For anyone wanting rear-fog control the Russian-domestic stalk doesn't provide, aftermarket options exist: full AliExpress fog-light kits with add-on buttons (non-OEM appearance) 1234 56789.

    The fog lights use an H11 socket, so an H11-based LED bulb is the correct starting point 10. However, many LED bulbs sold generically as "H11" are actually designed for headlight (low-beam) housings rather than fog lights, and won't physically fit: their lens/heatsink shroud extends to roughly 20mm in diameter, while the fog-light housing opening is only about 18mm — close enough on paper to cause confusion when ordering, but too large in practice 1112. A specific failure mode reported: bulbs bought as "H11" turned out to be H11 LED units with an integrated lens, meant for headlight/high-beam-style projector housings — what's actually needed for a fog light retrofit is a plain diode-style H11 bulb without that lens assembly 13. Fog light housing depth is 84mm — check bulb length against this before buying, in addition to the diameter issue 1415. Practical advice: measure/compare diameter and length carefully (or search specifically for "fog light" LED variants rather than generic H11 headlight LEDs) before ordering, and be prepared to return a bulb that doesn't physically fit even though the socket type matches.

    Bulb type by model year — front vs rear, pre- vs post-facelift. Consolidating community reports on socket types: pre-facelift models use H11 for the front fog lamps and W16W for the rear fog lamps 16. Post-2020 facelift models also use H11 for the front; some facelift trims may use P21W instead, but H11-based universal aftermarket alternatives are reported to give noticeably better illumination than the factory fog bulbs regardless of which stock type your car has 1718192021. LED upgrade kits for these sockets are widely available on AliExpress, with discounts up to roughly 54% reported 20.

    Aftermarket fog lamps with integrated lens (ZMB brand). Beyond straight bulb swaps, some owners have upgraded to complete ZMB-brand fog lamp units with an integrated lens (giving a sharp light/dark cutoff line rather than a diffuse beam), still using the H11 bulb type and fitting directly into the factory fog-light housing. Reported benefit: noticeably improved visibility in dark conditions, especially illuminating the area just ahead of the bumper that factory fogs light poorly 222324.

    Rear fog light activation on restyled 2019+ automatic models — no separate switch. Directly extending the switch-difference notes above: on restyled (2019+) automatic-transmission X80s, the rear fog lights are not controlled by a separate button — activation is via rotating the ring element on the steering-column light stalk itself to its maximum position. This only works when low beams are already switched on; if rotating the ring doesn't produce rear fog light, check the fuse next 252627282930. This resolves what otherwise looks like a missing feature — the control exists, it's just non-obvious (a rotatable ring rather than a discrete switch) if you don't already know to look for it.

    Aftermarket LED fog-light options and "lensed" terminology confusion. LED fog lights can be successfully adapted to the X80. Regular diode LED fog lights (light pattern formed via an internal reflector, not a lens) are commonly sold at 30W, 50W, or 70W power levels; true lensed fog lights (with an external protruding or internal lens element that actually shapes the beam) are a separate product category. Some owners mounted lens-less LED flood units behind aftermarket housings; multiple owners confirmed 360°-type LEDs also fit in the fog-light housings. Brightness at 6000K (white) is excellent and may warm toward 4300K over time as the LEDs age. 30W units are the most common choice; 50–70W units are extremely bright 3132 3334353637383940 4142434445464748. Conflict: owners frequently debate whether their installed LED fog lights are genuinely "lensed" (линзованные) — most are actually reflector-based LEDs, not true lensed units; true lensed fog lights have a distinct external/internal lens element forming the beam, whereas reflector-based LEDs shape light purely via internal reflector geometry.

    Fog-light bulb spec for the 2018 restyle: H11. The 2018-restyle X80 uses H11 halogen bulbs for the front fog lights. One owner found the previous owner had installed mismatched LED bulbs between the left and right fenders — the recommendation is to stick with standard H11 halogen, or if going LED, use a matched pair rather than mixed brands 4950.

    Universal 70 W fog lamps (EAL, 5500K), September 2026. One owner fitted universal fog lamps (ПТФ, противотуманные фары) from EAL: 70 W, 5500K white, 12 V, 120-degree beam, listed on Ozon at ~2,131 RUB (RU market, September 2026; struck-through price 6,990 RUB), 365-day warranty. The brackets had to be improvised and the headlight levelling re-aimed to fit them 5152. Another owner warned that such bright white fog lamps blind oncoming drivers, while the installer says they do no harm when properly aimed 5352.
