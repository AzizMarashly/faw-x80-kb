    Rear parking sensors — diagnostics and replacement

    Tags: #parking-sensor · #diagnostics · #price

    Prices and suppliers on this page are Russian-market (RUB, at the dates shown). Verify locally before relying on them in Syria.

    Mounting and wiring. The 4 rear parking sensors (parktronics, ПАРКТРОНИКИ) are glued with double-sided tape inside the rear bumper. Each sensor has its own independent connector and the sensors are wired in parallel, not in series (unlike some competitor systems), so one failed sensor does not take down the others 12345678910111213141516171819202122.

    Diagnostics. Water or dirt ingress causes false beeping. Several checks were reported: - Click test: ignition on, reverse engaged; working sensors click when touched 12345678910. Ultrasonic sensors give no physical vibration when pressed with a finger, so a finger press is not a valid manual test 111213141516171819202122. - Wall test (reliable): drive to within 20-30 cm of a wall, engage reverse and watch the display; working sensors show their response for about 1 second before the reading drops off 111213141516171819202122. - Voice-recorder app test: with reverse engaged, hold the phone microphone tip against each sensor and watch the waveform; a working sensor shows frequency changes compared with the surrounding bumper (baseline). One owner confirmed it tells a dead sensor from a working one without a scan tool 23242526. - Error without sound: with reverse engaged, a sensor error on the dashboard but no beeping means the sensors need replacing 27282930.

    Used cars. Non-working rear parktronics are frequently found on used X80s; several buyers report inheriting them as a common issue 27282930.

    Control module. The parktronic control module is on the right side of the trunk, toward the rear seats; reaching it means removing the rear trim panel (and the interior upholstery panel). If the sensors start misbehaving shortly after a car wash, first check the module's electrical contacts for corrosion or moisture before blaming a sensor 31323334353637383940.

    Access and removal. Two reports differ on how much of the bumper must come off: - All sensors can be removed without taking off the rear bumper's silver trim panel. The central sensors are easiest; the outer ones need thin hands or flexible tools but are still removable. Each sensor is held by two clips (top and bottom) that must be pried apart 41424344454647. Another route is from underneath via the trim panel, or removing the gray plastic trim for easier access 12345678910. - Another owner recommends removing the rear bumper completely for better access, a cleaner installation and proper tape contact for the new sensor: about 15 minutes with the bumper off, noticeably harder and slower without removing it (the alternative being the lower gray bumper cover panel, with limited working room) 4849.

    Replacement. Replacement sensors are aftermarket-available and fairly universal 12345678910. The mechanism is a simple side clip, taking under 1 minute per sensor with some dexterity 111213141516171819202122. After the part swap, plan a code-clear step: replacing sensors flagged as faulty (positions "2 and 3" glitching) did not clear the dashboard error until the stored codes were cleared with a scan tool at a service centre (2024-08 report); the community compares this to rear brake pad replacement, which likewise does not clear related stored codes 5051.

    Part number, cost and failure rate. OEM sensor part number 5GA179120; replacement about 1,625 RUB per sensor (RU market, July 2025) 111213141516171819202122. An earlier report (RU market, May 2024) gave the sensor at about 750 RUB plus labour of about 1,000 RUB per sensor; some owners replaced 4 sensors in 5 years, suggesting a fairly high failure rate, and the official dealer warranty may cover it in the first year of ownership 52.
