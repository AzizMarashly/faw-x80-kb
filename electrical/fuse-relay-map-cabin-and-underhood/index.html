    Fuse and relay map — cabin and under-hood blocks

    Tags: #fuse-box · #relay · #reference · #airbag · #restyle

    This card reproduces the factory fuse-and-relay diagram that circulates in the group as the file "Предохранители.pdf" (fuses, "предохранители"), labelled in English and Russian. It has three drawings: the cabin (salon) fuse block, the under-hood fuse block and the under-hood relay block beside it. The values below were read from the text extracted from the PDF, so the pairing of a label with its amperage is approximate and must be checked against the picture or the real box 1234. For the older community-built description of the same blocks and the pre-facelift vs facelift differences see Fuse box layout — pre-facelift vs facelift; the Si-numbered fuses of the wiring diagram are in Wiring diagram (EWD) fuses and relays.

    Cabin relays on the same block: FRONT CLEAN MOTOR RLY (РЕЛЕ ПЕРЕДНИХ СТЕКЛООЧИСТИТЕЛЕЙ), REAR CLEAN MOTOR RLY and REAR WIPER RLY (РЕЛЕ ЗАДНИХ СТЕКЛООЧИСТИТЕЛЕЙ) 1234.

    Under-hood relay positions 1234: on the fuse block - COMPRESSOR RLY (РЕЛЕ КОМПРЕССОРА), MAIN RLY (ГЛАВНОЕ РЕЛЕ), H/L LOW RLY (РЕЛЕ БЛИЖНЕГО СВЕТА), H/L HIGH RLY (РЕЛЕ ДАЛЬНЕГО СВЕТА), TNS RLY (РЕЛЕ ПОВОРОТОВ, ГАБАРИТОВ). In the relay block next to the fuses - START RLY (РЕЛЕ ЗАПУСКА), START ACCESSORY RLY (РЕЛЕ ОБОРУДОВАНИЯ ЗАПУСКА), HORN RLY, BLOWER RLY, FUEL PUMP RLY, DEFROST RLY, FAN 1 RLY, FAN 2 RLY, R.FOG LAMP RLY, F.FOG LAMP RLY, R.F. SEAT WARM RLY and L.F. SEAT WARM RLY, one empty (ПУСТО) position, and two FUSE CLIP spare-fuse holders (ЗАЖИМ ПРЕДОХРАНИТЕЛЯ).

    Differences from the earlier screenshot reading. The screenshot of the same file summarised in the older fuse card gives some ratings differently from the text extraction above (for example sunroof 30 A instead of 20 A, IG KEY 15 A instead of 40 A, oxygen sensor 10 A instead of 15 A, a 5 A backup light) 56. Both readings are reported; the Si-numbered fuse list of the wiring diagram agrees with the text extraction for fuel pump 20 A, TCU 15 A, airbag 10 A, audio 25 A, ignition coil 15 A, oxygen sensor 15 A, valve body 20 A and compressor 10 A 7. Treat any single reading as a starting point and confirm on the car.
