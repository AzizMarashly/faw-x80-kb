    LED headlight bulbs — recommendations and fitment

    Tags: #led · #headlight · #fog-light

    Prices and suppliers on this page are Russian-market (RUB, at the dates shown). Verify locally before relying on them in Syria.

    Fitment fix: LED lamp sockets often have thinner contact tabs than OEM halogen lamps, causing a loose fit in the lamp holder. Fix: wrap electrical tape around the LED lamp's base contacts to build up thickness and get a snug fit — this prevents lamp vibration and intermittent contact 1234.

    Recommendations: Carshark 12V LED lamps (available on Ozon) are recommended for cost and light quality. Use a warm 4300K color temperature for both headlights and fog lights to match the factory aesthetic. Previous HID/Xenon lamps can burn the reflector/lens coating; LED lamps don't have this issue. LED 360° 4300K lamps give good output for nighttime visibility 56789.

    LED tail-light bulb replacement: LED replacement bulbs for the brake lights can be installed without additional adapters or circuits — a direct replacement works with the OEM socket (low-confidence report) 1011.

    Bulb replacement without removing the bumper: headlight bulbs can be swapped without pulling the front bumper, by working through the wheel arch. Procedure: pop out the ~3 plastic plugs/caps in the wheel arch liner to create a gap between the liner and the fender. The driver side is easier — a hand can be threaded in from above, between the air filter housing and the headlight. The passenger side is harder, obstructed by the washer-fluid reservoir; reaching around it and back out through the gap between the arch liner and fender takes more effort (thin/small hands help). For a fully cracked lens that needs replacing (not just the bulb), partial bolt removal via a ratchet from the top, combined with working through the wheel-arch gap, is enough — the lens can be pulled out (and back in) through the same gap without full bumper removal. Wearing gloves/mitts is recommended, since the gap has sharp edges that can cut hands 12.

    Yellow-tinted H11 bulbs — poor brightness (avoid for primary use): H11 yellow-tinted bulbs produce the intended yellow glow but with very poor brightness/light output — described as inadequate. Not recommended as primary headlight bulbs 13.

    Headlight bulb type and recommended LED replacement: the X80 uses a single HIR2 (9012) bulb socket for both high beam and low beam. Recommended LED alternatives: DAWNKNIGHT K7C at 4300K (110W) — praised by multiple owners for brightness and color temperature; Red Stil brand also performs well. Caveat: LED bulbs with heat sinks struggle in the X80's sealed headlight housing, which limits cooling airflow — output can be ~20% lower than in open-design headlight fixtures on other cars. Cost: LED bulb pairs run 1,600–1,700 RUB (RU market, July 2025); halogen alternatives are cheaper but have a shorter lifespan 14151617181920212223242526.

    Xenon vs. LED — community split, no clear consensus: when asked which low-beam bulb upgrade is best, community opinions split. One long-time owner runs xenon, purchased a while ago (specific product links no longer saved/available), and reports not liking LED due to unspecified "nuances," though acknowledges many other owners are happy with LED. This suggests the LED-vs-halogen fitment/output caveats noted above (sealed housing limiting cooling, ~20% output loss) may be part of why some owners still prefer xenon despite LED being the community's more commonly recommended path 2728.

    LED bulb single-diode failure — halogen swap workaround: LED headlight bulb failure can show as one side burning white (normal) while the other burns weak/blue — a sign that one internal LED diode has died. To replace: remove the LED bulb's adapter/mounting ring from the headlight housing, pull out the old bulb, and insert a halogen bulb as a temporary fix while sourcing a replacement LED. ⚠️ Note: the adapter/mounting rings are fragile and may break during removal 29303132333435363738.

    Headlamp socket type, self-installation, and beam-height adjustment: the X80 headlamps use an HIR2/9012 socket (both near and far beam share one bulb). LED bulbs fit this socket and can be self-installed by removing the lamp housing — no special tools needed, and installation takes only minutes. Polarity does not matter for some LED bulbs (per one owner's confirmed test). After installing new bulbs, beam height can be adjusted using the exterior adjustment dial — no professional alignment stand is required, though a proper alignment remains optional/recommended. If new bulbs cause glare to oncoming traffic, lower the beam dial slightly. Popular upgrade options: MTF Cyber Light (~6,000 RUB, RU market, March 2026); budget alternatives (~1,500 RUB, RU market, March 2026); and Novsight N37 LED (recommended, see below) 394041424344454647484950515253545556.

    Novsight N37 LED bulbs (HIR2/9012, 120W-equivalent pair): the Novsight N37 LED headlamp bulbs, sized for the HIR2/9012 socket above, are sold as a 120W-equivalent pair and available on OZON. One owner ordered them and confirmed they're a good fit for the X80 54.

    DawnKnight K7C — full part number confirmed: the full part number for the DawnKnight HIR2/9012 4300K 110W bulb set (2 bulbs) referenced above is K7C-4300K-110W-9012. Owners describe it as a "light bomb" upgrade — reportedly excellent brightness improvement over stock 575859.

    Early independent corroboration — HIR2 single-bulb type and K7C 110W brand, with a power-rating clarification: an earlier, independent thread corroborates two points above: (1) the X80 headlight uses a single HIR2 bulb type for both beams; (2) a K7C 110W HIR2 upgrade bulb (available via online retailers) was recommended and purchased successfully for improved low-light penetration over the stock 55W bulbs. Power clarification: the "110W" rating is the combined power of both headlight bulbs (2×55W each) — this matches the factory-stock bulbs' total power as well, so a "110W" aftermarket bulb set is not inherently more powerful than stock on a per-bulb basis; any brightness gain comes from bulb efficiency/technology rather than raw wattage 6061626364.

    In-progress LED headlight install — Ozon sourcing, price (low confidence): one owner installed a set of aftermarket LED headlight lamps purchased on Ozon for 2,452 RUB (RU market, July 2026) and booked a professional headlight-adjustment appointment for the following week to finish dialing in the beam; performance evaluation was still pending at report time 65.
