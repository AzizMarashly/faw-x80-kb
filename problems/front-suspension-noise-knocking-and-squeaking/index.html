    Front suspension knocking and squeaking noise

    Tags: #squeak · #control-arm · #brake-pads

    Prices and suppliers on this page are Russian-market (RUB, at the dates shown). Verify locally before relying on them in Syria.

    Some advice here concerns sub-zero winters and is less relevant in a hot climate.

    Knocking noise from the front suspension over 50k km. Symptom: an audible knocking sound from the front suspension area while driving, reported at 50k–80k km mileage. A service diagnostic on one car found: worn brake pads, worn rear brake dust shields, upper rear control arms worn and requiring replacement (typically both sides), damaged cabin seals at the door latches, and general looseness of brake system components1. Likely root causes: worn suspension components (control arms), loose brake dust shields, or deteriorated cabin seals. Recommendation: perform a detailed front-end inspection covering control-arm ball joints, stabilizer links, and brake dust-shield fasteners2.

    Squeaking at low speed near the front wheel. A separate, more common complaint: squeaking at low speed near a front wheel. Checklist: (1) brake pad wear and dust-cover pockets for trapped rocks/debris — the most common cause34; (2) if pads were recently replaced, verify their quality — poor-quality pads squeak when heated5; (3) inspect caliper guides and the piston, which may be sticking if not fully retracting6; (4) winter-specific: ball joints in the lower front control arms can squeak during suspension movement in cold weather7; (5) as a last resort, check for moisture and surface coating buildup on caliper hardware8.

    Acceleration jerks traced to the front lower arm. One owner traced jerks under acceleration to his front lower control arm (нижний рычаг). The original from a Chinese-parts shop (китзапчасти) was of poor quality and removing it took heat and cutting910. He replaced it with a MASUMA arm, which he found excellent, for about 4,700 RUB (RU market, October 2026)9. He could not knock the bolt out of the fork and the bushing pulled out with it, so he recommends a puller (съёмник)11.
