    Catalytic converter failure — P0420/P0430 codes

    Tags: #catalytic-converter

    Check-engine light from catalyst-efficiency codes at low mileage. Multiple owners report the check-engine light coming on with P0420/P0430 (catalytic converter efficiency) codes at mileage as low as 38,000–63,000 km123. Endoscopic inspection often reveals catalyst substrate clogging, visible as darker deposits in the honeycomb cells45. Diagnostic tools used: Car Scanner OBD2 app and an ELM327 scanner, both of which show the catalyst-efficiency code678. Solutions: replace the catalyst outright, or remove it and reprogram the ECU to delete the code (a common but emissions-defeating workaround), or try an additive fuel treatment first91011.

    Catastrophic catalyst failure from a lean fuel mixture. In a separate, more serious case, an owner reported complete failure (melting) of the rear catalytic converter after a prolonged lean fuel-mixture condition caused by poor fuel injectors and possibly a bad ECU tune. The converter melted after the vehicle ran on short trips without adequate warm-up time for several months12. Symptom: loss of power during highway driving after long cold starts. Root cause identified: both injectors on cylinder 2 were over-fueling (flooding the chamber), which paradoxically created a lean condition downstream that overheated the rear catalyst. Poor ECU firmware versions were suspected to make this worse.

    Don't condemn the catalyst on mileage or a lambda code alone. At 81,000 km, an owner asked whether the catalytic converter needed removal; community advice was that at this mileage the converter likely does not need immediate replacement, and even at 100,000 km the honeycomb internals are usually still intact — an endoscope inspection is recommended to verify condition before removing anything1314. Separately, a lambda-sensor fault code ("зонт"/oxygen sensor reading the mixture incorrectly, triggering the check-engine light) does not necessarily mean the catalyst itself has failed — it more likely means the oxygen sensor needs replacement instead1516. General advice: get a second diagnostic opinion from a specialist, ideally one experienced with catalysts specifically, before condemning the converter171816.

    Lambda sensor replacement when diagnosis confirms it. When diagnostics do show the lambda/oxygen sensor itself misreading the fuel mixture and triggering the check-engine light, replacement of that sensor (not the catalyst) is the recommended fix — have a qualified technician confirm exactly which sensor is at fault and source the correct replacement part1516.

    Catalyst removal and flame-suppressor conversion — a low-confidence, community-discussed workaround. For owners who decide the catalyst is damaged beyond repair and want to avoid replacement cost, one documented approach is to remove the catalytic converter, weld in a flame suppressor (пламягаситель) in its place, and reprogram/reflash the ECU accordingly192021. This was raised in the context of a restyled X80 owner whose catalyst was audibly rattling ("звенит катализатор")20. Confidence in this approach is low — it is an emissions-defeating workaround (similar in spirit to the code-deletion workaround described above) and the thread did not report a verified outcome; treat as anecdotal.
