    PDC sensor issues after bumper removal

    Tags: #bumper

    Errors after rear bumper removal. Multiple users reported parking-sensor errors appearing after the rear bumper was removed for unrelated maintenance work. Common causes: a connector not fully re-seated, a damaged wire inside the connector, or sensor positions swapped during reassembly123. The system allows sensor position swapping without immediately triggering an error code, which can make the fault confusing to trace456. Temporary workaround: apply thick transparent 3M tape over the malfunctioning sensor to stop the beeping while arranging a repair. Dealership replacement is possible under warranty789. Community dispute: HS Development argued that all stock sensors are electrically identical and shouldn't error if swapped, but user experience showed problems occurring after swaps — the likely resolution is that some non-OEM replacement sensors have enough drift/variance that position-matching does matter in practice10.

    Non-original sensors from Ozone marketplace. Separately, non-original PDC sensors bought on the Ozone marketplace showed internal resistance/impedance problems (0–10Ω across all directions instead of the proper impedance curve), causing persistent ERROR codes with the engine running — even though the sensors buzzed normally when bench-tested1112. Original OEM sensors worked reliably by comparison. Root cause: a faulty internal potentiometer assembly in the cheap sensors13141516. Solution: buy original OEM sensors, or carefully vetted third-party units with a verified proper impedance curve — check seller reviews closely171819. Some owners found partial success by flushing/reseating the BCM connections or checking grounds, but sensor quality was identified as the primary issue202122232425.

    Rear parking-sensor error that clears itself. One owner saw a rear parking-sensor error that cleared on its own and suspected a frozen control unit26. Victor says the failing part is an individual parking sensor (парктроник), not the control unit: once a sensor starts dying the error keeps coming back27. Igor agrees that sensors fail on their own and suggests checking by reversing close to a wall and watching the display28. Victor points to a Drive2 guide with a step "find which parking sensor is faulty"29.
