    Air filter compatibility — wrong part numbers

    Tags: #air-filter · #part-number · #cross-compatibility

    Some air filters listed for sale as fitting the X80 are actually made for other FAW models (B50, B70). Parts even marked with what looks like the correct X80 article number can arrive with a different form factor that does not fit123. Recommendation: verify the physical fit before completing a purchase, and cross-check the article number against multiple community sources rather than trusting the listing alone4567.
