    Folding side mirror gear slippage in cold weather

    Tags: #mirrors · #cold-weather · #winter

    Some advice here concerns sub-zero winters and is less relevant in a hot climate.

    In freezing conditions, folding side mirrors can disconnect from the drive mechanism if ice/frost buildup increases friction: when the motor tries to close the mirror, the internal gear skips and disengages12345. Recovery: manually open the mirror by hand to re-seat the gears, or apply de-icing spray on the hinges6789. If snow/ice is coating the mirror frame, it prevents smooth operation until cleared101112. Disabling the auto-fold function for winter is an available workaround1314.
