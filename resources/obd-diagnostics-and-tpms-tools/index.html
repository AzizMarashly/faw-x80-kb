    OBD diagnostic scanners, software, and TPMS tools

    Tags: #obd · #diagnostics · #scanner · #tpms · #elm327 · #battery

    Prices and suppliers on this page are Russian-market (RUB, at the dates shown). Verify locally before relying on them in Syria.

    Software/platform. Diagzone (X431 ecosystem) is the community's main diagnostic software; the Diagzone Pro subscription covers 650+ vehicle brands and includes the X80 12. It pairs with a cheap Mucar BT200 Wi-Fi/OBD dongle re-flashed to run the Diagzone backend, which can read the whole car (not just the engine) 12. A "FAWCARS" module for Diagzone with updates specific to facelift (2019+) models is needed to read those cars correctly 3. The Android app CarMin contains wiring schematics (electrical diagrams) for the X80, separate from any scan tool 4.

    Hardware options. - Mucar/Makar BT200 + Diagzone Pro: the community's default budget combo, ~8,000 RUB for the dongle alone (RU market, October 2024) 5; needs the Diagzone firmware flashed onto it (or paying an extra 8,000–10,000 RUB (RU market, October 2024) for a pre-flashed unit) to unlock full-vehicle diagnostics 6. Bought without flashing, it reads engine (ECU) codes only, and even then not on every model 7. For transmission/electrical fault diagnosis, professional shop diagnostics (with a mechanic, not just the scanner) is recommended 8. As of 2024-12-27, community pricing for a minimal working setup was: Mucar BT200 hardware (~10,000–25,000 RUB retail) + Diagzone software/FAWCARS module (~720 RUB) (RU market, December 2024) + a smartphone/tablet to run it 9. Several members bought units via Ozon links shared in-chat around that date 10. - Thinkdiag 2: an alternative first-generation scanner that uses the same server/database backend as Diagzone-flashed Mucar units, so it should support the same vehicle list 11. - Autel TS508: a separate, dedicated tool — not a general OBD scanner — used specifically for TPMS (tire pressure sensor) programming/cloning 12. - ELM327 Bluetooth Mini v1.5 (Ozon product 929042666): a cheap generic Bluetooth OBD2 adapter confirmed to pair with the X80 without issues 13. Recommended free companion apps: Car Scanner or ScanMaster 14. Limitation: this class of adapter reads/clears only engine (ECU) codes over the OBD2 protocol — it cannot read mileage, airbag status, or other body-control-module data 15. The same class of generic ELM327 adapter was also independently confirmed to work for reading Opel and Škoda vehicles, not just FAW — i.e. it isn't FAW-specific hardware 16.

    OBD-II port location. Under the steering wheel, slightly to the right of center, pointing downward. No trim/plastic needs to be removed to access it 1718.

    ELM327 adapters — a separate cheap tier from Diagzone/Mucar. Distinct from the Diagzone-ecosystem hardware above, plain ELM327 clones are the budget default many owners simply keep in the glovebox: a "ELM327 OBD2 WI-FI v1.5, chip PIC18F25K80" unit sold on Ozon (https://ozon.ru/t/ynzRtEQ) pairs with the free Car Scanner Android app 1920. Reliability is inconsistent — quality control on ELM327 clones is "luck of the draw": one owner's unit connected intermittently and dropped every 5 minutes and was called unusable, while another reported the same class of adapter working without issues since 2015; a third bought a v2.1 model hoping for better luck 212223. Cost is low enough (~900 RUB (RU market, March 2024)) that replacing a bad unit isn't a big loss 22. - Car Scanner ELM OBD2 (Google Play: com.ovz.carscanner) is the community's preferred free app over the alternative OBD Mari, per an owner who tried both 2425. It displays lambda-sensor readings, engine load %, intake-manifold pressure, long- and short-term fuel-trim correction, coolant temperature and idle RPM, in addition to reading/clearing engine codes 2526. - Hard limit, confirmed by two different owners independently: a plain ELM327 can read/clear engine (ECU) codes only — it cannot read the airbag module, the AKPP (AISIN automatic transmission) module, or other body-control-module data, matching the limitation already noted for the ELM327 Bluetooth Mini above 2728. No Android app exists that surfaces X80-specific real-time parameters beyond the generic OBD2 PIDs — asked directly in-chat, the answer was a flat "no" 2930. - Battery-drain false codes. A flat/weak battery can trigger a wave of simultaneous fault codes (including on the AKPP) that clear on their own once the battery is reconnected/recharged. One owner had an airbag warning come on, took the car to a shop, was shown a long list of fault codes, and paid 1,500 RUB (RU market, February 2026) for a "diagnosis" that turned out to be exactly this — a fully discharged battery, not a real fault; afterwards they bought a cheap ELM327 adapter off AliExpress to check codes themselves in future and haven't seen any errors on it since 3132. A second, independent report corroborates the pattern: another owner's AKPP threw a low-voltage fault code after their battery went flat, and it likewise cleared once the battery issue was resolved 33. - Erasing (not just reading) codes may need a "dual-board" ELM327. One owner who bought an extended-chip ELM327 (paired with PC/phone software) a few years earlier noted that clearing/erasing fault codes needs a "2-плата" (two-PCB) version of the adapter — the listing has to say this explicitly; a plain single-board unit reportedly can read but not reliably erase 34. - FCAR CW700 — a separate professional-tier OBD2 scanner marketed for Chinese brands (Chery, Haval, Geely, Changan) with full-system code reading and free updates; suggested as an alternative when an ELM327 isn't enough, sold on Ozon (https://ozon.ru/t/b2ThFxa) 35.

    TPMS (tire pressure sensors). - Sensor batteries are normally sealed inside the sensor housing and not meant to be user-replaceable, though a video exists showing a practical workaround for opening a sensor and replacing its battery 36. - A Moscow-based Avito service listing offers professional TPMS sensor programming, cloning, and adaptation to the vehicle — relevant for owners who buy generic/used replacement sensors and need them matched to the car 37. - A RuTube video walks through TPMS sensor installation and programming for the X80, including adapting universal (non-OEM) sensors 3839.
