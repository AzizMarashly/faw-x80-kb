    Head units, multimedia, and firmware resources

    Tags: #head-unit · #firmware

    Prices and suppliers on this page are Russian-market (RUB, at the dates shown). Verify locally before relying on them in Syria.

    Stock (Baidu) head unit — Android Auto workaround. The factory Baidu multimedia unit doesn't run Android Auto natively, and Carlinkit dongles do not work with it 1. A wireless adapter sold on AliExpress (~3,800 RUB (RU market, April 2024), listed as "FAW X80 X40 T77 T33 WIZCAR A1 multimedia block adapter") adds wireless Android Auto to the stock unit without replacing the stereo or flashing firmware, and works with both iPhone and Android 23.

    CarlinKit wireless adapter options and pricing (as of 2026-03-04). For owners running an aftermarket Android head unit (where wired Carlinkit dongles work, unlike on the stock Baidu unit above), three CarlinKit tiers were compared in-chat 4567: 1. A premium unit sold on OZON is CarPlay-only with higher specs but is expensive — likely 20,000+ RUB (RU market, March 2026) once Russian VAT/import markup is added 5. 2. CarlinKit Ultra2 (Android 15, SM6350 chipset, 8GB+128GB) on AliExpress at 11,128 RUB (RU market, March 2026) with a -53% discount applied 6. 3. CarlinKit X1CA/X2CA, a budget wireless CarPlay/Android Auto adapter supporting the Baidu Carlife protocol (relevant for better compatibility with Chinese-platform head units), on AliExpress at 3,659 RUB (RU market, March 2026) with a -48% discount — one owner confirmed running this exact model, noting the same unit runs ~20,000 RUB (RU market, March 2026) on Yandex Market once VAT/import markup is applied 7. Separately noted: Android Auto video playback is limited to "parked vehicle" mode unless using an app like CarStream or Fermata Auto to unlock in-motion video.

    Firmware and technical documentation. - fawforum.ru — a broad community-run resource with head-unit/camera firmware, patches, activation codes for OEM electronics, wiring/electrical schematics, tuning guides, and modification write-ups 8. - A Yandex.Disk archive ("FAW_X80_UPD") shared in-chat contains head-unit firmware files (including a firmware for the 4-camera system) and TPMS diagnostic data 910. - 4PDA forum (4pda.to) has an extensive collection of aftermarket radio firmware, update procedures, and CAN-bus adapter info for radios compatible with the X80 — useful when the factory unit is swapped for an aftermarket Android head unit 11.

    OEM-shape replacement head units. - Canbox H-Line 5612-1083, sold on OZON, is an Android-10 unit shaped for the pre-facelift X80 (2013–2018): 4G/SIM, 4GB/64GB RAM/storage, DSP audio, QLED display, "Tesla-style" interface (as of 2025-01-04) 12. Note: the China-market X80 "Sport" trim (1.8L engine) instead ships with a factory Tesla-style unit with full climate-control integration on-screen, no Android, and a proprietary Chinese mapping app — not comparable to this aftermarket product 12. - Teyes head units are widely used and well regarded in the community for the 2018–2022 (facelift, "CC3L" variant) X80: 4GB/64GB RAM/storage, 8-core Android 10, IPS screen, DSP, EN7388 amplifier chip, 4G+Wi-Fi, 2-DIN, bundled CAN-bus adapter and steering-wheel-control frame 1314. One owner who also ran a Teyes unit on a Duster called it reliable 14; a second owner confirmed it installs and works well, with camera and parking-sensor integration functioning correctly — but reported an intermittent, device-specific issue where the head unit fails to pair with an OBD2 Bluetooth adapter (while the same adapter pairs fine with a phone); no confirmed workaround 15. A community member (Валерий) who bought a Teyes unit points to the seller's Avito profile ("Teyes/AutoUnit") for automotive electronics and installation 16. - Generic Android head units + CAN-bus adapters are notably cheaper on AliExpress than Russian retailers (Wildberries/OZON): roughly 1,200–1,500₽ on Ali vs. 4,000–8,000₽ in Russia for comparable frame+CAN-bus kits — trade- off is 2–3 weeks shipping from China vs. 1–2 days domestically. Frame and CAN-bus adapter can be bought separately from different vendors if needed 17.

    Dashcam/multimedia fitment guide. frontcam.ru hosts a dashcam installation guide specific to the X80 2018–2020, and is also referenced generally for multimedia unit compatibility/installation research on the X80 1819.
