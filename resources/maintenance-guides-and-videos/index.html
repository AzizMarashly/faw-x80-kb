    Maintenance guides, videos, and reference articles

    Tags: #reference · #automatic-transmission

    Prices and suppliers on this page are Russian-market (RUB, at the dates shown). Verify locally before relying on them in Syria.

    Some advice here concerns sub-zero winters and is less relevant in a hot climate.

    A collection of external videos and articles shared in-chat as references for specific maintenance jobs.

    • Serpentine belt squealing — diagnosis and fix. Article/guide covering checking belt tension, inspecting condition, and distinguishing temporary fixes (e.g. belt dressing/silicone spray — works, but only temporarily) from a proper permanent fix (replacement) 1.
    • Air filter brand comparison video. Compares multiple aftermarket air filter brands usable on the X80/similar platforms: Mann, Filtron, Marshall, Fortech, Finwhale, Kortex, Miles 2.
    • Radiator fan removal / intake manifold work — video guide. Answers a member's question about how much disassembly is needed to remove the radiator fan; video also covers thermostat replacement and intake manifold work as related context 34.
    • Rear longitudinal-arm bushing replacement — video guide 5.
    • Door panel soundproofing (shumoizolyatsiya), part 1 — Drive2 article by a community member, covering door-panel sound deadening 6.
    • Motor oil authenticity — "oil scammer exposure" article. Drive2 article explaining how to identify counterfeit motor oil and naming manufacturers implicated in counterfeiting 7.
    • Antifreeze G13 — specification and compatibility article. Covers G13 antifreeze specs, its environmental profile, and what it can/cannot be safely mixed with 8.
    • AKPP (automatic transmission) reference. Drive2 article with detail on the X80's automatic transmission: fluid capacity, part numbering, filter specs, and maintenance procedure 9. A second, dedicated Drive2 write-up ("AKPP X80, part 2") by the same kind of owner-documentation covers the fluid change procedure itself with filler/drain locations 10. A separate site, MirSmazok.ru, covers the Aisin 09G (09G-TripTronic) transmission specifically — fluid spec and recommended change interval — and discusses the gap between conservative manufacturer intervals and real-world owner practice, citing Wikipedia and other fluid-spec databases as cross-references 11. Kixx ATF Multi (product page: kixxlube.ru/products/34) was shared as a fluid confirmed to meet the JWS 3309 specification needed for FAW automatic-transmission applications 12. Separately, a Pravda.ru article argues for using AKPP "Sport"/S mode in stop-and-go traffic, on the reasoning that it reduces wear from constant shifting — shared as a discussion point, not a verified recommendation (low confidence) 13.
    • Sunroof lubricant guidance — Drive2 article. Covers proper lubricants for sunroof mechanisms, alongside related transmission-fluid guidance; shared as reference for sunroof maintenance 14.
    • Windshield/cowl drain cleaning. An AliExpress brush tool for clearing windshield drains/gutters to prevent water leaking into the cabin during rain or winter, ~110 RUB (RU market, February 2025) 15. Related: a YouTube Shorts video on avoiding electrical water damage in modern vehicles with sealed electronics, relevant to the X80's known water-leak issues 16.
    • Engine insulation blanket for extreme cold. "Автотепло" brand blanket from mirdopov.ru, recommended for Arctic/far-north conditions to improve warm-up time and heat retention 1718.
    • Tire/wheel sizing calculator. mosautoshina.ru/calc/ — lets you compare different tire/wheel size combinations and profiles and see the resulting change in vehicle ride height 19.
    • Driver's door trim removal video. Yandex Video-hosted (source: RuTube) tutorial demonstrating removal and reinstallation of the driver's door trim panel, including correct clip-removal technique to avoid breaking fasteners 20.
    • Engine-bay water leak (monoblock) — diagnostic video. YouTube Short showing a water leak at the monoblock/firewall area on a weathered X80, used in-chat to illustrate what this known leak point looks like 21. Related to the windshield/cowl drain cleaning entry above, which addresses a similar water-intrusion path.
    • Dashboard warning messages — RuTube video. "Essential warning messages for all FAW BESTURN X80 owners" — walks through the vehicle's warning lights/messages (service reminder, TPMS fault, turn-signal bulb fault, low battery voltage, overspeed, prolonged-driving alert) with timestamps for each 22.
    • Fuel filter replacement — YouTube video. Step-by-step walkthrough ("How to change the FUEL filter on FAW X80"): accessing the filter cover, removing the filter, pump disassembly, correct reinstallation, and post-replacement startup 2324.
    • DIY radiator cleaning tool — T-shaped tube design (low confidence). A member's suggested approach for cleaning the radiator without removing it: inject water via a T-shaped tube entering from the middle, perpendicular to the fins; since the radiator fins are mounted vertically, a horizontal tube in this orientation causes less fin damage than a single flat-tube pass 25.
    • Oil/air/cabin filter part numbers — pinned group message. The exact part numbers for the engine oil filter, air filter, and cabin filter are maintained in a pinned message at the top of the Telegram group chat rather than a single external site — check pinned messages first before asking 2627.
    • Fuel-standards article (Auto.ru, low confidence, general context, not X80-specific). "Fuel norms 2026: why refineries were allowed to produce Euro-3" — discusses Russian refineries being permitted to produce lower-grade Euro-3 fuel instead of Euro-5, attributed to refinery damage and capacity constraints; shared as background on fuel-quality risk rather than a car-specific fact 28.
    • Fuse and relay diagram database. vsepredohraniteli.ru hosts a comprehensive, X80-specific fuse/relay location reference with schematic diagrams, descriptions, and photos of block locations (e.g. headlight fuses, instrument-cluster fuses, cigarette-lighter fuse) — useful for electrical troubleshooting 29. Independently re-shared and endorsed again about a year later; the poster noted the site is thorough enough in Russian that non-Russian speakers shouldn't bother trying to translate it — everything needed is there 303132.
    • "Slippery road" warning light — Drive2 forum post. Community-written post detailing causes and fixes for the "скользкая дорога" (slippery-road) dashboard warning 33.
