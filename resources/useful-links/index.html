    Useful links — community sites and owner logs

    Tags: #tpms

    Prices and suppliers on this page are Russian-market (RUB, at the dates shown). Verify locally before relying on them in Syria.

    Community knowledge base. A community member built a free, ad-free X80 knowledge base in Russian at fawx80.car.atjed.com/ru from this group and the sister group "FAW BESTURN X80". It covers repairs, part numbers and cross-references, prices, typical faults and maintenance, and every fact links back to its source message. The author asked for feedback, missing sources and removal requests, and warned the content is machine-generated, so errors are possible 1.

    Outside forums and owner logs. - Owner reviews and logbooks on the Drive2 X80 page (drive2.ru/experience/faw/g5413) are a useful outside source; an auto.ironhorse.ru forum thread on the FAW Besturn X80 (comment page 6) is another. The poster suggested feeding such forum content into the knowledge base 234. - A drive2.ru article (https://www.drive2.ru/l/634094129248225421/) tabulates unsprung-mass parts (неподрессоренная масса) of the Mazda 6 GG and which brands fit them. Victor notes the X80 is not identical to the GG, and that the article itself puts compatibility near 70%, not 80% 56. See Mazda 6 platform parts compatibility. - Victor's Drive2 blog, entry 14, covers the rear springs and shock absorbers; he points to the second photo and the text for the setup 7. - Victor's Drive2 blog, entry 12, describes restoring the tyre-pressure sensors (TPMS) so they work on his X80; it is a first-hand reference in Russian 8.

    TPMS services on Avito. Che Guevara posted two Avito listings as examples of local offers: a TPMS sensor cloning service in Aleksin (клонирование датчиков давления) and a Tula seller of universal TPMS sensors for any car 910. See TPMS sensor cloning.

    Dismantler. RazborNF, a dismantler with an Avito page, lists original FAW X80 parts; search "faw x80" on its page to see the stock 11. Victor clarifies that these listings (including drive shafts) come from automatic transmission (AKPP / АКПП) cars, so check the gearbox type before buying 12.

    Parts shops named in the chat. Members named kitzapchasti.ru (catalogue with photo-based listings), chinaspare.ru (Chinese car parts, Trialli belts) and frontcam.ru (a catalogue for the Besturn X80 2018-2020); one message adds that the Vostochny Express shop had a fender at a discount and that Kitparts shows prices. Details in parts suppliers directory 131415.

    Shop and importer sites on shared files. The shared order exports come from hapbryansk.ru and the ELM327 manual is published by ORION (orionspb.ru); see Shared files and manuals 161718.
