    Workshop quality: chains vs. private mechanics

    Prices and suppliers on this page are Russian-market (RUB, at the dates shown). Verify locally before relying on them in Syria.

    A community thread comparing named repair shops in the Moscow area, sparked by a member about to visit FIT before a suspension job. General takeaway: large chain service centers tend to oversell work, while independent/private mechanics are cheaper and often better, but hard to find.

    • Remtech (ЮВАО district, Moscow) — not recommended 1.
    • FIT — the most-discussed and most-criticized chain in this thread. Repeated complaints of overselling and inflated quotes: one member was quoted about 100,000 RUB (RU market, August 2026) to rebuild the entire rear suspension for a problem that a different (unnamed) shop fixed properly for roughly 5,000 RUB (RU market, August 2026) 2345. Another member was told the whole suspension needed replacing at a cost of 100,000+ RUB (RU market, August 2026), and when they asked what happens if that doesn't fix the issue, the mechanic's answer was only "we'll see" 678. FIT reportedly won't let customers supply their own parts unless ordered through the shop — and stock is often unavailable 9. A separate member (Victor) reports FIT billed a friend for repairs the friend had already had done elsewhere two months earlier, after a diagnostic visit 10. FIT also failed to set wheel alignment correctly for one member, unlike a shop in their home city 11. One member noted this behavior isn't unique to FIT — "basically every shop does this, just to charge more" 12 — though another pointed out shops may vary by city/region 13. Even going through personal connections at a shop, one member was still quoted a large round sum 14.
    • Автопилот (Autopilot) — reported as usable but expensive; notably it does allow customers to bring their own parts, unlike FIT 115.
    • Private/independent mechanics ("частники"). Consistently described as 3-5x cheaper than chain shops, often with better quality, but rare and hard to find — mainly locatable through friends/word of mouth rather than ads, and the good ones have waiting lists of 1-2 weeks 1617. One member's concrete comparison: a full upper front control-arm replacement cost 5,000 RUB (RU market, August 2026) at a large chain shop vs. an estimated 1,500 RUB (RU market, August 2026) from their usual private mechanic — they used the chain shop only because they needed the work done urgently and didn't want to wait out the private shop's queue 18. Another member who moved to the FAW never managed to find a trustworthy private option after their previous go-to shop closed 192021. A member from Saransk separately vouches for a good mechanic there, without naming the shop 22.
    • Bottom line from the thread: no single honest/cheap chain shop was ever actually named in response to a direct request for one 23 — the community's practical answer is "find a private mechanic through someone you know," not "go to a specific chain."
