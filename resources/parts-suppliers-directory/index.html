    Parts suppliers and catalogs directory (Russia)

    Tags: #part-number · #catalog

    Prices and suppliers on this page are Russian-market (RUB, at the dates shown). Verify locally before relying on them in Syria.

    Community-recommended suppliers and catalogs for sourcing FAW X80 parts in Russia, gathered from repeated recommendations over the chat's history. Reliability/trust levels below reflect what posters said at the time — always re-verify before ordering, since sites and stock come and go (one 2025-07-06 report found a previously-cited site no longer maintained 1).

    Specialist FAW/Chinese-car parts retailers (most frequently recommended): - kitzapchasti.ru (also mirrored per-city, e.g. irkutsk.kitzapchasti.ru, cherkessk...) — general FAW X80 parts catalog, incl. suspension/steering section; stocked brake disc 5DA033251 at 3,140 RUB (RU market, April 2025) and caliper repair kits B5CA0339KIT/B5CA0339NAP in one supplier check 2345. Also stocks steering-rack dust boot (пыльник рулевой рейки), part 5AA032122, at 530 RUB (RU market, June 2025) with delivery to Naberezhnye Chelny 6. One member found some parts showed as available but were actually out of stock 7. Also stocks a complete GRM (timing chain) replacement kit, part 1026040-37KIT, with full assembly (chain, gears, tensioners, seals) 8. A front-suppport repair kit for the related B50 model (seals + piston, FC013501020) was also pointed to on this site when a member asked whether X80/B50 parts overlap 9. Independently confirmed multiple times over the chat's history as a first-stop catalog link 1011. - kitparts.ru — FAW Besturn X80 and X80F (facelift) catalog by section (bumpers, fenders, hoods, headlights, air filter housing, fuse blocks, hood hinges, door seals/window regulators, etc.); allows pre-orders with ~4-day wait 121314. Multiple members pointed newcomers here as the default first stop 1516. - kitzap.ru — sister/related FAW parts catalog (rear bumper trim, heater resistor/rheostat, hood — e.g. hood/bonnet for the FAW Bestune X80 NEW, part 5CA152310-#M3 — etc.) 1718192010. Disagreement: one member later cautioned against this site (referred to in-chat as "Китзап"/Kitzap), saying many parts listed as compatible do not actually fit despite the site claiming they do, quality on some items is inconsistent, and shipping deadlines are often missed — recommending kitparts.ru or other sellers instead 21. This is the opposite of the generally positive mentions above, so treat compatibility claims on this site with extra caution and verify before ordering. Also trades under the KITZAP brand name with its own Avito storefront (avito.ru/brands/kitzap) 22; the vendor has directly shared its own FAW Besturn X80 category page (kitzap.ru/spares/faw/faw_besturn_x80/) with members in-chat 23. - autocraft-kzn.ru (Kazan-based) — sells parts unpackaged (no OEM packaging), which keeps prices low; one member called it "a good shop, no issues so far"; front suspension/drivetrain parts confirmed sourced from here 2425. - chinaspare.ru and ooove.ru — Chinese-vehicle parts wholesalers; chinaspare recommended by a member who sources both parts and body panels there 262710. ooove.ru specifically was used by one member to cross-check a right-side engine mount (опора двигателя): the seller confirmed the FAW X80 and FAW Besturn B50 right engine mounts are identical parts despite being priced differently in ooove's own catalog (X80 catalog page vs. B50 catalog page) — a concrete example of why it's worth checking the B50 catalog for X80-compatible parts 28. - HemenPro (OZON marketplace store) — extensive FAW X80 plastic trim (moldings, grilles) primarily for pre-restyle models 29. - KitZapchasti (Irkutsk mirror) — brake disc pricing/availability specifically 30.

    Aggregators / price comparison: - zzap.ru — parts aggregator; search by part number shows prices across multiple stores and regions 31. - emex.ru — an online parts database where entering the needed part brings up all compatible cross-referenced alternatives with prices; useful for finding fitments from other brands (Mazda GG6, early-2000s Ford Focus, etc. commonly turn up as cheaper analogs) — one member noted ordering through emex directly, versus Ozon where the same analog parts can be a bit pricier but returns are easier and delivery faster 32. Example product page: front shock absorber, part 5CA034700 (emex.ru/products/5CA034700/faw/40358) 33. - DRIVE2.ru parts database (drive2.ru/parts) — cross-referenced part numbers and alternatives, e.g. rear shock absorber reference for part 5CA028700A; useful for finding equivalents and comparing prices 34. - Drive2.ru "Part codes for FAW Besturn X80 2.0" article (drive2.ru/l/504151646054384568) — a frequently-reshared FAQ-style post listing OEM part codes (e.g. rear hatch struts, extended hood struts); shared independently at least twice, including once pinned as a standard answer to "where do I find part numbers" 3536. - Drive2.ru community write-ups (blog posts, not the parts DB) — owner guides covering trunk water drainage, fuel filter replacement, thermostat work, LED lighting upgrades, engine mount replacement, and emissions-system mods, each with photos and step-by-step instructions 37. One member also pointed newcomers generally to Drive2 as the best-documented community knowledge base for the X80, noting owner guides there go back to 2018-model-year cars — though a second member cautioned the forum also contains some incorrect information, so cross-check claims 3839. - Telegraph-hosted catalog mirrors — a community-maintained, section-by- section FAW X80 parts catalog (Body / Engine & Transmission / Suspension & Chassis / Cabin / Maintenance) with part-number search; open to correction 4041. - Pinned in-group PDF parts catalog. Separately from any external site, the Telegram group's own "catalog" tab has a PDF parts catalog pinned — members are encouraged to check it first before asking part-number questions in chat 42.

    Marketplace/classifieds routes: - Drom.ru classifieds — useful for sourcing used suspension arms and other components, with part-number cross-referencing 19. - Avito — individual sellers list used/new body panels and other parts regionally (see the marketplace-part-listings card for specific examples). Example: a rear spring-mount control arm for Mazda 6 GG/GY (cross- compatible), Japan-made (Mibu), listed on Avito Moscow 43. - Wildberries and Ozon as general-marketplace routes (distinct from the specialist FAW shops above) — used for hub assemblies, springs, gaskets, and other parts that don't require the specialist catalogs; Ozon in particular is favored for CV boots, filters, and oil because returns are easier even if price is sometimes slightly higher 32. - vedro.pro — a marketplace where individual sellers list parts matched to the original FAW part number; one member noted that ordering a branded "FAW" part rarely gets you a genuinely FAW-branded item regardless of source, so treated as roughly equivalent to other aftermarket routes; example item found there at 3,990 RUB (RU market, July 2026) 44. - KitZap (kitzap.ru/kitzapchasti.ru), buying experience. One member who couldn't get a local market vendor to even measure/compare CV-joint and drive-shaft sizes for him ended up ordering from KitZap instead, on the basis that "it just needs to fit and work" — while waiting on a parallel order of a complete CV joint + hubs from another seller (Василий) 45. Another regular seller of the same parts (Arсhi 77) noted members generally test-fit parts themselves and offered to share links directly next time, saying he has swapped this type of dust boot "more than once" 4647. General guidance from this exchange: Chinese-import routes (chinaspare, KitZap, etc.) run roughly 10–30% cheaper than OEM/branded parts but typically add 500+ RUB (RU market, July 2026) shipping, and returns can incur additional fees — factor total cost, not just sticker price 4546.

    Dealer/group-buy and community-vetted individual sellers: - Mastrgtr / mastergtr75 (VK group vk.com/mastrgtr, Telegram @mastergtr75) — official FAW dealer based in Chita; sells original parts with nationwide courier delivery; multiple members report successful purchases and recommend contacting the group directly with questions 484950. One member cautioned that some pushback on this seller came from people invested in selling Mazda-sourced "alternative" parts instead, suggesting bias in that criticism 51. - VK group vk.com/zapchasti_bez_natsenki ("parts without markup") — raised in-chat as a possible source; a member noted the group had posted specifically about the X80, but no first-hand purchase confirmation is on record yet 5.

    Adjacent specialist retailers (single item, still useful as a channel): - Autochehly.ru — aftermarket plastic wheel-arch trim (podkrylki) from 1,150 RUB (RU market, December 2024), an alternative to OEM 52. - AutoMarket116 — multi-platform seller (OZON, Yandex Market, AliExpress) for window deflectors, radio frames, and general parts, with occasional discounts 53. - delot.ru — general tool retailer; useful for individual open-end wrenches (17–19mm sizes needed for suspension work) rather than buying a full wrench set 54. - Ashot-X80 — not a shop but an individual community member who makes custom-designed radiator-grille emblems and trunk badges for owners wanting non-OEM styling; contacted via the Telegram group directly for design/ordering 55. - izap24.ru — used/OEM-parts marketplace that ships Europe-sourced auto parts across Russia; referenced in-chat for a used catalytic converter for the X80 (2019), listed at 35,360 RUB (RU market, October 2025) 56. - nakuzov.ru — tow-hook (farkop) specialist retailer; stocks the Sheriff 3951.12 farkop specifically fitted for the FAW Besturn X80, with installation service and delivery/pickup options in Moscow 57.

    General buying-route guidance. - Members repeatedly emphasize buying locally/in-region first when a local supplier stocks the part, to avoid long delivery waits from internet retailers — with kitzapchasti.ru, kitzap.ru, and ooove.ru as the fallback online catalogs when nothing local is available 1158. - Most parts for the X80, original or aftermarket, ultimately come from China; only smaller consumables (filters etc.) are commonly sourced from other manufacturers. Mazda 6 (2002–2008)-compatible parts are another common cross-reference route, though specific manufacturers for those weren't identified in this exchange 58. - Community forums and groups (general, not a shop). Beyond the parts catalogs above, owners point newcomers to: fawforum.ru (general discussion forum), the Drive2.ru FAW Besturn X80 section (drive2.ru/r/faw/besturn_x80/503628553397469808/, individual owner write-ups and technical discussion), and regional WhatsApp/Telegram owner groups — with this Telegram group cited as the most active one for the model 596061.
