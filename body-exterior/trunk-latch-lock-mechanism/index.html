    Trunk latch/lock — diagnosis and replacement

    Tags: #trunk · #part-number · #alarm

    Prices and suppliers on this page are Russian-market (RUB, at the dates shown). Verify locally before relying on them in Syria.

    Some advice here concerns sub-zero winters and is less relevant in a hot climate.

    Symptom — trunk won't lock when the alarm is armed, but opens fine by button while armed: first try wiggling the latch's small mechanical "flag" lever — sometimes it just needs to be moved to seat correctly. If that doesn't fix it, the latch needs replacing. A physical knock on the left side of the latch was reported to "temporarily" force it closed as a workaround, but is not a fix 12345. As a temporary measure until parts arrive, some owners tie the trunk lid closed with a strap to stop it popping open over bumps 6. Wiring corrosion from cabin/trunk moisture can also cause this fault — check and clean the contacts under the driver's seat, since one owner's identical symptom turned out to be a dirty connector in that harness, not the latch itself 7.

    Replacement part source: a direct replacement trunk latch (замок двери задка) is available at kitzap.ru, plus other Russian parts suppliers 8. On the restyle-2020 trunk latch specifically, one owner reported difficulty getting the part through official dealers after two units failed; a Lada Granta trunk latch was used as a compatible substitute, though the Granta part lacks the electric actuator, so remote/button release from the alarm does not work with it — manual key/mechanical release only 910.

    Trunk-button (electric opener) troubleshooting sequence: if the trunk's electric-open button (электрический открыватель) stops working: (1) test the button with a multimeter for continuity — press the button and check for continuity/tone; if it passes, the button itself is fine; (2) check the connector (фишка) for oxidation/corrosion and clean it with fine abrasive (sandpaper) if needed; (3) if the connector is corroded, try bridging it with a jumper wire to see if the lock mechanism responds; (4) inspect the wiring inside the trunk's corrugated conduit (гофра) for breaks, especially where the conduit enters the trunk opening; (5) check all relevant fuses for continuity. If all of the above check out, replace the connector or wiring harness. Note: the factory lock mechanism has built-in travel limits and fuse protection 11121314151617181920212223242526272829303132333435363738.

    Trunk-button alternatives and common failure point: two additional data points on trunk-button reliability: (1) a Lada (ВАЗ) 2110 trunk-open button works as a mechanical/budget alternative if the OEM button fails outright; (2) the connector-with-wire (фишка с проводом) is a frequent failure point on the X80's trunk-button circuit specifically — consistent with the connector-corrosion troubleshooting step above. OEM trunk buttons are available from dealers (one community member keeps stock in his shop); the Lada alternative is positioned as a budget/emergency option rather than a long-term fix 3940414243444546.

    Cleaning oxidized trunk-button contacts — often a permanent fix: an intermittent trunk lock button (inconsistent open/close) was resolved by carefully disassembling the button, cleaning the green oxidized contacts with electrical contact cleaner and contact conditioner — contacts returned to normal operation. This avoided needing to replace the entire assembly and can be a permanent fix, not just a temporary one. If replacement does become necessary, a complete handle+button unit is available on AliExpress for ~3,000 RUB (RU market, August 2025)

    47484950.

    Trunk lock repair via full contact cleaning (earlier, corroborating report): another owner independently reports the same permanent-fix approach: when the trunk lock becomes inoperable/unreliable, disassembly and cleaning restores function — (1) remove the trim panel; (2) disconnect all connectors on the lock assembly; (3) remove the mounting hardware; (4) clean all electrical contacts; (5) reassemble and reconnect. After cleaning, the lock becomes reliable again, though it may still occasionally need multiple attempts before consistent operation returns. Intermittent trunk-lock failure is a recurring symptom that this cleaning procedure resolves 515253.

    Trunk door sensor shows "open" when the door is actually closed: separate fault from the latch/button issues above — the trunk-door sensor (distinct from the latch mechanism itself, which works fine) reports the door as open on the dash even when it's closed. Diagnostic steps used in the community: (1) close the latch while the trunk is open and check whether the sensor responds; (2) try mechanically forcing/snapping the sensor closed with a flat tool (e.g. a screwdriver) to see if the reading changes; (3) check whether the sensor is normally-closed or normally-open with the door shut, since this affects how to interpret its readings; (4) inspect for a loosened sensor body behind the trim, or wire breakage inside the harness 545556575859606162.

    Trunk door sensor diagnostic — isolate sensor vs. wiring/ECU by unplugging it: to further isolate the fault, remove the latch and unplug the sensor's connector completely. If the dashboard still shows "open" with the connector unplugged, the fault is in the wiring harness or ECU logic, not the sensor itself; if the indicator changes state when the connector is unplugged, the sensor or latch mechanism itself is at fault 636465.

    Trunk door lock — part number 5CA162310A, Granta cross-compatibility: FAW Besturn X80 rear/trunk door lock part number is 5CA162310A, available at kitzap.ru. Cross-compatibility confirmed with the Lada Granta rear door lock, though it requires modification to fit (see below) 66. The same lock is listed for the Bestune X80 by a parts shop as the tailgate (задка) door lock in October 2025 67.

    Granta door lock modification for X80 fitment: a Lada Granta rear door lock fits the X80 mechanically but needs adaptation: (1) file down the lock's "dog"/locking pin (using a file/rasp — "напильник") so it doesn't interfere with surrounding parts; (2) install two M6 bolts as spacers to achieve proper alignment and prevent the lock rattling against the plastic trim. During installation you'll notice the "собачка" (lock pawl) interferes with fitment — that's expected and is what the filing step addresses 68697071.

    Electric trunk lock total failure — wiring diagnostic and alternate Granta part number: separately, a complete electric trunk-lock failure (no clicking sound, hazard-light indicator blinks, neither the button nor the remote works — only the manual release lever functions) was traced to a possible broken wire inside the rubber hose/grommet routing the harness to the trunk; check that wiring first. A Lada Granta trunk latch assembly, part 21900560601010 (~2,000 RUB, RU market, January 2026), was used as a working replacement — consistent with the Granta cross-compatibility noted above, though under a different catalog number. Installation notes: the electrical connector pins are fragile and bend easily when mating the connector, and the plastic lock lever/pawl may need filing to fit the X80 opening 72737475767778798081.

    Trunk-open button replacement — correct sequence to avoid a fiddly connector: two independent reports on replacing the electric trunk-open button (part sold e.g. via mastergtr.ru, link: https://mastergtr.ru/faw/faw-besturn-x80f/knopka-otkry-vaniya-bagazhnika-dlya-faw-besturn-x80-new). The correct sequence to avoid fighting with the small connector inside the confined door cavity: (1) remove the old button first; (2) pull the connector out through the hole in the trunk door so it's accessible outside the door; (3) snap/connect the new button to the connector while it's outside the door; (4) only then insert the connected button assembly into the door hole. Doing it in the wrong order (trying to mate the connector while it's still inside the door, then fishing the button back out to reconnect it from outside) wastes about an hour of fiddly, frustrating effort 828384.

    Trunk sensor false "open" signal specifically in freezing temperatures: a related but distinct symptom from the general "shows open when closed" sensor fault above: in sub-zero freezing weather, the trunk-lid sensor shows the door as open on the dashboard (with lights staying on) despite the lid being physically closed. This variant specifically affects the limit-switch (концевик) contact and is tied to cold temperatures. Solutions reported: (1) adjust the sensor's mounting bracket with a hammer/bolts underneath, using a star wrench to reposition it; (2) test by disconnecting the sensor wires and shorting the contacts — if the warning clears, the issue is the sensor; (3) apply thick 3M tape to the contact point on the door (one owner reports this worked for 2 years before the tape thickness wore down); (4) contact cleaning may help; (5) replace just the microswitch, not the full lock assembly. Warm weather temporarily resolves the symptom on its own 85868788899091929394. ⚠️ Conflict/note: multiple solutions are proposed (bracket adjustment, tape, microswitch replacement); thermal expansion/contraction of the sensor mechanism is the likely common root cause across reports.

    Collision-damage frame deformation blocking the lock — repair options and cost: after a collision impact to the trunk/rear area, even slight frame deformation can prevent the door lock from seating and closing properly (lock internals are visible under the door's interior trim, requiring trim removal to inspect). Solutions: (1) straighten the frame with a spot welder plus paint — roughly 37,000 RUB (RU market, January 2026); (2) adjust or disconnect the lock sensor as a temporary workaround. Professional service is recommended; a full door replacement is expensive (a new door sourced from China runs roughly 59,000 RUB plus labor; RU market, January 2026). Separately, on door sealing: if the rib-stiffening structure is damaged, replacement is preferred over straightening to avoid future rust, since zinc plating on X80 doors is insufficient protection on its own 9596979899100101102103104105106107108109110111112113114115116117118119120121122123124125126127128129130131132133134135136137138139140141142143144145.

    Trunk latch button-area corrosion — cosmetic, not critical: separate from the functional lock issues above, the trunk latch button area commonly shows rust spots in the bottom corner where the button is located, reported on many X80 examples. This is a cosmetic issue (does not affect latch function) — treatable by covering with stickers or touch-up painting. It's regarded as a factory-quality issue seen widely across the model, considered acceptable/expected for a Chinese-market vehicle by the community 146147148149150151.
