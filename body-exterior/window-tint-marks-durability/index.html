    Autopilot window marks — long-term durability

    Tags: #window · #low-confidence

    "Autopilot" brand window marks (likely an electrochromic or UV-reactive window tint/marking product) installed in 2018 showed minimal visible wear by 2024, with only small cracks developing on the front glass. The rear glass remained in pristine condition. The materials appear durable, though the owner was uncertain whether a protective treatment would help extend their life further, and no specific maintenance method was identified 1234.
