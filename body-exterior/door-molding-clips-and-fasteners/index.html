    Door and wheel-arch molding clips

    Tags: #molding · #clips · #part-number

    Prices and suppliers on this page are Russian-market (RUB, at the dates shown). Verify locally before relying on them in Syria.

    General clip replacement procedure: door molding ("накладка") clips are replaceable without full door-card removal. Procedure: (1) carefully pry/remove the molding — some adhesion from double-sided tape; (2) replace the damaged clips; (3) reattach the molding with new double-sided tape (2-sided foam tape commonly used). Reference: Drive2.ru user "k404k" documented the full procedure with the dual-tape method. This prevents plastic trim breakage 1234.

    Fastening details and part numbers: front door molding uses both double-sided tape and mechanical clips for fastening. Lifan door molding fits the X80 with minor adjustment. FAW part number FC016888502 covers the molding clips. Generic WRT-type clips from Wildberries or Opel-sourced clips can substitute, though Opel compatibility is unclear. Wheel-arch molding uses 4 unique proprietary clips that are difficult to source 567891011121314.

    Lada Priora door-panel clips (SKU confirmed): door-panel retaining clips sold on Wildberries under SKU 163460312, originally listed for Lada Priora, fit the X80's door panels. These are prone to breakage during removal — buy spares before starting a job. Anti-squeak tape is recommended alongside installation; rubber-lined clip variants are also available 15.

    Sourcing hard-to-find arch molding clips — auto paint suppliers: arch molding trim clips (крепления молдинга арок) commonly break on the X80. When exact OEM clips can't be found on the usual online marketplaces, local auto paint suppliers (малярка) — shops that stock body-repair consumables — often carry various generic clip types that can substitute 16171819.

    Cargo (rear/trunk) door trim clip compatibility: the cargo-door (rear/trunk) trim piece (накладка двери багажника) uses clips that can wear or break. A Nissan part 909901KA0B (clips, pack of 10) was suggested as an alternative, but several owners report these do not hold well on the X80. Trim pieces with clips already attached are available on Chinese parts sites for around 2,700 RUB (RU market, October 2025), but fitment varies. Note that the original clips are part of a complete trim assembly that includes the clips pre-mounted — they are not sold separately from the factory trim 2021222324.

    Door window top-edge gasket (уплотнитель) — shrinkage and replacement: door window top-edge gaskets (уплотнители на двери, where the glass enters at the top) shrink over time, causing rattling in the mirrors and possibly glass rattle. Replacement gaskets are available; price approximately 1,200 RUB (RU market, December 2025) for a single door gasket (part example: art. 68135, front-left) via automotive-dismantler/marketplace sites like drom.ru. Only front and rear gasket variants may be readily available — front-left and front-right gaskets differ and are not interchangeable 25262728293031. ⚠️ Conflict/note: front-left and front-right door gaskets use different parts; pricing and availability may vary by position.

    Door panel trim clips — Lada/Priora cross-compatibility: Lada Priora (Приора) door-panel clips fit the X80's door cards perfectly. Available on Wildberries, part #163460312, at approximately 100 RUB (RU market, January 2026) for a 30-piece pack. Clips from a Lada Tazik/Granta are also mentioned as compatible. Single-clip replacement cost is minimal when repairing torn or broken door panels 32333435363738.

    Sill/body trim fastening clips — OEM part code: plastic clips that hold the sill trim and adjacent body panels: part code 5CA051SJ4. These are commonly broken during door and sill disassembly work and should be pre-ordered when planning interior work that involves removing those panels 3940.

    Rear door trim clips and fasteners — additional sourcing options: beyond the Lada/Priora cross-compatible clips above, plastic trim clips and metal fasteners for rear door panels specifically are available from: (1) Ozon, searchable under "Мазда клипсы обивки" ("Mazda trim clips") with multiple variants; (2) auto-parts stores that keep physical samples on hand for matching by eye 414243.

    Dorestyle door/window trim molding — clips detach, sourcing difficult, Mazda 6 cross-fit suggestion (unconfirmed): some X80 dorestyle (pre-facelift) trim moldings have an issue with their clips falling off or becoming loose over time — one owner found their molding had detached entirely. Replacement sourcing is difficult; AliExpress has some options. One suggestion from the community: check a Mazda 6 (2014 model) for a similarly-shaped molding — external shape looks similar but fitment is uncertain; the suggested approach was to purchase and test, with the option to return if it doesn't fit 4445464748.
