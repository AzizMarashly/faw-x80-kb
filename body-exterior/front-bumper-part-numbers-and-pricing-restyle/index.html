    Front bumper — part numbers and pricing (restyle)

    Tags: #bumper · #part-number · #price · #restyle

    Prices and suppliers on this page are Russian-market (RUB, at the dates shown). Verify locally before relying on them in Syria.

    The complete front bumper assembly for the restyle X80 consists of multiple components:

    part part number price (RUB; RU market, December 2025)
    Front wing/fender arch molding, right 5CA15004602 2,000
    Front bumper cap 5CA150A1102 750
    Front bumper bracket, right 5CA150E11 900
    Front bumper overlay/cover 5CA250031#E1 27,000
    Front bumper grille, right 5CA350C1102 1,650
    Front bumper splitter 5CA15003202 11,700

    Total: approximately 42,000 RUB (RU market, December 2025) for the full set.

    Note: part numbers with an E1 suffix vs. a #E1 suffix may indicate restyle vs. pre-restyle variants — some sources report part-number discrepancies between dealers, so confirm the exact variant before ordering. OEM bumper plastic quality is noted by the community as superior to competing brands such as Hyundai 1234567.

    Front bumper cap confirmed as the tow-hook plug — sourcing and DIY alternative: the "Front bumper cap" (part number 5CA150A1102, table above) is the tow-hook plug/cap (заглушка переднего бампера буксировочного крюка) — listed under the fuller OEM designation "FAW Besturn X80 NEW 5CA150A1102" and available at kitzapchasti.ru. DIY alternative if the part isn't available: borrow one from another X80 to scan/measure and 3D print a replacement for roughly 250 RUB (RU market, March 2026)

    8910.

    Alternate/pre-restyle part number: an alternate part number for the same bumper plug/cap is given as 5CA050A1102 (vs. 5CA150A1102 above — likely a pre-restyle/restyle distinction), available from Kitparts starting at 371 RUB (RU market, April 2026) — cheaper than the price quoted above, possibly reflecting a different seller or point in time 1112131415.

    Dealer quote basis and market comparison: the table above is a dealer quote for the FAW X80F from December 2025 (prepayment, 21 working days); the owner says the factory plastic is good 162. The bumper cover 5CA250031E1 was being sold on the market at about 8,500 RUB (RU market, December 2025), against 27,000 RUB from the dealer for the same cover listed as 5CA250031#E1 1718. The upper cover is also listed on Avito for the X80 NEW (2018 onward); a member says it is one of several parts needed to finish a repair, and the photo suggests only the central part of the bumper was damaged 192021.

    Left front bumper bracket 5CA150E12: the left front bumper bracket for the X80 New is listed on Avito under 5CA150E12 (the right bracket in the table above is 5CA150E11). A member noted that the listing shows the bumper bracket, not the fender bracket, and advised asking the seller for a photo before buying 22.

    Tow-hook cover listing: the front bumper tow-hook cover (заглушка) 5CA150A1102 for the X80 NEW was listed again in October 2025 23.
