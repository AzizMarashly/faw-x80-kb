    Splash guards / mud flaps — fitment

    Tags: #fitment · #restyle · #part-number

    Prices and suppliers on this page are Russian-market (RUB, at the dates shown). Verify locally before relying on them in Syria.

    Factory mud guards (подкрылки) are smooth plastic without integrated clip holes. Aftermarket splash guards (брызговики) typically mount via clips through existing holes and/or bolts. The restyle X80's mud-guard design differs from pre-restyle — splash-guard geometry and attachment points may not match between model years, so check compatibility with your generation before buying. Properly fitting X80 splash guards feature a bracket/angle at the bottom for a lower-body attachment point — guards without this bracket can leave a visible gap and lack proper attachment 12345678.

    Rear mud flaps — TOTEM plastic replacements: the original felt/cloth rear mud flaps deteriorate after about 110,000 km, curling and shedding. Plastic replacements (TOTEM brand, part number TOTEM.62.16.004) are available for the right-rear position from Kitzapchasti.ru, autochehly.ru, and mirdopov.ru — multiple retailers stock these. Once the felt guards become unusable, the plastic upgrade is practical for higher-mileage vehicles 9101112131415.

    Spare-wheel/fender-liner rubbing fix: a spare wheel ("докатка") with offset (ET 45) fits the X80 fine. The front fender liner has a protruding lip designed to cover welded seams — this lip can catch on the tire during turns, scrubbing the inner fender. Resolved by properly securing the lower fastener when reinstalling the fender liner; the rubbing occurred on the inner side of the liner near the weld-seam protector 16.

    Rear mud flaps — Autofamily part: Autofamily part NLF.62.16.E13 (polyurethane, 2-piece kit) works well as rear mud flaps on the X80, available on Yandex Market. Universal rubber mud flaps also fit but require drilling additional mounting holes beyond what's pre-drilled — one owner needed extra holes for a longer universal rubber set to seat properly 171819202122232425262728. The material should be flexible rubberized rubber, not hard plastic — one owner has run a rubberized (not plastic) set for 4 years successfully 25.

    Rear brake dust cover/splash guard — Frosch part: model Frosch NLF.62.16.E13 (also written NLF6216E13 — same catalog number family as the Autofamily rear mud flap above) fits X80 2018+ models. Flexible rubber/soft material lets it fold if struck by the tire, but a rigid curb strike while parking tightly can still damage the guard if clearance is tight. Available at online retailers and local auto-parts shops, often cheaper than ordering through Ozon 29.

    Rear wheel-arch mud flap material (factory felt vs. rust risk): OEM rear wheel-arch mud flaps are pressed felt-like material (прессованный войлок) on both the outer and inner sides. The felt saturates with water during rain and becomes soft, then dries hard again — this cycle traps moisture against the body metal underneath, contributing to rust formation over time. Aftermarket plastic alternatives are available on Yandex Market for 3,000+ RUB; it's unclear whether the aftermarket plastic fitment matches OEM exactly or needs modification 30313233343536.

    TOTEM wheel-arch liner part numbers (full set, all 4 corners): plastic wheel-arch liner covers (подкрылки) with TOTEM part numbers: TOTEM.62.16.001 (front left), TOTEM.62.16.002 (front right), TOTEM.S.62.16.003 (rear left, with sound-damping), TOTEM.S.62.16.004 (rear right, with sound-damping — same family as the rear TOTEM part noted above). Price ~1,150 RUB per piece (RU market, June 2025), available on Ozon and other retailers. Pre-2017 models use the felt/wool version; newer models use plastic. Sellers often list outdated prices on their own sites, so verify current pricing before ordering 3738394041.

    Plastic mudguard availability — discontinued domestic production: Russian-made plastic rear mudguards (подкрылки из пластика) have been discontinued from local production. Current availability: (1) original felt/wool mudguards — still available from the FAW factory and authorized dealers; (2) Chinese aftermarket plastic versions — available on AliExpress, Yandex Market, Drom, and other platforms, but at a significant price premium over the original felt option; (3) some regional dealer stock may still exist (e.g. one owner in the Rostov area found front-and-rear sets available as combined units). Chinese retailers often fail to clearly differentiate between similar-looking products, so confirm exact fitment before ordering. Front mudguards are sometimes available separately from rear 424344454647484950515253545556.

    Front mud flaps sold as a pair only (2017 model): front mud flaps (брызговик) for the 2017 X80 generally available only as a pair from vendors, though some sellers may offer individual units 575859.

    Front fender splash guard — sourcing failure and unconfirmed Mazda 6 cross-fit suggestion: sourcing issues for the front fender splash guard were reported by multiple users — one plastic-parts supplier returned the buyer's money after 1 month without being able to fulfill the order. Community suggestion: a Mazda 6 (2014 model) splash guard may share a similar shape, but cross-compatibility is uncertain and worth testing before committing to a purchase 606162636465.

    KITZAP vs. KITZAPCHASTI — retailer sourcing distinction: when sourcing rear mud flaps for the 2017–2021 X80 (noting that pre-facelift 2017–2018 and facelift/restyle 2018–2021 fitment may differ, so verify compatibility with your generation before buying), community feedback distinguishes between two similarly-named retailers: KITZAP carries more original (OEM) parts, while KITZAPCHASTI mostly carries aftermarket analogues 6667686970.

    Rear plastic fender liner (mudguard) — part numbers: OEM part numbers for the rear plastic fender liners (подкрылки): right fender is 5CA0561H1, left fender is 5CA0561J1. Generic aftermarket alternatives are available from online retailers 71. The left one is also listed on Ozon (November 2025) 72.
