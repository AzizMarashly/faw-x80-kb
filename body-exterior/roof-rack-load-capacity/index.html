    Roof rack load capacity — rating vs. real use

    Tags: #price · #conflict

    Prices and suppliers on this page are Russian-market (RUB, at the dates shown). Verify locally before relying on them in Syria.

    Community members disagree on this topic — both positions are reported.

    Factory rating: a dealership told one owner the maximum roof load is 30 kg 12. This is echoed for crossbars separately, with a factory spec of roughly 50–60 kg for crossbar-mounted loads 3.

    Conflict — real-world use well above the factory number: multiple owners report routinely carrying much more without issue: 60 kg cargo box 4, ~80 kg of boards 5, and up to "100 kg, load confidently" per community consensus, while avoiding sharp cornering above that load 6. On Ozon reviews for aftermarket crossbars, owners report 95+ kg in personal use, with some reviews describing 100–110 kg carried without problems 7. The dealership's conservative 30 kg figure is likely set for liability reasons rather than reflecting a hard mechanical limit.

    Recommendation: stay under ~100 kg total load, avoid high-speed operation (roughly 130 km/h or less), drive slowly/cautiously with a large load ("very slowly and not far" was one report for long 6-metre items) 8, and brake/corner gradually since the load raises and shifts the centre of gravity.

    Aftermarket crossbars — sourcing and pricing: aluminum crossbars from Chinese sellers on Ozon run about 3,300 RUB with ~1 month delivery, or about 4,500 RUB with faster Russian-domestic shipping (2025 prices) 910.

    Roof panel flexibility — separate from crossbar load-capacity question: distinct from crossbar/rack ratings above, the X80's roof panel itself is notably flexible and soft, bending noticeably under moderate weight. Concern was raised about roof strength specifically under roof-rack loads, but reports indicate a light cargo item (e.g. a ~40 kg boat) with a properly-attached rack causes no visible damage. Not recommended for concentrated point loads or for standing/climbing on the roof 1112131415.
