    Body galvanization and panel corrosion

    Tags: #corrosion · #restyle · #paint

    Galvanization is not uniform across the body: doors and hood are galvanized (zinc-coated, "цинк") on the X80, confirmed by multiple owners 12. The tailgate/trunk lid (дверь багажника) is a known exception — not galvanized on at least some cars, which several owners independently identified as a weak point for corrosion 345. Corrosion patterns vary significantly between individual vehicles depending on climate, maintenance, and how the car is kept — some 2018–2019 cars show no meaningful rust after 100,000+ km, others develop reddish rust spots ("рыжики"), especially on door edges and around the tailgate/5th door 67. ⚠️ Conflict: one reply in the chat says the body is galvanized but the doors and fenders are not, which contradicts the doors-galvanized reports above (the original question is not preserved) 8.

    Tailgate chrome-trim corner corrosion — a recurring "Restyling" weak point: multiple owners report rust/corrosion appearing specifically at the tailgate corners, where the chrome trim meets the body panel around the rear window glass frame. Paint oxidation and rust form beneath the chrome trim at both bottom outer corners of the tailgate. Root cause: weak clear coat combined with the tailgate's non-galvanized (bare steel) construction 91011121314151617181920521.

    Conflict — generation difference: one owner reports "all restyle [facelift] cars have this problem" with corner corrosion appearing, while a pre-facelift (2017) owner with 7 years of ownership reports the car is "fully galvanized, zero corrosion" 2223. This is consistent with the broader galvanization-coverage reports above and suggests the pre-facelift generation may have had more complete factory galvanization than the restyle generation, though sample sizes are small.

    Preventive measures: regular inspection of the tailgate corners and trim junctions; apply protective wax/sealant at trim junctions; monitor for trapped moisture under the chrome trim.

    Broader galvanization assessment: main chassis protected but quality declined vs. Soviet-era process: the X80 body is described as selectively galvanized — the main chassis is protected, but doors, fenders, and the hood lack zinc coating (this conflicts with an earlier report above stating doors and hood ARE galvanized — may reflect generation differences or inconsistent assessments). Pre-facelift has better galvanization than facelift. Modern cold-galvanizing (per current TU/ТУ standard) is inferior in quality to the old Soviet hot-dip galvanizing process (per GOST/ГОСТ standard) — "market economy" is cited as the reason for the shift. Corrosion appears under chromed edges and at uncoated panel joints. For comparison: a Dongfeng AX7 suffered complete rust failure within just 2 years, despite marketing claims of "excellent body protection technology" tied to its being co-manufactured with Citroën in China — doors were repainted or fully replaced under warranty, and engine-bay-side hoods were uniformly brown (rusted) with no galvanization at all; the X80 by comparison appears to have at least some protection, even if reduced from earlier standards 24252627828293031323334353637.

    Rear-door rust susceptibility — variation by color and assembly batch: rear door panels show rust issues on some units, with variation reported by both paint color and assembly factory (the Kaliningrad factory specifically mentioned). Brown-colored cars appear particularly prone to rust on panel edges and under chrome trim; white and red colors reportedly show better corrosion resistance. The issue may correlate with paint-adhesion quality or a material batch difference from manufacturing 383940414243. ⚠️ Conflict: the color/rust correlation is unclear — one owner (38730) reports no rust complaints on red cars, while another (38728) reports heavy rust on a brown car despite Kaliningrad assembly.

    Roof added to the known non-galvanized panels; general paint-quality complaint: consistent with the tailgate/trunk-lid exception noted above, the roof is also reported as not galvanized on the X80 chassis — otherwise the chassis is galvanized. Separately, community reports indicate generally poor paint quality (ЛКП) as an ongoing issue affecting the vehicle. Options discussed for paint protection: liquid glass coating or vinyl wrap 444546.

    Trunk door (5th door) interior weld corrosion — common failure point: rust around the trunk-button area on the inside of the trunk/5th door is a common X80 failure point, distinct from the tailgate-corner chrome-trim corrosion above. Location: interior welds where two body panels are joined together. Progression: rust first appears as a coating beneath the sealer, then spreads outward. Treatment option: inject Mowil (a rust-preventative product) into the interior seams from the inside. A full replacement fix requires cutting and re-gluing the window glass plus new moldings — which can themselves leak. Most FAW X80 owners reportedly experience this to some degree; by comparison, other Chinese-market cars in the same class (Geely Emgrand, Lifan) were reported as worse 474849505152535455.

    Zinc coating coverage — roof and trunk lid confirmed NOT zinc-coated despite marketing claims (reinforcing report): further confirmation, consistent with the roof/trunk-lid exception noted above: the roof and baggage/trunk (5th door) are not zinc-coated on the X80, contrary to marketing claims — only the external side panels receive zinc coating. The hood, by contrast, is zinc-coated and shows visibly better rust resistance when exposed 56575859.
