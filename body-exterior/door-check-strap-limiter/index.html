    Door check strap/limiter — sourcing

    Tags: #door · #part-number · #cross-compatibility · #mazda-6-compat

    Prices and suppliers on this page are Russian-market (RUB, at the dates shown). Verify locally before relying on them in Syria.

    Reported issue: the driver's door does not hold/stay in an open position (no working check strap/limiter). A door limiter/strap is available from KitParts (kitparts.ru), around 1,500 RUB (RU market, March 2025) 12.

    Cross-compatibility: a Mazda 6 (GG) repair kit for this part does not fit the X80 — one owner has one sitting unused after it failed to work. A CX-5 variant is reported to fit instead, though this wasn't independently confirmed by the reporting owner (secondhand information — "they say it fits") 3.

    Mazda generation matters — Mazda 6 II–III (GH/GJ/GL) fits, GG does not: this is a generation-specific compatibility, consistent with the CX-5-fits/GG-doesn't-fit finding above. Door limiters/check straps from Mazda 6 II–III (2007–2017, bodies GH/GJ/GL) fit and function correctly, preventing door slamming with smooth damping. Limiters from the older Mazda 6 GG (2002–2008) do not fit 45678910.

    Repair-kit version — full disassembly required, fragile parts: a repair kit for the limiter (rather than a full pre-assembled unit) is also cross-compatible from Mazda 6 II–III (GH/GJ/GL, part TYPE 14016), available on Ozon.ru. Installation requires removing the entire door panel to access the mechanism, removing the plastic shims, and inserting the kit's pins in a specific herringbone ("ёлочкой") pattern; clips are reused as needed during reassembly. The kit's plastic teeth are fragile — handle carefully, since they're prone to breaking, especially on a second attempt at repositioning 111213141516171819.

    Door hinge limiter kits (4-door set) — also Mazda 6 II–III: door hinge limiter/gap-stopper kits (distinct from the check-strap kits above, though from the same donor generation) designed for Mazda 6 II–III (2007–2017) fit the X80 as a direct replacement without modification. Kits come as a 4-door set. Installation: remove the door panel, remove the door speaker, disconnect the old hinge limiter, install the new kit — about 30–40 minutes per door. These kits run tighter tolerances than OEM, so assembling with a vise is recommended for a proper fit 202122.

    Door limiter/catch clip replacement — full procedure, sizing, and break-in: limiter/catch clips (the shock-absorbing part of the mechanism) commonly fail after 50,000–200,000 km, causing the symptom "door swings closed too easily / won't stay open." Fix: replace with Mazda 6 II–III door-limiter repair kits (Ozon listing: https://ozon.ru/t/dQ9CuRk or similar); Mazda 6 GG-II or CX-5-II variants are also reported to fit. Installation is easier with a vise for gently bending the clips, but can be done with a slotted screwdriver, hammer, pliers, and a pry bar. All four doors can be fitted. Sizing note: replacement clips sit slightly lower in profile than the original (13.23 mm stock vs. 12.75–13.6 mm for the aftermarket alternatives), but they still function correctly — new clips may click/squeak initially but typically break in over time 2324252627282930313233343536373839404142434445.

    Squeak can persist after replacement — internal mechanism, not the pivot: even after replacing the limiter straps with Mazda 6 units and applying silicone, dry lubricant, or graphite-based lubricants, a squeak can return after roughly 6 months, because the noise originates inside the mechanism itself, not at the external pivot point where lubricant is normally applied. The fix in that case is to replace the entire pre-assembled limiter unit again rather than trying to service individual components 464748.

    Cross-brand compatible limiters — Mazda CX-5/CX-9 (confirmed); Mazda GG (does not fit): factory FAW X80 door limiters can be replaced with Mazda CX-5 or CX-9 limiters. Mazda GG door limiters do NOT fit properly. Original FAW part number: 5CA058270. Mazda CX-5 limiters are an inexpensive alternative (available via an Ozon listing). Installation requires opening 4 metal clips per door with pliers/chisel, taking roughly 10 minutes per door. The stock design uses white rubber pads with a limited service life 49505152535455565758596061626364. The front-door limiter (ограничитель передней двери) 5CA058270 is listed at kitzapchasti; a zzap search for 5CA058270A was also posted, and the A suffix may be a different variant, so confirm with the seller 5365.

    Door-limiter repair-kit pricing: Mazda CX-5 door-limiter repair kits are available on Ozon or AliExpress; price reference: approximately 1,500 RUB from AliExpress (RU market, May 2024). Manual installation takes 1–2 hours per pair of doors. Alternatively, some owners buy complete replacement limiters instead of repair kits 665467.

    Mazda 6 GH/GJ/GL limiters install without removing the door card — update to the GG compatibility note above: Mazda 6 GH (2007–2017) door limiters, particularly GH/GJ/GL body-code variants, can be installed on the X80 without removing the interior door card, per owner Eduard, who confirms GH/GJ/GL limiters install like OEM parts. ⚠️ Conflict/refinement: this nuances the "Mazda GG does not fit" note above — earlier Mazda 6 GG (1st gen, 2002–2007) limiters do work but require door-card disassembly first, whereas GH/GJ/GL versions fit more easily without that step; one source (Александр Марощук) notes the GG versions need internal door work, while another (Эдуард Янонис) reports GH/GJ/GL fitting more readily 6869707172.

    Squeaking limiter — plastic-spacer remedy (stiffer door as trade-off): some Mazda door-limiter replacements squeak once installed. One owner (Сергей Елисеев) resolved this by installing limiter versions with plastic transparent spacers instead of metal-only designs. This reduced the squeak, but the doors became noticeably stiffer to close — his wife needed both hands to close them afterward 737470.

    Additional confirmation — TYPE 14016 kit reported after limiters go loose: another separate owner report corroborates the Mazda 6 (II–III, GH/GJ/GL) TYPE 14016 repair-kit cross-compatibility noted above, after the factory door limiters had become loose and barely held the doors open ("родные практически не держут") 757677.
