    Tow hitch — certification status and installation

    Tags: #towing · #legal · #part-number · #conflict

    Prices and suppliers on this page are Russian-market (RUB, at the dates shown). Verify locally before relying on them in Syria.

    Community members disagree on this topic — both positions are reported.

    The X80 owner's manual states towing is NOT RECOMMENDED (not explicitly prohibited). The car is not certified for trailer use per the manufacturer's ID plate — the towing-load line is left blank. The AUPA (CVT/automatic) transmission is not rated for towing per some reports. Recommended load: under 200 kg of cargo, though practical users report carrying 200–300 kg periodically without issues 1234.

    Installation: the Sheriff model 3951.12 tow hitch is available aftermarket; installation requires cutting/modifying the gray plastic bumper cover — there is no OEM pre-drilled bumper mount, so the bumper must be removed for fitment 567.

    Legal status in Russia: installing an unapproved tow hitch voids some warranties; traffic police may require inspection/certification. Some owners have installed one after the warranty period expired without issues 8910.

    Conflict: the manual says towing is NOT RECOMMENDED; some owners interpret this as "not prohibited" and install a hitch anyway. There is also a transmission debate — one user claims the AUPA transmission is not certified for towing, while another notes that American-market AUPA-equipped cars tow routinely. General recommendation from the thread: assume the unofficial mod voids warranty, and install at your own risk, ideally after the guarantee period ends 278.

    Tow-hook installation — manual and automatic transmissions: a tow hook can be installed on both manual and automatic-transmission X80s. Installation takes roughly 2 hours: it requires removing the bumper and replacing the stock bumper reinforcer with the tow-hook unit; a coordination/adapter module must also be installed for the electrical connections (lights). Both manual and automatic owners report successful installs, though some recommend not towing heavy loads on the automatic 1112131415.

    Sheriff 3951.12 — capacity and suppliers: the Sheriff 3951.12 tow hitch for the FAW Besturn X80 offers 1.2-ton capacity. Available from multiple retailers including OZON (ozon.ru) and specialized tow-hitch (farkop) suppliers. Installation by the owner is feasible given basic mechanical ability 1617.

    Sheriff 3951.12 — professional install confirmed: a Sheriff 3951.12 tow hitch was installed by a specialist installer (ES-FARKOP.RU, professional tow-hitch installation service) and confirmed as a fitting option for the X80 181920.

    ⚠️ Conflict — does installation require bumper removal? A separate report describes the Sheriff 3951.12 fitting into the factory mounting points without modifying the vehicle body, with no bumper removal required — only unclipping the bumper's lower section — and states the supplier provided a factory certificate confirming no structural changes were made. This directly contradicts the installation note above (which describes cutting/modifying the bumper cover with no pre-drilled OEM mount). The discrepancy may reflect different hitch models, model years, or installer technique; verify with your specific kit/installer before assuming either method 2122232425.

    Tow hitch reduces ground clearance: an owner with a tow hitch installed reports less comfortable off-road driving due to reduced ground clearance — the vehicle scrapes on uneven terrain where it previously cleared without the hitch fitted. This is noted as a known trade-off of aftermarket hitch installation 26.

    Manufacturer's passport sheet for article 3951.12: the Russian passport sheet shared for the X80 hitch (тягово-сцепное устройство, ТСУ — towing hitch) lists a permitted total trailer mass of 1,200 kg, a vertical load on the ball (вертикальная нагрузка на шар) of 50 kg and a net weight of 16.07 kg, for cars built from 2013. The hitch is stated to comply with GOST 28248-89, OST 37.001.299-93 and TU 4591-001-64264699-2012, with a 12-month warranty from the date of sale 18. Its installation steps are: (1) remove the bumper; (2) trim the bumper to the cut diagram "Рис. А" (cut dimensions 60, 100 and 210 mm); (3) remove the bumper reinforcement (усилитель бампера); (4) install brackets "18"; (5) join brackets "17" and "16" to brackets "18"; (6) attach the bar (штанга) "1" to the brackets; (7) trim the bumper to fit; (8) fit the hook (крюк) and the socket box (подрозетник); (9) wire the electrical equipment 18. This sheet calls for removing and trimming the bumper, so it supports the first installation note above rather than the no-removal report 18.

    Fastener torque from the same sheet: the parts list uses DIN bolts M8x30, M10x30, M12x1.25x40 and M12x1.25x70. Tightening torque by thread: M6x1.0 = 9.8 N·m; M8x1.25 = 24.0 N·m; M10x1.5 = 47.0 N·m; M12x1.75 = 81.0 N·m; M16x2 = 157.0 N·m. The table gives M12 as 1.75 pitch while the parts list uses M12x1.25 bolts, so check the pitch on the actual hardware before torquing 18.
