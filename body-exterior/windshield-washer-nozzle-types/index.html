    Windshield washer nozzle types

    Tags: #part-number

    Prices and suppliers on this page are Russian-market (RUB, at the dates shown). Verify locally before relying on them in Syria.

    Two nozzle types are used for the washer system: (1) fan-type (веерные) — spray spreads and disperses easily but is less effective at highway speed due to wind pressure; (2) nozzles mounted on the wiper blades (поводки дворников) — more effective at highway speeds since the spray point moves with the blade. One owner attempted to replace the factory nozzles and accidentally cracked the windshield during installation — caution advised. Another owner reports the fan-type nozzles give a noticeably weak stream 123456.

    No factory washer-fluid level sensor: the X80 does not have a windshield-washer fluid level sensor, unlike some other FAW models or previous cars like the Duster. Drivers must manually monitor the fluid level and refill — there's no dashboard warning indicator when it runs out. Community consensus: this feature was omitted despite the dashboard's indicator icons suggesting otherwise 7891011.

    Universal fan-type nozzles with 4-way guides (DIY upgrade): universal fan-type washer nozzles with 4-way guide tubes ("поводки") give better spray coverage than the stock nozzles. Recommended part numbers: KD4567510, KD5367510. The nozzles need fine adjustment with a needle to direct the spray pattern, and should be set up at around 110 km/h for proper wind-adjusted spray. A check valve ("обратный клапан") can be added to prevent backflow to the tank while wiping 121314151617181920.

    Nozzle installation on wiper-blade guides: install washer nozzles on the wiper-blade arms by routing tubing along the wiper-arm structure. Remove the hood seal strip ("жабо") to access the mounting area underneath. Route the tube through the hood-seal area and split it to both wiper blades with a T-connector; secure with mounting brackets so it doesn't interfere with wiper-blade motion 21222324252627282930313233343536.

    Spray-pattern (распыл) nozzle cross-compatibility: the stock X80 nozzles spray in streams only, with no adjustable pattern. CX-5 nozzles reportedly provide a spray pattern (распыл) with adjustment capability; Mazda 6 nozzles can also be sourced for compatibility. Universal spray-type washer nozzles from Ozon are available as well (product links shared in the community). These nozzles allow fine adjustment to optimize the spray pattern for both wipers, and several owners report good results switching from the stream-only stock type to a spray-type replacement 3738394041424344.

    Adjusting spray direction toward windshield center: the stock nozzles can be adjusted to redirect their spray toward the center of the windshield instead of straight down. Method: use a hard metal awl or needle to nudge the nozzle opening — a paper clip is too soft and won't work. Access is through the open hood. The nozzles are seated tightly and require firm tool pressure to adjust; one owner mentioned using an awl/pin attached to a keychain as a handy always-available tool for this 4546474849505152535455.

    Mazda CX-5 fan-spray nozzle cross-fit — part numbers and adjustment technique: stock washer nozzles spray weakly. Replacement with Mazda CX-5 fan-spray nozzles — part KD53-67-510 (approx. 650–1,200 RUB per pair, RU market, June 2026) or KD4567510 (approx. 1,200 RUB; RU market, June 2026) — gives a superior fan-spray pattern that covers more of the windshield. One owner sourced Mazda CX-5 units secondhand via Avito for a lower cost, confirmed fit without modification. Installation: (1) swap in the nozzles for the factory units; (2) the spray angle is adjustable — use small tweezers to nudge the direction up/down by pivoting the outer housing, and rotate the nozzle itself for left/right spray vector; (3) do not insert tools into the slit on the nozzle's internal siphon — the plastic will crack. The internal siphon regulates pressure/volume and cannot be field-adjusted. The stock pump itself is weak, so high-quality nozzles only partially solve the problem — one user also tried a new pump but reported limited spray improvement, indicating the weakness is system-level rather than nozzle-only 56575859606162636465666768.

    Spacer tip for spray-angle clearance, broader compatibility list, and price (~2,500 RUB, RU market, March 2026): further confirms the Mazda CX-5 fan-nozzle cross-fit above — the same nozzle part is also listed as compatible with Mazda 6 GJ/GL (3rd gen), CX-5 KE/KF, and CX-9 TC. If a nozzle's spray hits the wiper blade, the community recommends adding thin spacers underneath the nozzle to adjust the spray angle. Price point: ~2,500 RUB via Ozon (RU market, March 2026) 697071727374.

    Fan-to-jet nozzle swap — resale value of stock fan nozzles: stock X80 washer nozzles are the fan/spray pattern type. One owner replaced them with jet-type nozzles sourced from a Mazda CX-5, reporting jet nozzles are better suited to highway use. The removed stock fan nozzles can be resold used for roughly 500 RUB per pair (RU market, June 2026) 75.

    Spray-direction adjustment — needle/wire technique (earlier report): consistent with the awl/needle adjustment method above, an earlier report gives the same core technique with more procedural detail: if one nozzle hits the wiper blade and the other sprays too high (toward the roof), correct the direction using a needle or small-diameter stiff wire (not sharp). Procedure: raise the hood and the wipers, access the nozzles, rotate them slightly — moves as small as 0.5 mm are significant — test the spray on the windshield, and repeat until the desired direction is achieved. Use wire rather than sharp implements to avoid damage 767778798081.

    Clearing a clogged or corroded nozzle. Washer nozzles can corrode or clog over time, reducing or blocking the spray. The tool of choice is a needle, used gently in the opening — the same tool as for aiming the spray above. A shim or thin blade is discouraged: it can bend or snap off inside the nozzle and make the clog worse (one owner who tried a shim only bent it, while a needle worked cleanly). If the nozzles are severely corroded/seized ("закисли") and needle-clearing is not reliable, some owners suggest skipping the repair and fitting ready-made fan-style spray nozzles instead (see the fan-type and Mazda CX-5 options above).828384
