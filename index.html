    FAW Besturn X80 Knowledge Base

    Community knowledge base for the FAW Besturn X80: fixes, part numbers and cross-brand alternatives, prices, specs, and maintenance — compiled from owner communities. Every fact is attributed to its original author with a link to the original message; this site organizes the community's knowledge, it does not claim it.

    Where this knowledge comes from. The sources are Russian-speaking owner communities. Prices are for the Russian market (RUB) at the date shown next to each one, and shops named are Russian. Advice about frost and winter applies to Russian climate. Facts about the car itself (part numbers, procedures, faults) transfer to any market — verify prices and suppliers locally in Syria.
