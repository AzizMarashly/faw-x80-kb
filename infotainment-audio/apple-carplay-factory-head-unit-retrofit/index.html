    Wireless CarPlay retrofit (Carlinkit)

    Tags: #head-unit · #firmware

    Prices and suppliers on this page are Russian-market (RUB, at the dates shown). Verify locally before relying on them in Syria.

    CarPlay can be retrofitted onto the factory head unit (no aftermarket Android unit required): (1) download the firmware from the faw-cars.ru website, which also has a detailed installation guide; (2) flash the factory unit following the included instructions; (3) purchase a Carlinkit wireless CarPlay adapter; (4) connect the Carlinkit adapter via USB to the head unit. This method works with iOS devices only. Android-phone users who want a similar wireless-mirroring experience should instead consider replacing the head unit with an aftermarket Android unit, or adding a separate Android mirroring display 123.

    Carlinkit wireless adapters — general product line and pricing: Carlinkit wireless-connectivity adapters enable wireless CarPlay or Android Auto on factory radios that already support the wired versions. Model 4.0 CPC200-CP2A works with both iPhone and Android devices. Typical cost: 2,000–3,500 RUB depending on source (RU market; Ozon ~3,500 RUB in spring 2025; secondhand from local sellers ~2,000 RUB). Some cheaper models only support iPhone — dual-OS models cost more. Build quality varies between sellers (some units arrive with no manufacturer branding). Function: converts the factory unit's wired CarPlay/Android Auto port into a wireless connection. Works reliably with navigation, messaging, and music apps once paired 4567891011121314151617.

    Carlinkit CP2A — Android Auto installation, features, and limitations: the CP2A (v4.0) wireless adapter enables Android Auto specifically on the factory head unit via an OTA (over-the-air) connection. Cost: approximately 3,000 RUB (July 2025, RU market). Setup: install the adapter, pair with the phone over Bluetooth — the phone then acts as the wireless bridge (no wired connection needed; phone battery drains normally, no faster than usual). Load time from ignition-on: roughly 3 seconds. Working features: navigation (Yandex.Navigator, Google Maps, 2GIS), music playback, voice control (Google Assistant), and reading/replying to SMS/Telegram messages (reply only — voice reply works, but typing/composing a new Telegram message directly is not supported). Compatibility caveats: Samsung/Huawei phones can have issues due to Knox antivirus software and the absence of Google Play Services; only apps in Android Auto's supported list work (custom apps and games are not supported); the head unit has no backup battery, so navigation state resets when the engine is turned off but resumes once restarted. Installation time: about 30 minutes with prior preparation (closer to 2 hours the first time; ~20 minutes on subsequent installs/reinstalls). Pairing is immediate on a OnePlus 11 or other "pure"/stock Android device; Samsung/Huawei users may experience pairing delays 1819202122232425.

    CarlinKit X1CA/X2CA pricing and prerequisite (Baidu CarLife): the CarlinKit X1CA/X2CA wireless adapter costs 3,000–3,700 RUB on AliExpress, or roughly 20,000 RUB on Russian marketplaces (January 2026) — the large price gap makes AliExpress clearly the better source. It enables wireless Android Auto and Apple CarPlay via the factory head unit's USB connection. Requires the factory head unit to already support wired CarPlay/Android Auto via the Baidu CarLife protocol. It's a cheaper alternative to a full 5,000–40,000 RUB (Jan 2026, RU market) aftermarket head-unit replacement, though audio quality still depends on the factory sound preparation — aftermarket head units (4,000–40,000 RUB) offer better sound at higher cost 26272829303132.

    Firmware update for wired CarPlay support (community-shared files): the factory head-unit firmware can be updated to enable wired Apple CarPlay support directly (as an alternative/precursor to the faw-cars.ru method above). Procedure: (1) download the firmware files from a Yandex Disk link shared within the community; (2) prepare two USB drives with the correct formatting; (3) follow the step-by-step visual instructions provided by the community; (4) execute the firmware update, which runs as a two-stage process. After the update, wireless CarPlay/Android Auto adapters (Carlinkit-type, see above) work seamlessly on top of the updated firmware. Some owners prefer to keep the original OEM interface for aesthetics while adding wireless functionality via the external adapter rather than switching to an aftermarket screen; professional installation is available at some service shops for owners who don't want to DIY-flash the unit 333435363738394041424344.

    Carlinkit adapter tiers — basic vs. Android-OS premium model: two Carlinkit product tiers are reported, distinct from the CP2A/X1CA/X2CA models above: (1) a basic model without built-in Android — roughly 2,000 RUB (March 2026, RU market), minimal functionality (phone-mirroring connection only); (2) a premium model with a built-in Android OS — roughly 9,800 RUB on AliExpress, or ~20,000 RUB on Ozon (https://ozon.ru/t/lwvPyAx). The premium version displays a full Android interface directly on the stock monitor, with integrated navigation, music, and radar-detector ("antiradar") apps — no separate phone mount or Baidu CarLife integration is needed once installed. Works with the factory head unit after the wired-CarPlay firmware update described above 45464748.

    Carlinkit Tbox Ambient (8GB/128GB) — Android head-unit-in-a-dongle: the Carlinkit Tbox Ambient is effectively a 10-inch-capable Android computer-in-a-box (8/128GB storage) that runs through the stock head unit via wireless CarPlay, working on both restyle and pre-restyle models. It requires the wired-CarPlay firmware update (above) for full functionality. Performance is rated 8–9/10, with no reported lag; occasional screen-responsiveness inconsistency is attributed to the factory touchscreen digitizer itself rather than the Tbox adapter. Available on OZON 4950515253545556575859.

    Reports from other threads (2025-2026). Firmware route, secondhand: one member says a YouTube video showed an iPhone connecting by CarPlay to the X80 head unit, and that the unit needs a firmware update first; he found no working Android Auto method and says official dealers told him they cannot help if the phone does not connect. This is secondhand and unverified, but it matches the firmware-then-adapter route described above 60. Android Auto add-on: an owner reports a 3,000 RUB Android Auto add-on for the stock screen (July 2025, Russia) that adds navigation (Yandex, Google, 2GIS), music, SMS reply and voice control; it does not add arbitrary apps, Telegram shows notifications only, and voice dialling works without touching the screen. This agrees with the CP2A figures above 6162. Android box price: a CarlinKit CarPlay AI box (Android 13, 8 GB RAM, 128 GB storage) was listed at 12,594 RUB after a 56% discount (April 2026, Russia), the same 8/128 GB class as the Tbox Ambient above 63. For installing a full replacement unit instead, see the Android head unit buying guide.
