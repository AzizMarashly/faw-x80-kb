    Audio and panoramic camera wiring diagrams

    Tags: #wiring-diagram · #antenna · #fuse · #head-unit

    Two pages of the factory X80 wiring-diagram set (EWD, English) cover the audio system and the panoramic view monitor. The files are in the diagnostic-manual chapter list in shared files and manuals. The text extracted from the audio page is truncated at 40,000 characters, so the rest of that diagram is missing.

    Audio system (EWD page 1109, file Z009 AUDIO.pdf). Fuses as printed: Audio Si52 25A (the rating's position next to the label is ambiguous), BTN (Si1) 40A, Audio/PKE (Si60) 5A. Speakers: FL1 driver door (LH), FR4 front passenger door (RH), RL3 rear door LH, RR4 rear door RH; tweeters FL3 (LH) and FR1 (RH). Other parts: antenna assembly AN1 (with antenna power), USB-A port I24, microphone H4, multi-function control panels 1 (I20) and 2 (I23), steering-wheel spiral cable I29, LCD I7, rear view monitor SR1 (gray)-1, and LVDS links to the panoramic monitor controller I61 and the TFT I19. The gateway control unit and BCM (refer to page 1117) are on the same bus 1. For speaker sizes and upgrades see factory speakers and audio upgrades.

    Panoramic view monitor (EWD page 1110, file Z010.pdf). Fuse: PKE/EPB 5A (Si58), with the main fuses (Si13 125A, Si71 generator 175A, Si72 175A) on the same feed. Cameras and monitors: F49 front camera (green), FL6 panoramic view monitor (blue), FR7 panoramic view monitor (brown), SR1 rear view monitor (gray)-1. Controller: I62 Panoramic View Monitor Controller. Connectors: I60 front (green)-2, I56 LH (blue)-2, I59 LH (gray)-2, I52 RH (brown)-2, I61 LVDS controller (green)-2, I47 audio H (green)-1. Cross-references: gateway control unit to page 1113, PKE controller to 1119, radar switch to 1125. Pin numbers are printed in the text but the connector mapping is not fully clear 2. For the aftermarket side of a 360° system see side-mirror camera installation.
