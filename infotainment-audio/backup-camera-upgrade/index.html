    Backup camera upgrade

    Tags: #connector

    Prices and suppliers on this page are Russian-market (RUB, at the dates shown). Verify locally before relying on them in Syria.

    A backup (reversing) camera can be upgraded to a higher-resolution model without any rewiring. The standard connector pin count on the X80 remains the same across camera models — the new camera simply plugs into the existing harness via its connector. No cable modification or rewiring is needed 12.

    Aftermarket rear-camera installation — three mounting methods compared: three approaches for installing an aftermarket reversing camera: Method 1 — drill a hole in the trunk lid and seal it with sealant; this causes rust after ~3 years and is not recommended. Method 2 — route the wire through the license-plate light fixture, cutting a corner of the plastic trim to fit the wire, and mount the camera above the plate; this has proven stable for 3+ years. Method 3 — use a license-plate-light bracket with a built-in camera mount, requiring no drilling; described as the better alternative overall 34567891011.

    Upgrading to a Sony 170° camera, Teyes head-unit route: the stock reversing camera can be upgraded to an aftermarket Sony 170-degree camera for improved wide-angle visibility. These aftermarket units are sold either bundled with Teyes-branded head units or as standalone accessories. Installation requires a frame adapter and a wiring harness; some fitments may require minor dashboard modifications 1213141516.

    Weak backup-camera visibility — reverse-light angle mismatch (auxiliary light fix): factory reverse lights in the tail-light clusters aim upward, while the reversing camera mounted in the hatch aims downward — this angular mismatch leaves the area directly behind the car (the camera's field of view) poorly lit, so the backup-camera image on the monitor shows little to no illumination when reversing at night. Suggested fix: add an auxiliary reversing light that aims downward to properly light the area the camera is actually looking at 171819.

    Recamlux AHD 1080p camera. A Recamlux AHD 1080p rear-view camera for 12-24 V vehicles is sold on Wildberries (low-confidence mention; no price or fitment report) 20. For the X80's own connector and mounting options see the sections above.
