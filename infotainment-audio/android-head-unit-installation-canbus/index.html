    Aftermarket Android head unit — CANBUS wiring

    Tags: #head-unit · #esp · #installation · #parking-sensor

    Prices and suppliers on this page are Russian-market (RUB, at the dates shown). Verify locally before relying on them in Syria.

    CANBUS adapter is required on restyle X80 (2018+) when installing an aftermarket Android head unit, otherwise a false ESP error light appears on the dashboard 12345. Verified working adapters: - Hiworld CANBUS, model FW08.11 H2AGY9D30A — confirmed no false errors 67. - Raysen CANBUS — alternative option 8. - Mayifun BOS-mini A5 CANBUS — also confirmed working 9. - Cable cost: ~2,000 RUB on AliExpress (Sept 2024, RU market) 10.

    If the ESP light appears despite a CANBUS adapter, an alternative/additional fix is updating the original radio's MCU firmware (file CR482XA_V1.56_20200224.MCU); some owners needed to update both the radio firmware and the CANBUS adapter firmware together 1110. TEYES CC3L/CC3 are the most commonly installed models for the 2018–2022 restyle 6.

    Dashboard panel removal (to access the factory radio slot): use plastic trim-removal tools (127–1,500 RUB on OZON depending on quality; Sept 2024, RU market); improvised plastic pry tools work too, with higher risk of panel damage. Procedure: remove trim panels around the radio, disconnect electrical connectors, remove mounting clips, slide the unit out; reverse to install. Clips/connectors are fairly durable 121314151617.

    CANBUS cable for tablet-style units: FAW X80 2018+ specific cable interfaces factory wiring with the aftermarket radio, available on Ozon and other retailers 1819.

    Antenna: the factory antenna is very short and needs an extension when installing an aftermarket unit; extension cables with standard connectors are sold on Ozon — route the extended antenna to the upper dash or under roof trim. Complete installation kits often include the extension 202122.

    Full install checklist (from a documented DIY installation): route wires through the air vents to the dash using anti-squeak tape; integrate the rear camera with parking sensors (may need to remove broken factory connector clamps); install an external microphone on the dash panel to avoid a Bluetooth feedback loop — internal mics on the factory unit are disabled by the manufacturer; expect extra time if wire channels are tight 23242526272829.

    Total antenna count on a fully-loaded install: 3–6 antennas (3 GPS + 2 GSM/ GLONASS + 2 Starline + 2 head-unit, up to 6 with OTA) — plus relocating the climate control module and rerouting harnesses 3031323334353637.

    Known fix — outside temperature stops showing after stereo swap: after installing an aftermarket stereo (e.g. Teyes), the outside-temperature display can disappear from the instrument cluster. Causes/fixes: (1) enable the temperature toggle in the stereo's own settings menu ("Наружная температура"); (2) verify the correct X80 model/CANBUS profile is selected in setup; (3) some trims simply lack the external temp sensor 38394041424344454647484950515253.

    Parking sensor compatibility after Android head unit swap: parking sensors may stop working after installing an aftermarket Android head unit. Cause: the Android unit may not support the original CANBUS protocol, or the aftermarket parking sensors themselves lack CAN compatibility. Workarounds: (1) verify the Android unit supports CANBUS for original-sensor integration (check its settings); (2) if the sensors are aftermarket/non-OEM, they may be incompatible with the CAN adapter — some installers add an extra interface block to bridge the sensors and the stereo; (3) some installations cannot simultaneously support both parking sensors and steering-wheel controls through a single CAN interface — choose the priority based on preference 545556.

    Steering-wheel button integration: when installing an aftermarket infotainment unit, the head unit auto-detects the vehicle and CANbus signals in its settings during installation — the CANbus profile must be manually configured to match the correct FAW X80 model/year, or steering-wheel buttons will not function. One owner initially skipped this step and had no button response; the fix (matching CANbus profile) was worked out via private message with another group member 57585960616263.

    Aftermarket infotainment installation with factory integration: aftermarket head units can integrate with several factory systems — rear camera (where fitted), steering-wheel buttons (via CANbus), and remote control (on some trims). Installation vendors such as frontcam.ru sell pre-configured units for the X80 with mounting brackets and wiring harnesses, and offer bundle promotions (head unit + accessories such as a dash cam, voice-assistant activation, or rear camera). CANbus settings must match the vehicle's actual configuration — automatic-transmission models use a different CANbus profile than manual-transmission ones, and manual (MKPP) installs are more prone to wiring/parking-sensor/rear-camera issues that need an electrician's help to resolve. Installers report the process is well-documented ("installation for dummies"), with the CANbus profile usually obtained from the seller via chat 646566676869707172737475.

    Facelift model — Teyes CC3L popular choice: Teyes CC3L (4GB RAM / 32GB storage, 10-inch screen) is a popular Android head unit for the facelift (рестайлинг) X80, sold with a CAN bus adapter included. Price on Wildberries: ~5,000–6,000 RUB for the unit alone, plus ~4,000 RUB more for a premium installation (December 2025, RU market) 7677787980818283848586. Installation notes: factory trim clips are very tight and easy to break — take your time; the plastic housing under the rear-view camera needs filing/cutting because the factory camera connector differs in size from the aftermarket one; antenna and USB wiring often can't reach the new head-unit location and must be rerouted through the dashboard, commonly via the glove-box area. The factory rear-view camera's image can look worse on the larger new screen; some owners plan to pair the head unit with a higher-resolution aftermarket camera 86.

    Teyes CC3L CAN bus profile mismatch — use Mazda 6 settings: on a 2017 pre-restyle X80, a Teyes CC3L initially set to the X80 CAN bus profile left the parktronics and multifunction steering-wheel controls non-working and the climate display blank. Fix: switch the CAN bus configuration to Mazda 6 settings — this restored all controls except the climate feedback display (still blank). Installation requires careful wire routing; the climate harness is short and may need slight trim removal to reach 87888990919293949596979899100.

    10-inch retrofit — universal adapter frame, rear-frame trimming, power steering cooler clearance: installing a 10-inch Android head unit requires a 10-inch universal adapter frame mounted to the factory fascia. The rear lip of the frame may need trimming to fit; resulting gaps can be sealed with epoxy resin. Before finalizing the install, verify the power steering cooler depth for clearance behind the new unit — the cooler can interfere with the larger tablet-style unit's housing depth 101102.

    Teyes UIS8141E tablet-style unit — connector-damage risk and performance notes: one owner (Kirill) installed a Teyes UIS8141E Android tablet-style head unit (specs: 4 cores @ 1.3 GHz, 2GB RAM, 32GB storage, Android 8.1). Issues encountered during installation: the rear camera connector was damaged (a contact torn) and the physical radio buttons below the screen stopped working (suspected loose cable/connector) — a caution for anyone installing this specific tablet-style unit. Performance notes: Yandex Navigator v3.96 runs acceptably fast on this hardware; the newer v27.0.2 is noticeably slow. Recommendation from the reporting owner: consult the 4PDA forum for custom ROMs and hardware-specific optimizations for tablet radios. Overall, tablet-radio integration is viable but requires careful connector handling during install 103104105106107108.

    10-inch head-unit mounting panel — dorestyle only: a custom mounting panel for a 10-inch aftermarket head unit ("magnetola") is available for pre-restyle FAW X80 models only — not compatible with restyle (2018+) variants. It allows installing a large modern touchscreen unit in place of the stock radio opening 109110.

    BOS-MINI A2 multimedia player: the BOS-MINI A2 car multimedia player (4K AHD, CarPlay, steering-wheel control, MirrorLink, voice command) is compatible with the X80. Installation requires a CANbus adapter (purchased separately, not included) to keep steering-wheel control and dashboard integration working. The unit has a 1280×720 screen, FM radio, Bluetooth, and USB 111112113114115116117.

    GU Element-5 Android head unit — pricing and features (2022): a popular aftermarket head unit, the GU Element-5 (Android OS, optional sound processor), cost 27,000 RUB (2022, reported March 2024, RU market), negotiable down to 29,000 RUB — professional installation labor added 1,700 RUB. Features: display-timeout/screen-always-off mode, adjustable time-display angle, anti-glare settings. Note: some restyle-model owners experience windshield glare reflections off the unit's screen due to a different tint angle versus pre-restyle vehicles. Compatibility is confirmed across generations 118119120121122123.

    Teyes tablet head-unit — CANbus + mounting-frame requirement (2018+): to install a Teyes tablet-style head unit on a FAW Besturn X80 2018+, two additional parts are needed beyond the head unit itself: (1) a CANbus cable adapter (available on AliExpress for roughly 2,000 RUB (July 2024, RU market)); (2) an installation frame/mounting bracket sized for the X80 124125126.

    9-inch to 10.1-inch mounting-frame adapter: the factory head-unit opening is 10.1 inches. Owners retrofitting a 9-inch aftermarket unit (e.g. Teyes) need an adapter frame to bridge the size gap — example: Carav 22-1200, roughly 800 RUB (Aug 2024, RU market) from Ozon. Some adapter frames use self-tapping screws for mounting; shop around for the mounting style you prefer 127128129130131.

    False "drunk road" warning after head-unit replacement — CAN-bus wiring fix: after replacing the factory head unit (ГУ) with an aftermarket one, a false "drunk driving/road" warning light can appear on the dashboard. Fix: proper CAN-bus wiring connection — a video walkthrough identifies a specific pin sequence, connecting a yellow wire to a particular position on the brown connector. For one affected owner (Марина), this correct connection resolved both the false warning light and a parking-sensor malfunction simultaneously. Alternative approach if the exact pin isn't known: source the correct CANBUS connector for the vehicle, or add a specific bridging wire to the correct pins 132133134135136137138139140141142143.

    Audio interface adapter codes (unverified, 2020 car). For a 2020 car, the audio interface adapter codes RZ-YQBT11 and RW-NCL-0504 were given in reply to a question; the reply adds that the exact head-unit model must be specified when buying the adapter. The thread does not say which adapter type these are, so confirm with the seller 144.
