    Subwoofer installation — wiring and signal sourcing

    Tags: #audio · #head-unit · #wiring

    Prices and suppliers on this page are Russian-market (RUB, at the dates shown). Verify locally before relying on them in Syria.

    The stock FAW X80 head unit has no dedicated RCA subwoofer output ("no RCA/tulip jacks visible" on the factory unit) 123. Community-recommended approaches:

    • Active subwoofer (built-in amplifier): will play from any head unit, including the factory one — "the amp doesn't care what radio is in the car" 45. If there's no dedicated sub-out on the head unit, take the signal straight from the door speaker wires 67.
    • Signal without a dedicated sub-out: any audio channel — RCA/"tulip" or speaker-level wire — can feed a subwoofer if routed through a high-to-low frequency crossover/converter first 7. A suitable crossover/converter is sold on Ozon for ≈1,780 RUB (Jan 2025, RU market) (https://ozon.ru/t/q3K6Bew) 8.
    • Passive subwoofer: needs a separate amplifier and box design — treated by the community as "more of a pro/enthusiast setup," not recommended for a casual install 910.

    Community view: if you've never run a subwoofer before, try an active unit first — if it's not for you, an active sub is easy to resell; if you like it, you can upgrade later 11.

    Line-level converter: last resort (contrasting view). The advice above allows taking the signal from speaker wires through a converter 67. One member counters that a speaker-to-line converter should be the last resort: the signal should come from the head unit's rear output or a USB feed. A converter means disconnecting one speaker or door channel, and equaliser cuts then affect every channel, which makes tuning hard 12.
