    Restyled X80 speaker sizes and round conversion

    Tags: #fitment · #audio

    Oval size on the restyled X80: on the restyled X80 the factory speakers are ovals, and one member said the same four ovals fit both front and rear 12. One member gave the size as 6x9, but another corrected it to 5x7 (as written) 13. A third member said 6x9 can be forced in, but then part of the speaker ends up under the trim panel 4. The 5x7 figure matches the size documented in factory speakers and audio upgrades.

    Round rear speakers: one member fitted round speakers at the rear by making mounting brackets (крепления) for them, and said the round and oval units are the same size 567. Another member noted that 16 cm round speakers have a wider choice on the market than ovals 8.
