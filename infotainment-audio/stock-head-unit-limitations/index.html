    Factory head unit: versions and limitations

    Tags: #head-unit · #radio · #restyle

    Versions. The pre-restyling X80 is equipped with a monochrome radio display unit with no Bluetooth, no aux input and no backup-camera capability — only AM/FM radio and basic controls 12. See photo 10679 of the monochrome unit. The restyled X80 (2018+) has a larger touchscreen radio as standard equipment on the "люкс" (lux) trim, including Bluetooth connectivity 3. The stock Android 6 unit has a hidden service menu accessible with password 3369, which allows changing the launcher and screensaver 45.

    Media and phone limitations. The factory head unit can play video files 67, demonstrated by an owner playing a car scene video from a USB stick — but USB compatibility is inconsistent: a 32GB drive was not recognized while an 8GB drive worked 687. Supported media formats are listed in the printed owner's manual — one owner found this only after consulting the manual rather than trial-and-error 8. Bluetooth connectivity with modern Android phones is limited because the stock unit's Bluetooth protocol is outdated 910. Owners with the touchscreen unit appreciate its Bluetooth support 3. Navigation historically depended on Yandex Navigator integration, which stopped working / was deprecated in Russia 111213. Apple iPhone integration reportedly works better, but requires a paid Yandex subscription for full functionality 1415.

    Why owners replace it. The lack of Bluetooth, aux and camera support on the monochrome unit is a common reason to upgrade to an aftermarket Android head unit, despite the low cost of the original unit 16. Even with the restyle's touchscreen and Bluetooth, many owners still consider an Android unit for a larger display and additional features 3. Multiple owners cite frustration with Android integration overall and recommend replacing the stock unit with an aftermarket Android Auto / Apple CarPlay unit for better media/phone support 1718 — see the head-unit installation and CAN-bus card for how to do that.

    Aftermarket experience. Reverse-camera wiring (Tiyis SSS3K2): after installing a third-party Tiyis SSS3K2 head unit, one owner found the reverse camera activating automatically when shifting into Drive rather than only Reverse, since the camera's activation is tied into the vehicle's power circuit and the unit's automatic-engagement settings 19. Community troubleshooting: check whether the wiring was connected according to the official vehicle wiring schematic 20; another suggestion was to change the firmware/factory settings on the head unit itself, since some units have a configurable setting controlling camera trigger behavior 21. Long-term Teyes reliability: one owner installed a Teyes 4/64 head unit and, after continuous use, rates Teyes units as the best aftermarket option they have tried 22. Another owner notes they have wanted a similar full setup (including a proper CANBUS adapter alongside the Teyes unit) for 5 years but has been held back by budget/priority constraints — an indirect data point that the Teyes-plus-CANBUS combination is viewed by the community as the aspirational, more expensive-but-correct way to do the upgrade 23.

    MCU firmware update from USB (X80F radio, guide shared 2024). The community-shared update guide for the X80F radio works as follows: put only the file CR482XA_V1.56_20200224.MCU on a clean USB stick, plus at least one music file so the unit can identify the drive, and make sure no other file with the .MCU suffix is on it. Start the engine before updating. Plug the stick in; if the radio does not switch to USB playback on its own, select USB mode manually. In USB playback, press and hold [RADIO] + [|<<] + [>>|] for more than 5 seconds to enter update mode, choose MCU, then press the right button (SEL) to start. The update takes about 1 minute, shows progress on the display and the unit reboots by itself. If the ESP warning lamp stays on afterwards, select reverse gear with the engine running (the radio volume drops) and wait a few seconds; the lamp should go out 242526. See also ESP lamp false warnings and the CarPlay retrofit, which also relies on factory-unit firmware.
