    X80 "Sport" variant — Ukraine/Kazakhstan

    Tags: #trim · #radio · #hvac

    Prices and suppliers on this page are Russian-market (RUB, at the dates shown). Verify locally before relying on them in Syria.

    FAW produced a limited-edition Besturn X80 Sport variant sold only in the Ukraine and Kazakhstan markets — it was never released to the mass market in Russia or elsewhere.

    Distinguishing features: - An integrated touchscreen radio/climate-control unit — no physical buttons/dials for climate control, unlike the standard X80's separate climate panel. - Front fog lights. - A unique radiator grille design, different from both the pre-restyle and restyle grilles seen on standard X80s.

    Parts availability: the specific touchscreen radio/climate configuration used on the Sport variant was never released to the mass market and is reported as virtually impossible to source for standard X80 models — so this is a curiosity/rarity note rather than a practical upgrade path 1234567.
