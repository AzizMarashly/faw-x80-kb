    Wheels, tires & alignment specifications

    Tags: #wheels · #tires · #tire-pressure

    OEM wheel/tire specification: standard wheel diameter is 17 inches; factory tire size 215/60R17. Bolt pattern 5×114.3 (5-lug, 114.3 mm bolt circle). Center bore 67.1 mm. Confirmed by official documentation as factory-standard for all X80 variants.123 A separate report from the other community group confirms the same factory tire size, 215/60/17.4

    Tire size options in practice (varies by trim/generation/owner choice): 225/55/17 (common), 225/60/17 (factory option), 225/65 (considered oversized — noticeably reduces visibility and handling due to increased sidewall height). An 18-inch wheel option is confirmed available in the Chinese market (approximately 235/55 fitment); a newer 235/55/18 combination has also been tested by an owner. Lower-profile 215/70/16 tires are available, trading performance for ride comfort. General guidance: lower profile → sharper handling; higher profile → softer ride and more ground clearance.567

    Recommended tire pressure: 2.2–2.4 bar, with 2.2 bar the most commonly used setting in the community. The exact factory specification is printed on the driver's door jamb sticker. Pressure affects handling, comfort, and fuel economy.891011

    Spare tire: the rear-mounted spare uses a standard rubber valve stem with no pressure sensor. The TPMS (tire pressure monitoring system) only monitors the four road wheels, not the spare — simple rubber valve, no electronics.121314

    Factory wheel alignment specs (unloaded vehicle, from the factory manual): - Front — Camber −20′±30′ (converted from source; consult manual for exact degree/minute notation), Caster 5.93°±1°, wheel steering angle 6.53°, Toe-in 0±2 mm, inside wheel angle 36.5°±3°, outside wheel angle 30°±3°, vehicle height 435±20 mm. - Rear — Camber −1°20′±30′, Toe-in 2±2 mm, vehicle height 462±20 mm. - Vehicle height is measured from wheel center to wheel-arch edge.15
