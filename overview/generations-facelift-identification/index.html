    Pre-facelift vs. facelift identification

    Tags: #restyle

    The FAW X80 exists in two visual generations, referred to in the community by their Russian shorthand: Pre-Restyling / Pre-facelift (Дорестайлинг, до-рест) and Restyling / Facelift (Рестайлинг, рест).

    Timing: pre-facelift production runs through August 2018; facelift from August 2018 onward (some sources say January 2019). The styling shift is described informally as going from a "softer"/"kind" (добрый) to a more "aggressive"/"angry" (злой) look, with updated front/rear fascias and headlights, a larger/simpler center console change, and different infotainment capabilities. Because of the mid-year cutover, 2018-model-year cars exist in both versions depending on the production month.1234

    Visual identification between the two: headlight design, radiator grille shape, and rear light design differ. Pre-Restyling cars lack parking sensors. Community-recommended method to confirm which generation a given car is: compare photos against D2.ru or the manufacturer's specification sheets.5678910111213

    Door/panel interchange caution: pre-facelift (2017–2018) and facelift (2019+) door panels differ in internal structure and taillight mounting. Both generations share the same overall frame opening and body-line alignment, so doors may visually bolt on at the frame level — but internal reinforcement, mounting points, and decorative trim differ, and mixing generations produces an incomplete or mismatched appearance. Full door replacement requires matching the door to the body generation carefully.14151617181920

    Instrument cluster behavior (facelift only): on facelift-model automatics, the instrument cluster displays only the current gear-selector position (D, R, N, P) — not the actual gear the transmission has engaged. Pre-facelift models may behave differently on this point. This is described as a known display limitation on post-2019 facelift cars, not a fault.21222324
