    VIN location — windshield vs. door jamb

    Tags: #vin · #conflict

    Community members disagree on this topic — both positions are reported.

    Most FAW X80 units do not have a clearly visible or readable VIN embossed on the windshield, despite the owner's manual indicating one should be present there. Chinese-assembled vehicles instead have the VIN on a sticker on the door jamb. Russian-assembled vehicles (Autotors factory) may have VIN-placement issues or missing windshield embossing entirely. Some early-production units have a barely-visible window-frame area where the VIN should be, but it's either not embossed or heavily obscured. In practice, the VIN can be reliably found on the door-pillar sticker or the body plate. Vehicles have been successfully registered despite windshield VIN-visibility issues, though police acceptance of the missing embossing has varied by region/officer 1234567891011121314151617181920212223242526272829303132333435363738394041.

    ⚠️ Conflict: some owners did find a VIN embossed on the windshield (most likely early-production or Russian-assembled units), while the majority of Chinese-assembled X80s have no windshield VIN at all — only the door-jamb sticker. The restyling (facelift) model has an even lower likelihood of having a windshield VIN than pre-restyle units.

    VIN registration difficulty — illegible/missing body VIN: some vehicles lack a legible VIN code on the body in the expected under-hood location; in some cases the code is instead embossed under the driver-side carpet. The Russian vehicle-registration authority (МРЭО) may require a visible/readable VIN on the chassis to complete registration. Solutions reported: (1) locate and point to the alternate VIN location under the carpet, with the authority's permission to inspect it; (2) try registering at a different МРЭО office/location; (3) provide supporting documentation if available. One owner reports being unable to register the car despite having an accessible VIN code under the floor covering 4243444546474849505152.
