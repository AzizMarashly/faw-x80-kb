    Ownership essentials — warranty, towing, keys

    Tags: #warranty · #towing · #key-fob

    Prices and suppliers on this page are Russian-market (RUB, at the dates shown). Verify locally before relying on them in Syria.

    Warranty coverage on service: manufacturer warranty is 6 years from purchase date. Doing your own oil changes (DIY maintenance) is reported to void warranty coverage. Warranty is retained only if servicing is performed at certified service centers with proper documentation (receipts and service records). However, simple oil/filter changes at certified multi-brand service centers (not necessarily the dealership itself) with documentation may preserve the warranty — though dealer dispute over this is possible. Always verify with the dealer before using a non-OEM/non-dealer service provider.1234567

    Towing: the FAW X80 is not designed or approved for towing trailers. Neither the owner's manual nor the frame nameplate lists a towing capacity. Using the vehicle to tow a trailer is not recommended by the manufacturer, even if an aftermarket hitch (e.g., a SHERIFF-brand hitch) has been installed.89

    Key fob loss/replacement: FAW X80 key fobs cannot be duplicated with traditional key-copying methods. Replacement options: (1) buy a new key set from an official dealer with a programming service — expensive but fully supported; (2) use a specialist key-programmer (commonly found via Avito) who scans the vehicle's chip/VIN and creates a new key with reprogramming. Specialist cost reported at 6,000–12,000 RUB (RU market, March 2026) for a complete key replacement including all security features — requires professional chip-scanning and ECU-reprogramming equipment.1011121314

    Production timeline: FAW discontinued X80 production in 2020. There are no 2021+ model-year X80 vehicles in the market. Some 2020 stock vehicles were reportedly still being sold into 2022.151617
