    Power steering fluid — service procedure

    Tags: #power-steering · #gur

    Prices and suppliers on this page are Russian-market (RUB, at the dates shown). Verify locally before relying on them in Syria.

    Confirmed-compatible fluid brand — ZIC. ZIC-brand power steering fluid (PSF) is confirmed suitable for the X80's power-steering system. One owner referred to "our" original fluid when discussing it, suggesting an FAW-branded OEM PSF may also exist/be available, though this wasn't independently confirmed in the thread.123

    Level check and top-up. Power steering fluid level should be maintained at the proper mark on the dipstick/reservoir. If the level is at minimum but there's no visible leak, simply top up with the appropriate fluid — spec 3309 was cited for the correct fluid type.456789

    Full flush procedure (optional, not urgent unless fluid looks degraded). A complete fluid flush can be performed via the return-hose method: run clean fluid through the system continuously until the fluid discharging from the return line comes out clean. This can extend the system's service life. It is not necessary to perform a full flush immediately just because the level was low — only prioritize it if the existing fluid visibly looks degraded (dark, cloudy, or contaminated).456789

    Emergency repair for a ruptured return hose. If the low-pressure rubber return hose between the power-steering pump and the reservoir ruptures, all hydraulic fluid leaks out and the steering goes lock-solid immediately — this is a distinct failure from the level/topup guidance above and needs an emergency response, not a top-up. OEM replacement hose costs about 10,000 RUB (RU market, June 2026) with delivery and involves a wait; a faster/cheaper emergency option is a local RVD (hydraulic-hose repair) shop, which can re-crimp/reseal the old hose or fabricate a replacement for roughly 1,000–2,000 RUB, often same-day. One owner completed this repair for 2,000 RUB (RU market, June 2026) (re-crimp/reseal) and was back on the road the same day. Hose location: under the engine, connecting the hydraulic lines to the power-steering gear/rack.101112131415161718192021

    Fluid spec — Mobil 3309 / Type IV, or a matching multigrade synthetic/mineral oil. Restating and confirming the 3309 spec already noted above: Mobil 3309 or Type IV fluid is permitted for the GUR (power-steering) system. A synthetic/mineral multigrade oil ("мультик") is also acceptable as long as it matches FAW's approval for the spec. The same fluid can be used interchangeably in both the GUR and the transmission.2223242526

    Whining/howling noise from the engine bay — check GUR fluid first. A whining or howling noise from the engine bay, audible in the cabin at low RPM during acceleration and increasing during full wheel turns, has several possible causes, with GUR fluid level as the primary suspect: (1) low power-steering (GUR) fluid — check and top up first; (2) a worn bearing (alternator or another roller bearing); (3) a loose/slipping serpentine belt; (4) A/C-compressor-clutch engagement. First troubleshooting step: check and top up GUR fluid. If the noise continues afterward, inspect the GUR pump and steering rack for leaks. Reminder: the X80 uses hydraulic (not electronic) power steering, so this fluid-level check is a valid first diagnostic step for this noise pattern.27282930

    GUR fluid specification restated — ATF 3309 or Type IV, interchangeable. The X80's hydraulic steering system uses ATF 3309 or Type IV fluid — an older (3309, "vintage") and a newer (Type IV) specification that are compatible and can be mixed. When replacing the fluid, changing the entire volume gives the best results rather than a partial top-up.313233

    PSF = ATF confirmed, plus system pressure spec. Restating the ATF/PSF equivalence documented above with independent confirmation: power-steering fluid (PSF) is the same as the ATF used in the automatic transmission. System pressure spec, from a factory under-hood label: 127 kPa (18 psi).34353637

    Reservoir location. The power-steering fluid reservoir (бочок ГУРа) is located under the hood on the left side, behind the radiator.3839
