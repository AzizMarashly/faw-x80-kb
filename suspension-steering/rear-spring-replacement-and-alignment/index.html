    Rear spring replacement — spacers and alignment

    Tags: #springs · #mazda-6-compat · #conflict

    Prices and suppliers on this page are Russian-market (RUB, at the dates shown). Verify locally before relying on them in Syria.

    Some advice here concerns sub-zero winters and is less relevant in a hot climate.

    Community members disagree on this topic — both positions are reported.

    Parts note. Rear spring part numbers were found on kitzapchasti.ru at around 3,310 RUB (RU market, October 2024) each (left and right).1

    Updated pricing and sourcing (2025-03 report). OEM rear springs run approximately 3,000–7,000 RUB (RU market, March 2025) per piece. Available on OZON under part number 5CA028011 (https://ozon.ru/t/5VDGPXX). A quality aftermarket option from kitparts.ru costs 7,000 RUB (RU market, March 2025) per piece, described as matching original thickness. For reference, an earlier winter-2024 price point was 3,000 RUB (RU market, winter 2024, as reported in March 2025) for OEM springs — prices have trended up since.23

    Spacer recommendation. A 2 cm spacer installed with the springs is recommended by an owner with direct experience — without spacers the ride/geometry is reportedly "not great."456

    Alignment is mandatory after the job. After rear suspension spring replacement, a wheel alignment (сход-развал) must be performed, done with the car in a proper loaded/settled state and with the wheel size that will actually be used.7689

    Alignment shop debate — wheel-size claims. Some budget alignment shops incorrectly claim that only the OEM/factory wheels can be used for the alignment procedure. Community members with technical understanding push back: alignment angles (camber/toe) are geometric properties of the suspension and are independent of wheel type or thickness — this claim from some shops is considered a misunderstanding or an excuse for equipment limitations.10111213

    Recommended shops: Autopilot chain (Moscow) reported reliable by one owner for X80 alignment;8 a specific independent shop near Lyublino metro station (Moscow) also reported reliable.14 Reviews on Autopilot specifically are mixed ("двоякие") among other community members, so results may vary by branch.15

    Practical tip: when booking with any alignment shop unfamiliar with the X80, bring the correct alignment spec parameters (camber/toe/caster angles) with you — technicians will generally be able to hit the numbers if given them.1617

    Video reference — rear spring replacement in under 10 minutes (2024-03 report). A YouTube video demonstrating rear spring replacement on the X80 in under 10 minutes was shared in the group: https://youtube.com/shorts/fvVYyvg6faY?feature=share3 — useful as a quick visual overview alongside the spacer/alignment guidance above.18

    Mazda 6 GG/GH rear spring fitment — disputed, not a straightforward swap (2026-05 report, conflict documented). Community members tested/discussed using Mazda 6 GG (generations GG/GH/GY) rear springs as X80 replacements. The experienced-owner consensus: OEM X80 rear springs measure 13mm rod diameter, 350mm length. Mazda 6 GG springs have different geometry and are lighter-duty; the front springs specifically "from GG" are described as weaker than what the X80 uses (closer to the GH variant). Using GG springs with +40mm spacers may cause excessive ride height and poor handling. If a Mazda-based swap is attempted at all, community members lean toward parts from the GY (universal/wagon) Mazda 6 variant as the closer match — but this still needs verification via direct measurement or 3D scan of the actual spring parameters before assuming fitment. Conflict: two members (Jürgen and Denis) suggested Mazda 6 GH rear springs would work with spacers; a third member (Victor) disagreed strongly, citing sedan-vs-SUV suspension geometry differences and lower Mazda spring constants — Victor's position is that this is not recommended as a straightforward swap without engineering review.192021222324252627282930

    Spacers instead of auto-bumpers. KASPER says auto-bumpers (автобаферы) stiffen the ride and overload the springs unevenly, and recommends simple spring spacers (проставки) under the rear springs and strut spacers at the front; his father's car was raised about 4 cm this way.3132
