    Front springs — aftermarket brands and prices

    Tags: #springs · #price · #part-number · #fitment

    Prices and suppliers on this page are Russian-market (RUB, at the dates shown). Verify locally before relying on them in Syria.

    LINX SC1590 fitment (2025): LINX SC1590 front springs are compatible and fit stock X80 suspension mounts without modification. Recommendation: always buy new damper dust-boot seals (piston rod boots) when replacing springs, as they frequently tear during removal/installation. Context/date: 2025-05-02 1.

    SUFIX SZ1303 — confirmed direct fit, no modification: SUFIX-brand yellow front coil springs, part SZ1303 (also written SZ-1303, "20% stiffer" per the seller's listing), are confirmed to fit the X80 without cutting or welding. Reported pricing: ~3,600 RUB (RU market, July 2025) per pair as of 2025-07-28, sourced from the chitparts/kitparts online retailer. Perceived effect: raises the front stance and improves overall appearance. Conflict: this contradicts part of the coil-count note above where Mazda 6 springs were mentioned as an option with fit issues — SUFIX SZ1303 is the option the community confirms works without caveats 23.

    SUFIX SZ1303 — additional install confirmations (Oct–Nov 2025): further owners confirm the SUFIX SZ1303 yellow front spring noticeably raises the front and holds its height 4567, adding to the SUFIX SZ1303 fitment already documented above; one later report specifically reconfirms fitment on the restyle (facelift) body 8.

    Front spring cross-fit — further Mazda GG/GH dispute and compression data (Dec 2025): a fresh round of the GG-vs-GH cross-fit disagreement: one owner assumed the front springs sold as SUFIX SZ1303 are the Mazda GH part number 9; Sergey Arapkin disputes any blanket rule, noting that neither GG nor GH springs universally seat correctly on the X80 strut — fitment varies by the specific spring, not simply by generation 10. A seller/owner adds that Mazda 6 has roughly six different spring variants across its production run (consistent with the earlier model-year note on this card) and confirms, after considerable difficulty ("on the metro in a bag"), that the specific SUFIX SZ1303 spring does fit the restyle 11. Another owner reports riding on SUFIX springs since summer 2025 with no sagging 12, and a third notes they kept their old struts when fitting these springs, in hindsight considering the new struts they'd bought unnecessary 13. A press-compression test comparing stock and SUFIX springs found: stock front spring compresses at ~500kg load, SUFIX (yellow) at ~700kg — but on a vibration-table test the SUFIX springs actually rode slightly softer despite the higher static compression rating 14; one buyer reports being told at purchase that these springs were "reinforced" 15.

    Front spring and bushing sourcing — Moscow, KitParts/Ozon (Dec 2025): for sourcing front springs together with related bushings (upper control-arm silentblocks, rear longitudinal-arm silentblocks) in Moscow: SUFIX SZ1303 (yellow) front springs are suggested as fitting the restyle 16, and the community's pinned product links (Ozon) are recommended as a starting point for both springs and silentblocks 1718.

    Other replacement front spring options compared (March 2026): besides Sufix SZ1303 (~5,300–5,500 RUB/pair on Wildberries, RU market, most proven in the community with consistent positive reports), other 14mm wire-rate alternatives include OBK C5Z7971 (~7,500 RUB) and ST119063F (~8,000 RUB); Turkish-made 14mm springs are also available via Chip-Dip for ~5,500 RUB/pair. Higher-rate springs than stock may require rebalancing with matched shock absorbers to avoid an overly stiff ride 19202122.

    LYNX SC 1590 — alternative sourcing channel (May 2025): when official front springs were unavailable, one owner successfully sourced a replacement through the LYNX SC 1590 brand — a Chinese supplier who provided the complete original-spec spring assembly (spring plus spring cups) for 4,800 RUB (RU market, May 2025). The export restrictions cited as the reason for unavailability were described as temporary at the time 2324.

    Standard Springs ST119063F — 14mm aftermarket, requires press to install: Standard Springs ST119063F (cross-referenced to FAW part 5CA134011) are 14mm-thick springs (vs 13mm stock) with roughly 25% more coils than the factory spring. Price: 9,533₽ (April 2026). Installing them without a proper spring compressor is very difficult — cable ties alone are not enough; a pneumatic press at 2.5 atm is recommended, and a rubber support piece must be placed under the spring cup during installation 25262728.

    Sukhoi 1303 springs — thicker aftermarket, direct fit, ~3hr install per side: Sukhoi 1303 springs are a cross-brand front replacement option. They look visibly thicker than the OEM spring (roughly 13–13.5mm rod diameter), fit directly without modification, and can be installed one side at a time in about 3 hours per side. After installation, the suspension became higher and softer, handling improved on rough surfaces, and there was no noise at speed over bumps; measurement showed roughly 13mm rod diameter on both OEM and the Sukhoi spring 2930313233343536.

    Turkish 14mm-rod spring — stiffer, more body-roll control, more lift: a separate Turkish-made spring option with a 14mm rod diameter is noticeably stiffer than OEM, significantly reduces body roll in corners, and raises ride height more than the other aftermarket alternatives on this card 3738.

    SUFIX SZ1303 front springs — yellow, direct fit, ~1 year positive feedback: SUFIX-brand front springs (yellow-painted, part SZ1303) fit FAW Besturn X80 stock struts without modification. Multiple owners confirm successful fitment and roughly a year of trouble-free operation. They're slightly stiffer than OEM. Installation note: at the shop, these springs compress harder during fitting (need more force/load) than OEM, and afterward the front sits noticeably higher — some owners added rear spacers (+20mm) to level the resulting front/rear height imbalance (see the rear springs card for that spacer fix). Overall considered suitable for daily driving and moderate loads 394041424344.

    Front spring replacement pricing — dealer quote vs. LYNXauto alternative (June 2024): when a front spring breaks, KitZapchasti quoted 24,000₽ (June 2024) for a complete replacement kit/pair. LYNXauto SC-1590 springs (already documented above as a confirmed direct-fit option) are available on Ozon as a substantially cheaper alternative to that dealer-kit price 454647.

    Front spring cross-brand sourcing example — Mazda 6 (GH), JAPKO ZCJ3346A: as one purchase example (order history screenshot) for a front-spring cross-brand alternative, a JAPKO ZCJ3346A front spring for Mazda 6 (GH), 2007–2013 was ordered by an owner researching replacement options 48.

    SUFIX Mazda 6 front kit. A SUFIX front spring kit (2 pieces) for the Mazda 6 Atenza Saloon (GG) 2002-2008 2.3 was shown in a marketplace screenshot at about 5,593 RUB (RU market, Oct 2026), marked out of stock 49. Миша, who fitted these springs, says they are more than enough and have stood for two years 50; Тимур also fitted them and calls them good springs 51. Миша's one complaint is not the springs: his non-original struts are Mazda-analog Zekkert units, thinner than the FAW originals, and although new they cannot take full compression, so speed bumps must be crossed slowly or the rebound hits hard 52. Николай Цветков fitted rubber bump stops (буфера) and says they are coping with the problem so far 53.
