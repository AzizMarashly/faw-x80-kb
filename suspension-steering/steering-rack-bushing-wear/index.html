    Steering rack bushing wear and repair options

    Tags: #bushings

    Prices and suppliers on this page are Russian-market (RUB, at the dates shown). Verify locally before relying on them in Syria.

    Some advice here concerns sub-zero winters and is less relevant in a hot climate.

    Steering rack bushings (втулка рулевой рейки) can develop play over time, and owners report difficulty finding the bushing sold as a separate part — it is unclear whether it is available loose at all. If the rack is not leaking, knocking, or sticking, community advice is that repair is optional rather than urgent 123.

    Two repair paths if action is needed: (1) a specialized machine shop can turn/machine a new bushing surface onto the existing rod rather than sourcing a replacement part; or (2) replace the complete steering rack assembly outright. Which is more cost-effective depends heavily on local availability of a shop with the specialized machining capability — where none exists, full rack replacement may be the only practical option 23.

    Hissing noise at full lock is normal: a hissing sound from the steering rack area when the wheel is turned fully lock-to-lock is documented as normal X80 behavior, per an official dealer statement — described as a "construction feature." It occurs on a warm engine when the power steering system is under load and is not indicative of a malfunction 4.

    Taiwan-manufactured aftermarket racks and repair economics: Taiwan-manufactured aftermarket steering racks are reported to function adequately on Mazda-platform vehicles including the X80. Repair cost when seals fail runs 13,000+ RUB (RU market, April 2025); given labor cost and the lack of any warranty on a repair, full rack replacement is generally considered more economical than a repair. One owner's steering rack began leaking after extended use 5.

    Steering rack dust boot — part number: the steering rack dust boot (пыльник рулевой рейки) part number is 5AA032122, available from kitzapchasti.ru 6.

    OEM steering rack pricing and warranty (2025): a full OEM steering rack (sold as an assembly including tie rods/наконечники) costs approximately 24,000 RUB (RU market, July 2025). Supply is by factory order, typically arriving within about a month (sometimes faster); regional delivery via a courier service (e.g. Delovye Linii) adds roughly 1,000 RUB (RU market, July 2025). Aftermarket/Chinese racks run cheaper at 18,000-20,000 RUB (RU market, July 2025). Warranty terms are typically 1 year or 20,000 km per the service book (some components carry a shorter paint-coating warranty of around 10,000 km). Payment is generally 100% upfront, or a 50% deposit with the remaining 50% due on delivery to a local courier 7.

    Steering feel/character — heavier than modern designs, but responsive: several owners describe the X80's steering as notably tight/heavy compared to modern electric power-steering designs, while still transmitting road feedback well. One owner compared it to a VW Touareg, which he described as extremely heavy but smooth; by comparison the X80's steering is somewhat firm for the vehicle's weight/size but considered acceptable — some owners view this as inherent to the design (likely the Mazda-derived hydraulic rack) rather than a fault, with the steering responding noticeably to road imperfections through the wheel 89.

    Steering column vibration/dribble on cold start: the steering column shaft can vibrate or "dribble" at idle, especially noticeable on a cold winter start. Reported difficulty is low — accessing the shaft after removing the plastic shroud around the steering column takes about 15 minutes 10.

    Heavy steering, worse in mornings/cold, with grinding on turns — power steering pump failure suspected (Nov 2025): a case of steering becoming hard to turn, especially difficult on cold mornings, with a grinding noise during turns — persisting even after the steering rack had already been replaced. This points away from the rack itself and toward the power-steering pump (ГУР насос) as the likely cause; one owner with the identical symptom confirms replacing the pump alongside the rack resolved it. Recommended checks: replace the power-steering pump, and/or verify the fluid level in the pump reservoir first before assuming a pump failure 11121314.

    Heavy steering persists after rack AND pump replacement — break-in period, or inherent design heaviness (disputed, Nov 2025): extending the case above, one owner replaced both the steering rack and the hydraulic pump, yet steering remained heavy. Possible causes discussed: (1) new components may need a 100–200 km break-in period before feeling normal; (2) fittings/joints over-tightened during installation can restrict the rack's movement — on some designs (e.g. electric power steering) an over-tight bushing can nearly seize the rack and risk burning out the EPS motor, so installation torque matters; (3) the X80's design may simply be inherently heavier-steering than other cars due to the two lower control-arm geometry plus a comparatively weak hydraulic system — consistent with the "notably tight/heavy" characteristic already described elsewhere on this card. Recommendation: allow a break-in period before further diagnosis rather than immediately assuming a defective part 1516171819.

    Full steering rack replace-vs-restore decision, with pricing (June 2024): a fuller pricing picture for the rack decision already touched on elsewhere on this card — restoration: ~15,000₽ including removal/reinstallation labor, but the rack comes back noticeably stiffer than before, so a restored rack may not fully solve a "heavy steering" complaint. New: 20,000₽+. Genuine FAW part 5CA03296 available through KitZap and similar suppliers at ~11,267₽. A cross-brand alternative: Mazda 6 I steering rack, Motorherz art. R22101NW. Caution: some restoration-service listings advertise suspiciously low prices — verify the seller is actually supplying new stock, not a refurbished unit at a new-part price 20212223242526272829.

    Steering rack replacement cost — 17,000–17,340 RUB (RU market, April 2026), repair possible if only seals leak: another independent price data point: a full replacement rack costs 17,000–17,340 RUB via Ozon and Chinese suppliers ("Kitai.com"/kitzap-type sellers) — cheaper than the ~20,000₽+ new-rack figure quoted elsewhere on this card, suggesting price varies by supplier and should be shopped around. Repair may be worthwhile only if the fault is a seal/manifold leak with no play and no noise — in that case just the seals and piston would need replacing; finding a shop that actually does this specific repair (rather than just selling a swap rack) was reported as difficult. Conflict: one contributor considers a full replacement "safer" at similar total cost to a repair, given the difficulty of verifying repair quality, while also warning that sourcing a replacement rack itself is "a lottery" in terms of quality — so neither path is risk-free, and the choice may come down to whichever service is actually available locally 3031323334353637.

    Steering rack dust boot — part 5AA032122, Mazda 6 GG as an unconfirmed alternative: the steering rack dust boot (пыльник рулевой рейки) part number is 5AA032122, sourced via an Ozon listing. When a different owner separately asked for the dust boot's dimensions (rather than the part number) to find a compatible generic replacement, the only suggestion offered was to try a Mazda 6 GG boot — but the suggester explicitly did not know the required dimensions and this cross-fit was not confirmed by anyone in the thread; treat it as an unverified lead, not a confirmed fitment 38394041.

    Power steering pump noise/vibration at full steering lock — normal within limits, but don't hold it there (disputed in-thread): humming, vibration, and a slight increase in steering resistance when the wheel is turned to full lock and held is reported as normal — the pump is simply working at its maximum pressure limit, and the exact sound level varies between individual cars. Do not hold the wheel at full lock for an extended period — this can damage the pump over time even though the noise itself is expected. Conflict: one owner (Миша) called this "normal," while another (Василий) flatly disagreed ("this shouldn't happen, it's not normal") in the same thread. A third contributor clarified the distinction that resolves the disagreement: humming plus resistance at full lock is normal pump behavior, but grinding or crunching/clattering noises are not and indicate an actual problem needing service — so the two owners may have been describing different symptoms rather than genuinely disagreeing about the same noise 4243444546.
