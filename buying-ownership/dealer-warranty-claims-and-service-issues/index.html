    Dealer warranty claims and service trust

    Tags: #warranty · #dealer

    Prices and suppliers on this page are Russian-market (RUB, at the dates shown). Verify locally before relying on them in Syria.

    Warranty claims process: Warranty coverage varies by component (30,000 km for some parts, 60,000 km for others); the overall warranty period is 5 years 1. To claim warranty: present the vehicle with the defect to an official FAW dealership; the dealer submits the claim to FAW's representative office, which makes the final determination. Remote/photo-based decisions are not possible — an in-person inspection is required. Warranty details are listed in the service booklet, not the owner's manual. Dealers sometimes resist valid warranty claims 2.

    Dealer trust issues: Several owners report difficulties with official FAW dealers: service books are sometimes marked "electronic only" with no physical book provided 34; dealers have reported incorrect vehicle status/history to deny service claims 56; diagnostic equipment errors occur (e.g., one dealer's system misreported a wheel-speed sensor reading of 1,480 km/h) 78; general business practices at some dealers are described as untrustworthy 910. Recommendation: verify a dealer's standing on the official FAW website before purchasing or servicing there; independent shops are sometimes reported as more reliable 111213.

    Poor dealer workmanship — Samara official dealer (August 2024): A customer reports careless work after a dashboard leak repair at an official FAW dealership in Samara: panels were not properly reassembled/aligned, some parts were not fully reinstalled, and plastic trim may have been damaged in the process 1415. Steering-wheel alignment was also not done correctly, and the factory head unit's Bluetooth stopped working after the visit (suspected wiring disconnection). The customer's blunt characterization of the dealer's technical staff: "криворукие" (ham-fisted/incompetent) 16. Beyond the careless workmanship itself, the customer notes a dismissive attitude toward customers as an additional complaint 17. The dealership manager did apologize and offered to fix the issues on a return visit, and the customer planned to go back the next day to have the problems addressed 18 — illustrating that official-dealer staff unfamiliarity with Chinese-vehicle specifics can extend beyond diagnostics into basic reassembly quality.

    Finding a qualified independent electrician (Moscow): One owner asked the community for a recommendation for an auto-electrician in Moscow familiar with the FAW X80's electrical system 1920. Community response: the X80's electrical system is described as "standard, without quirks" — if a general auto-electrician refuses to work on it, that reflects unfamiliarity/reluctance rather than any special technical difficulty with the car itself 21. A separate owner of 4 years notes a general feeling that not every shop will take on their car, reflecting the same service-network hesitancy pattern seen elsewhere in the community 22.

    Dealer versus certified independent service station (STO, Taishet): One owner in Taishet services the car at a certified independent STO (service station) that stamps the service book and, he says, keeps the warranty valid; he has not visited a dealer in 60,000 km 23. Another owner prefers dealers, calling independent shops sloppy, and says dealer work costs more but he trusts it 24. The owners therefore disagree on whether a certified independent STO protects the warranty as well as a dealer does 2324.
