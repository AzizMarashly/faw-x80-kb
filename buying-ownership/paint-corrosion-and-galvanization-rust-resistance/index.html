    Paint, coating quality, and rust resistance

    Tags: #paint · #corrosion

    Coating-layer variation by assembly batch (Kaliningrad, June 2026): One owner reports their car — Kaliningrad-assembled, apparently from an early production batch, 9 years old at time of posting — has a full 4-layer protective system on the sills: zinc, cathodic e-coat (катофорез), primer, and paint, with zero rust anywhere despite the age 1234. Community discussion raised the question of whether this level of coating is universal or batch-dependent; one participant noted their own Kaliningrad-assembled car has a "crooked" (uneven-quality) build but is still holding up ["ничо пойдёт" — "it'll do"] 5. Another participant pushed back on generalizing "Kaliningrad assembly = bad," asking for the specific VIN to confirm assembly location before drawing conclusions, since not everything attributed to Kaliningrad assembly may actually be Kaliningrad-built 67 — illustrating that even within the community, attributing quality problems confidently to a specific plant is contested without VIN verification (see the assembly-quality card for the VIN-prefix check method).

    White paint corrosion pattern (2012–2017-era cars, June 2024): Multiple owners report rust/discoloration issues specifically on white-painted cars within 2–3 years, most visibly as a "рыжик" (rust-orange) spot in the lower-left corner of the rear hatch/trunk area — one owner who inspected three white examples at a dealership found the same lower-left rust spot on all three 891011. The pattern is reported as far less common on other colors; some owners half-joke that they wish they'd ordered gold/brown paint instead specifically to avoid this white-paint-linked corrosion issue, and there's community interest in gold-colored examples reportedly becoming available in the Russian market (including Tatarstan) as an alternative 121314.

    Long-term galvanization performance (2017 model, 109,000 km, July 2025): One 2017-model owner with 109,000 km reports no corrosion issues at all 15. A second owner states their car is fully galvanized and expects 3–5 more years of trouble-free driving on that basis, despite acknowledging some family skepticism about Chinese-brand durability generally 16.
