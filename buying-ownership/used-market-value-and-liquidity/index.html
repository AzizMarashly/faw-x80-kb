    Used-market value and resale liquidity

    Tags: #price

    Prices and suppliers on this page are Russian-market (RUB, at the dates shown). Verify locally before relying on them in Syria.

    January 2026 (RU market): Community pricing survey found the FAW X80 used market ranging roughly 840,000–1,350,000+ RUB. A pre-facelift 2019 manual "Lux" trim with 106,000 km sold for 840,000 RUB (October 2025 purchase, RU market) 1. A 2020 facelift model with 92,000 km (second owner, max trim, automatic) sold for 1,220,000 RUB (Jan 2026, RU market) 234. A 2020 facelift automatic with 40,000 km (second owner) sold for 1,350,000 RUB (October 2025, RU market) 5. General market consensus: fair baseline price is around 1,000,000 RUB (Jan 2026, RU market) with ±200,000–300,000 RUB variation depending on condition; a review of 49 Avito listings found prices ranging 600,000–1,699,000 RUB, with participants noting even AI pricing tools estimate the model around 1 million RUB; asking prices above ~1.4 million RUB are considered overpriced by the community 6789.

    April 2026 (RU market, Moscow): A 2019 model with roughly 99,000 km sold for 1,140,000 RUB. Initial asking price was 1,200,000 RUB (April 2026, RU market) with no buyers; the seller dropped to 1,170,000 RUB, then accepted 1,140,000 RUB after an in-person inspection 1011. Market observation: few X80s are available for sale overall; most listings are pre-facelift cars with 300,000–400,000+ km, while facelift models with lower mileage (~100,000 km) are considered the optimal used-car pick. A separate buyer reported paying 1,150,000 RUB in January 2026 (RU market) for a car with 79,000 km, broadly consistent with this pricing band 121314.

    April 2025 (RU market): A 2020 X80 with 40,000 km, in ideal condition with original paint, priced around 1.5 million RUB (automatic transmission) 1516. Prices had declined compared to summer 2023, when comparable units with 20,000–50,000 km sold for 1.55–1.65 million RUB 17.

    April 2025 (RU market), liquidity concerns: A 2019 X80 with 46,300 km was valued at 1,266,000 RUB. Market view: the Chinese FAW brand is considered illiquid on the Russian secondary market — asking prices range 1.5–1.7 million RUB, but selling time is unpredictable, and some buyers decline immediately upon learning the brand is FAW (Chinese, rare in Russia). Quick-sale scenarios may net 30–40% below asking price; holding for the right buyer interested in the specific vehicle's condition is recommended over expecting a rapid sale 1819.

    December 2025 (RU market): A restyle X80 with 106,000 km actual mileage (55,000 km shown at listing time), top trim, white interior, fitted with an aftermarket phone/remote autostart system, was listed around 350,000 RUB privately; an official dealership offered only 130,000 RUB trade-in value (for resale at 140,000–145,000 RUB). Private-sale estimates for a pristine example in this range: 140,000–150,000 RUB (Dec 2025, RU market) depending on negotiation 202122. For comparison, the FAW T77 (a restyle-era competitor) was noted for a responsive pedal and more interior options, but criticized for its turbo/robotized transmission and weak warranty 2324. Photos and video (media 36181, 36194) show the X80 interior and owner commentary from a T77 comparison test drive.

    September 2026 (RU market, Saint Petersburg): A used 2020 automatic X80 with 50,000 km was advertised for sale in Saint Petersburg at 1,400,000 RUB 25.
