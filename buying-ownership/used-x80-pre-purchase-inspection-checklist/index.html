    Used X80 (2017-era) — pre-purchase inspection checklist

    Tags: #buying-guide · #glass

    Prices and suppliers on this page are Russian-market (RUB, at the dates shown). Verify locally before relying on them in Syria.

    Community checklist for buying a used 2017-era X80: 1. Check paint quality carefully, especially the rear door — a common defect area 1. 2. Inspect for water ingress in the cabin: check floor mats, door seals, and sunroof drainage 2. 3. Valve-cover gasket leaks and condensation are common; budget for gasket replacement 3. 4. The stock head unit is problematic — plan on replacing it 4. 5. No factory soundproofing is a common complaint 5. 6. Rear wheel arches use fabric fenders rather than metal, giving inadequate mud protection — check underneath for rust/corrosion 6. 7. Automatic-transmission fuel consumption is typically 11.6–11.8 L/100 km, varying with driving style 4. 8. Always check the VIN before buying. A real case: a car presented for sale had flood damage (water pooled in the front-passenger footwell); the seller claimed it had been fixed, but water intrusion can hide electrical and drivetrain problems that are hard to diagnose later — get a professional inspection before buying, especially in private sales 78. 9. Water and corrosion checks (community-repeated, 2026): Look for water in the front-passenger and driver footwells and under the spare tire in the trunk; test rear parking sensors (parktronic) by engaging reverse — they should either work correctly or beep without fault codes; listen for engine ticking (possible stuck hydraulic tappets); check for corrosion above the rear license plate under the trim visor and along the bottom of doors (especially the 5th/tailgate door) — this rust is reported as nearly universal on older X80s (owners from Moscow and St. Petersburg confirm it) and is treated as cosmetic rather than structural, so a low price can still justify buying 91011121314151617. 10. Tire-pressure sensors (TPMS): if the sensors have not been replaced by the time of sale, assume 99% of them have already failed (they have roughly a 6-year battery life) 18. 11. No reliable way to check accident history at inspection time — buyers note there is no tool to confirm whether a car has been in a collision; some dealerships lend a paint-thickness "tester" (тыкалка) on request to check for bodywork/repaint, and community members recommend asking the dealer to test the car in front of you, or sourcing your own tester from Ozon beforehand 192021. 12. A fogged/condensed window can be present at time of sale from the dealer. One owner purchased an X80 with a fogged/condensed window already present at time of sale (as of 2024-07-22); it has been driven normally for several months since with no functional issues arising from the defect 22. A second owner reports the same experience — a fogged window does not affect vehicle operation 23.

    Positive aspects reported: spacious interior, attractive styling, and an overall reliable platform.
