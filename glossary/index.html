    Glossary

    The owner communities are Russian-speaking and use Russian shop jargon. This page explains the terms that appear on the site.

    Term Meaning Notes
    AKPP / АКПП Automatic transmission (автоматическая коробка передач) The X80 uses the Aisin-derived TF-61SN 6-speed automatic.
    MKPP / МКПП Manual transmission
    ШРУС (SHRUS) CV joint (constant-velocity joint) on the drive shaft Outer = at the wheel, inner = at the gearbox.
    ДВС The engine (internal combustion engine)
    ЭБУ / ECU Engine control unit
    ГРМ Timing drive (belt or chain and its tensioners)
    ГУР / ЭУР Hydraulic / electric power steering
    жабо (zhabo) Cowl panel — the plastic trim between the windshield and the hood, where the wipers sit Its drains clog and let water into the cabin — a frequent X80 topic.
    шумка (shumka) Sound deadening / noise insulation material
    мухобойка Hood deflector ("bug shield")
    сайлентблок Rubber-metal bushing (suspension arm / subframe bushing)
    стойка / амортизатор Strut / shock absorber
    рычаг Control arm (suspension arm)
    ступица Wheel hub (usually with the wheel bearing)
    колодки / диски Brake pads / brake discs
    помпа Water pump (coolant pump)
    сальник Oil seal (crankshaft, camshaft, axle seal)
    прокладка Gasket
    форсунки / свечи Fuel injectors / spark plugs
    катализатор / лямбда Catalytic converter / oxygen (lambda) sensor
    масложор Excessive oil consumption ("oil eating")
    рестайл / дорестайл Facelift (post-2019 restyle) / pre-facelift X80
    подрамник / лонжерон / порог Subframe / chassis rail / sill (rocker panel)
    дворники / бачок Wipers / reservoir (washer or expansion tank)
    кулиса Gear shifter linkage
    фаркоп Tow bar (trailer hitch)
    ТО Scheduled service (maintenance visit, e.g. ТО-1, ТО-2)
    ОЖ / антифриз Coolant / antifreeze
    Drive2 / Drom / Avito / Ozon / Exist / Autodoc Russian car forums (Drive2, Drom) and online shops/marketplaces for parts
    пыльник Dust boot — the rubber bellows on a CV joint, ball joint, tie-rod end or shock absorber
    сапун Crankcase breather / PCV vent (engine or gearbox)
    отбойник Bump stop — the rubber buffer that limits suspension travel
    подкрылок Wheel-arch liner (inner fender plastic)
    печка Cabin heater (heater core + blower)
    ролик (натяжной / обводной) Belt pulley — tensioner or idler roller of the drive belt
    коленвал / шкив коленвала Crankshaft / crankshaft pulley (harmonic damper)
    опора двигателя / подушка Engine mount
    КПП Gearbox (any type; see AKPP / MKPP)
    рейка (рулевая) Steering rack
    наконечник / тяга Tie-rod end / tie rod (steering)
    шаровая Ball joint
    развал / схождение Wheel alignment — camber / toe
    суппорт Brake caliper
    подшипник ступицы Wheel (hub) bearing
    концевик Limit switch (door / hood / brake-pedal micro-switch)
    гофра Flexible pipe — exhaust flex section, or a corrugated hose/duct
    патрубок / тройник Coolant or air hose / T-fitting
    проставки Spacers — lift spacers (suspension) or wheel spacers
    молдинг / накладка / уплотнитель Trim strip / cover panel / weatherstrip seal
    магнитола / приборка Head unit (radio) / instrument cluster
    обманка Spacer/emulator that hides a fault — e.g. an O2-sensor "cheater" after catalyst removal
    ДАД / ДМРВ / ДПКВ / ДПДЗ Sensors — MAP (manifold pressure) / MAF (air flow) / crankshaft position / throttle position
    разборка Vehicle dismantler (breaker's yard) selling used parts
    вилка (рычаг) "Fork" — the community's name for the X80's curved lower control arm
    жаба / гастроном Community slang — "жаба" = cowl panel (see жабо); "гастроном" = any parts shop
