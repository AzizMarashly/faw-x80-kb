    HVAC blower motor — failure and repair

    Tags: #hvac · #part-number · #mazda-6-compat

    Prices and suppliers on this page are Russian-market (RUB, at the dates shown). Verify locally before relying on them in Syria.

    Symptom pattern. The HVAC blower doesn't activate on a cold startup, but may kick in after the engine/cabin warms up, then runs with fluctuating power — pulsing on and off at intervals rather than running steadily.1

    Root cause. The X80 blower motor is a brushless type (electronic contacts, not a traditional brush/commutator design). When the contacting surfaces oxidize or start arcing, the motor becomes intermittent: hesitant to start, and pulsing even after warm-up.23 Historically, some service centers have replaced only the brush/contact parts when available, which restores function for a few hundred hours of operation before the issue tends to recur — full motor replacement is recommended for a durable fix rather than repeatedly re-servicing the contacts.3

    Access and removal — no glove-box removal required. The motor sits under the dashboard, below the glove box. Reported procedure: (1) glove-box removal is not necessary (it's an optional, easier-access alternative if you prefer to remove it); (2) access from underneath by kneeling in the footwell; (3) unfasten the engine-cover/undertray panel below the glove box; (4) remove 3× 10 mm bolts holding the motor assembly; (5) disconnect the motor's electrical connector.456 Total install/removal time is roughly 30 minutes.7 Caution before buying a replacement: some owners found the motor's contacts still functional after full disassembly and cleaning — suggesting contact oxidation, not a dead motor, may be the actual fault in some cases, worth trying before spending on a new unit.2

    Intermittent stop-restart failure — motor inspection. A separate but related failure pattern: the blower stops intermittently and won't restart for extended periods. Likely causes: worn brushes/contacts, water ingress into the motor, or seized bearings — consistent with the brushless-contact root cause above but presenting as a total stall rather than pulsing. Diagnostic order: (1) check the fuse and relay first, (2) if those are fine, disassemble the motor to inspect and clean the brush/contact surfaces. Possible water-ingress source: leakage from the cooling/ventilation system into the blower housing.891011

    Blower motor part numbers and cross-compatible alternatives. Original FAW Besturn X80 blower motor part number: GV7D61B10, priced roughly 4,000–8,500 RUB (RU market, December 2025). Dealers quote around 8,500 RUB for the original; supplier "KitZapchasti" reportedly stocks it for 4,000 RUB (RU market, December 2025).1213141516 Mazda 6 GH blower motors are reported as potential alternatives — part numbers RF1853, PFN305, LFH25LF, GS1D61B10 — but the rotor diameter differs: Mazda rotors are narrower than the X80's original, so the Mazda unit generally cannot be dropped in whole; only the motor housing is reported cross-compatible if you reuse the X80's native rotor. Two further alternative part numbers were mentioned with unconfirmed compatibility: HB100K26A and 5GA061B10.171819202122232425
