    Engine mounts — aftermarket brands and verdicts

    Tags: #engine-mount · #aftermarket · #patron

    Prices and suppliers on this page are Russian-market (RUB, at the dates shown). Verify locally before relying on them in Syria.

    Some advice here concerns sub-zero winters and is less relevant in a hot climate.

    Related cards:

    • Engine mounts — vibration and replacement
    • Symptoms and inspection

    • Prices and sourcing by date

    OEM Mazda hydro-mount vs. aftermarket rubber — cost and durability tradeoffs. OEM Mazda hydro-mount costs roughly 10,000+ RUB, with a noted risk of counterfeits on the market. A cheaper aftermarket rubber-only mount (sourced via the Drive/Драйв forum community) holds the engine but provides less vibration damping than a hydro-mount — it uses no hydraulic fluid, rubber only, vs. the Mazda mount's hydraulic fluid + rubber construction. Even a quality Mazda OEM hydro-mount can sag by ~47,000 km, causing noticeable engine vibration felt on the hood/windshield, especially in winter. One owner's 2020–2021-vintage Mazda mount was still performing well at time of posting. Net assessment from the thread: a rubber-only mount runs quieter than a sagging hydro-mount, but offers less vibration isolation than a fresh hydro-mount12345678910111213.

    Patron rear mount — excessive softness, no real improvement. One owner installed a Patron-brand polyurethane rear motor mount and found it excessively soft for the X80: it produced noticeable engine vibration and clutch chatter under acceleration, and did not deliver the expected noise-reduction benefit — an initial improvement felt after installation turned out to be a placebo effect coinciding with a fresh oil change. Recommendation from this report: keep the original (OEM) rear mount rather than switching to the Patron polyurethane version14.

    Right-side hydro-mount vs. third-party rubber, and left-mount sourcing — restated. A separate thread reconfirms the general mount guidance: cheap third-party rubber-only mounts (2,500-4,000 RUB, RU market, Apr 2025) are considered less effective and soften quickly. The right-side mount (гидроопора) should be an OEM Mazda hydraulic-damping unit — third-party hydro mounts are considered unreliable. The left mount should also be OEM Mazda original, or the mounting sealant block replaced as a less common alternative. The rear mount typically performs fine even as a non-hydro part. One owner's original rear mount had sagged to just 2mm clearance at 48,000 km; vibration or clunking on startup is cited as the tell-tale symptom of mount failure15161718192021.

    Aftermarket mount quality varies — Tzer Li rated poor. Engine mount quality varies significantly by manufacturer. Tzer Li mounts are rated poor quality by community feedback among the aftermarket alternatives tried. Separately, at 48,000 km, OEM mounts can show only minimal visible wear despite the owner still experiencing vibration symptoms — i.e. visual inspection alone may not catch a failing mount2223.

    Aftermarket mount comparison — Patron vs. TZerLi vs. ST-GJ6G-39-060E. Further aftermarket engine-mount comparisons: Patron-brand mounts show poor durability, sagging within 5,000-6,500 km with a characteristic noise. TZerLi (when in stock) performs better, reportedly lasting 60,000+ km with a significant vibration reduction versus OEM. ST-GJ6G-39-060E is a hydraulic-type mount that performs well and effectively eliminates vibration. Separately, a LYNXAUTO ME-1068 mount lasted 5 years before needing replacement242526272829303132333435363738. Conflict: vibration-elimination effectiveness is reported to vary — TZerLi users report near-complete elimination, while Patron users report residual vibration plus sagging.

    Right mount — OEM lifespan, and a detailed alternatives roundup. OEM part 5CA139060 (right/front mount) has a reported lifespan of roughly 45,000 km before failing. Alternatives compared: Patron PSE3325 lasted 9 months (13,000 km) with slight sag but retained meaningful vibration reduction. Torque AS1111 (cross-referenced OEM number GP9A39060D, sourced from Mazda 6) fits and works well, costing 4,400 RUB (RU market, Feb 2026). Kortex KEM144 (catalogued for Mazda 6 GG 02-07 1.8L) also fits. Avoid the cheap BM213906Z option. Overall, the part is compatible across Mazda 6 GG and FAW B50, consistent with the cross-fit options documented on the part-numbers and cross-fits card39404142434445464748495051.

    Engine hydro-support (mount) failure at ~50k km — Stellox replacement, alternative Golf-mount weld fix. On a separate sample X80, the engine hydro-support (гидро опора двс) failed at roughly 50,000 km. Replacement part: a Stellox-brand mount for approximately 1,870 RUB (RU market, Aug 2024) from online parts stores. An alternative workaround mentioned in the same thread (echoing a practice reported among Mazda owners): welding in a VW Golf engine mount with a matching gasket. After replacement, the owner reported a dramatic improvement — near-total elimination of engine vibration at idle52535455.

    Right mount = Mazda 6 GG, and how Mazda-type replacements feel (Sep-Oct 2026). Victor 🇷🇺 states that the X80 right mount is the same part as the Mazda 6 GG one 56. He fitted a Patron aftermarket mount in May 2025 after the original failed; it stayed firm, settled only about 2-3 mm, and the gap was 7.5-8 mm (three 10-ruble coins fit, four do not) 57. In September 2026 he fitted an original right mount, found its pin travel very stiff compared with Patron's soft rubber, and the car became "firm, as I like"; he also pushed hard rubber into the gaps of the lower mount's bushing (сайлентблок) 57. A silent video shows engine deflection with the new right mount and two other mounts at 70,000 km (parking brake on, first gear then reverse, clutch released) 58. He says almost all Mazda GG replacement mounts are soft: with Patron the engine rocked forward and back in neutral when he blipped the throttle, and Patron and VTR as a rear mount are "rubbish"; a Patron hydro mount is fine if a replacement is urgent, while the original is excellent 596061. Contrasting experience: Prosto Zuev fitted a Mazda cushion in the lower position and says the car is like new, with no jolts 62.

    Where the community disagrees

    • Owners disagree on which aftermarket engine-mount brand best eliminates vibration: TZerLi users report near-complete elimination and 60,000+ km life, while Patron users report sagging within 5,000-6,500 km and residual vibration. Not resolved within this data batch.
