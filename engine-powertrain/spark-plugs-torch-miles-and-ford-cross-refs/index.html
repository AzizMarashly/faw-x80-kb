    Spark plugs — Torch, Miles and Ford cross-refs

    Tags: #spark-plugs · #torch · #price · #cross-reference

    Prices and suppliers on this page are Russian-market (RUB, at the dates shown). Verify locally before relying on them in Syria.

    Part of the spark-plug series — overview: Spark plugs — options, prices, reliability. See also OEM spark plug specifications and Torch spark plugs — codes, listings, gap rules.

    Cross-brand / interchangeable options. - TORCH QH5RIU-13 and QH6RIU-11 are reported interchangeable with OEM; QH6RIU-11 has a lower heat rating than QH5RIU-13123. - Iridium options reported compatible: TORCH QH6RTIP-13, TR6B-13, ILTR6A-13G, PLTR6A-10G, LTR6AI13; one owner also cross-referenced a Ford part number 1202935 as an equivalent, sourced via AliExpress with reportedly good quality matching OEM spec4. - AMD AMD.PL116 also mentioned as a compatible option56.

    Prices (RU market, OZON, 4-plug sets unless noted): - TORCH QH5RIU-13: 1,496 RUB on sale (regular price 3,671 RUB, 59% discount), as of 2024-11-057. - TORCH QH6RIU-11: comparable price to the above, same listing period8. - TORCH QH6RTIP-13 iridium (2-8 pack): 1,444 RUB with a 36% discount, as of 2024-09-11, RU market, on AliExpress; reported fast delivery4. - Official dealership TORCH plugs: 950 RUB per plug in-store, or 800 RUB per plug if pre-ordered (November 2024, RU market)910.

    TORCH QH6RIU-11 — Ford cross-references. TORCH QH6RIU-11 iridium plugs also serve as replacements for several Ford part numbers: 41-988, 12565996, 12622561, 41-109, 1787829, 4M5G12405AA, L3BD-18-110, and L3YD18110. Available on AliExpress for 1,343 RUB (RU market, Jan 2025) with a 30% discount. Users report reliable performance: 5,000+ km and 30+ days of continuous operation without issues11121314.

    Additional verified options — Torch and AliExpress iridium double plugs. (1) Torch brand — standard choice, confirmed working by multiple owners. (2) AliExpress iridium "double" spark plugs (2,899 RUB, RU market, February 2025) — one user reported 15,000 km of operation with no issues after roughly one year of use. Both options are reported reliable151617181920.

    Miles ALSP067 — restated with pricing and a plug-count caution. A separate discussion reconfirms Miles ALSP067 iridium plugs as a recommended replacement, priced around 3,104 RUB (with an Ozon card discount, RU market, 2025); one owner reported 5,000 km trouble-free running on this set. The source specifically warns against buying a 4-plug set (e.g. TORCH QH5RIU-M3, shown for comparison in the same listing) by mistake — double-check the plug count needed before ordering212223242526.

    TORCH QH5RIU-13 restated with dimensions and long-term result. TORCH iridium plugs model QH5RIU-13 (analog to ILTR5A-13G) are confirmed compatible: M14x1.25 thread, 25mm reach. Rated good quality by the community and available on Ozon272829. A separate long-term report: changed at 80,000 km, still performing well at 130,000 km (the original factory plugs had lasted to 80,000 km). Warning: many iridium plugs on the market are counterfeit even at low prices — verify quality before buying; over-tightening during installation can damage the plugs and the spark-plug wells3031323334.

    QH5RIU-13 as a direct swap for stock QH6RIU-11 — same 1.1mm gap. Restating and refining the OEM/alternative pairing described on the Spark plugs — factory spec and gap disputes card: original spark plugs are QH6RIU-11 (conical seat). The community-recommended swap is TORCH QH5RIU-13, which keeps the same 1.1mm gap. The iridium version is available from Ozon for roughly 5,000+ RUB. Replacement takes about 15 minutes with a 16mm plug socket and an 8mm socket for the coil-pack bolts (consistent with the socket sizes on the Spark plugs — installation, torque and tools card)353637383940.

    Torch QH6RIU-11 — pricing and availability restated. Torch QH6RIU-11 is confirmed as suitable, still with the same "-11" gap-code marking seen on old/original plugs. Ozon listing price around 1,000 RUB per plug (RU market, April 2026) individually, with only single-plug (not 4-pack) listings available on Ozon/Wildberries at the time; buyers were waiting for a set listing rather than buying 4 singles4142434445.

    Torch QH6RIU-11 iridium +U, ordered via Emex — smoother/quieter after install. A Torch QH6RIU-11 iridium set (U-shaped side electrode, "+U" iridium type) was ordered through Emex Marketplace; Kitzap-stock alternatives were rejected as not matching the correct spec. Confirmed fitment for the X80, sold as a 4-piece set. After installing in May 2026, the reporting owner described the engine as running quieter, smoother, and with reduced knocking46474849.

    TORCH QH5RIU-13 — cross-vehicle listing and confirmed fitment. A specific Ozon/marketplace listing for TORCH QH5RIU-13 iridium plugs (analog ILTR5A-13G, 25mm reach) markets them as fitting Ford, Chevrolet, Mazda, Opel, Volvo, and Suzuki models (Focus, C-Max, Fiesta, Fusion, Mondeo, Galaxy, Captiva, Vectra, Zafira, Astra, Grand Vitara, Escudo, Tribute, CX-9, S40) in addition to the X80. Multiple owners in the group confirmed having installed this exact plug with good results, including one who installed it "just recently" and reports it working perfectly50515253545556. When asked what differentiates plug variants, the answer given was: heat rating (calorific number) and electrode gap57.

    TORCH QH6RIU-11 — an early, independent recommendation with 30,000+ km reports. An earlier, independent thread recommends TORCH QH6RIU-11 iridium plugs specifically, giving heat rating 6 and gap 1.1mm, describing them as superior to standard plugs and better than the cross-fit alternative QH5RTIP-13. Multiple users in this earlier thread report 30,000+ km of trouble-free service — consistent with, though earlier than, the later lifespan-variance discussion covered on the Spark plugs — lifespan and wear symptoms card5859606162.

    TORCH QH6RIU-11 iridium — 4-pack purchase confirmed, with product photo. A separate owner (Che Guevara) confirmed purchasing and installing TORCH QH6RIU-11 iridium spark plugs sold as a 4-piece pack, reinforcing the reliability track record already documented for this part number on this card63.
