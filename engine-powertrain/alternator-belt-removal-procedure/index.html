    Generator (alternator) belt removal procedure

    Tags: #alternator · #price · #part-number · #conflict

    Prices and suppliers on this page are Russian-market (RUB, at the dates shown). Verify locally before relying on them in Syria.

    Community members disagree on this topic — both positions are reported.

    To access and adjust the generator (alternator) belt tensioner and remove the belt, the front wheel and wheel-arch liner/fender guard must be removed — belt removal cannot be accomplished without this access. Multiple community members confirm this is the standard procedure for the X80.12

    Replacement interval — inspect, don't just calendar-replace. Recommendations vary: some sources online suggest replacing at 70,000+ km per manufacturer interval to preserve warranty, while experienced owners recommend inspecting the belt for visible wear/cracking instead of blind calendar replacement. One owner at 100,000 km had the belt inspected by a mechanic and it was confirmed to still be in good condition, with the mechanic recommending against replacement. Visual inspection is considered more reliable than mileage alone.34567

    Belt and roller part numbers. Serpentine (drive) belt part number: 5DA015909. Tensioner pulley (натяжитель/натяжной ролик): 5CA015980. The idler/bypass pulley (обводной ролик) is also sold separately. Recommendation: replace the belt and both pulleys together as a set. Prices vary significantly between retailers — some bundle at a higher cost, while individual parts can be found cheaper on other platforms.8910111213

    Belt length — manual spec vs. practical experience (2025-10 reports). The manual specifies a 6PK2260 belt, but owners report this exact length is very difficult to install. Multiple lengths were tested by the community: 2260 (per manual, very difficult), 2263 (extremely difficult — installation may require crankshaft rotation or special techniques, or cutting the old belt off), 2275 (fits with some difficulty but works reliably — reported as +2–3 mm longer than 2260), plus less-common reports of 2277, 2271, and 2270. Net community conclusion: despite the manual calling for 2260, 2275 is the length that works better in practice.14151617181920212223

    Installation procedure and challenges (2025-10 reports). Without a service pit, tension the roller by placing a wrench on the roller bolt and pulling it toward the rear of the vehicle, then fit the belt. A common trick for stubborn fits: press the belt against the crankshaft pulley and rotate the engine by hand using a socket wrench on the crankshaft bolt. Access is easier with the right front wheel removed and the plastic wheel-arch liner taken off. Warning: a belt that sits skewed on the pulleys (not properly centered) will cut/shear over time — verify correct tracking before finishing the job. Removing a stuck belt sometimes requires cutting it or using special tools.24252627282930313233343536373839404142434445

    Real-world install at 120,000 km. One owner (Vova) replaced the serpentine belt at 120,000 km: the manual-spec 2260 would not fit, so he used 2275 instead. After 10,000 km on the 2275 belt, it was working properly with no issues.464748495051525354

    Belt brand/pricing data points (2025-10). Dongil 6PK2263: ~850 RUB (RU market, Oct 2025), but installation difficulty is extreme (per the length notes above); also seen at 1,160 RUB at Auto-Doc, and 1,400 RUB for the Daico brand. Dongil 6PK2275 is separately recommended after several successful installs, available on Ozon.555657212223 Quality caution: one owner reported durability concerns with a JD-brand 6PK2275 belt purchase — in the same period, that owner's lower front control-arm (left) bushings and ball joint failed at only 30,000 km, and stabilizer-bar links failed at 15,000 km, describing overall poor durability from that supplier batch.56

    Mileage-based belt/pulley inspection (2025-10/11 reports). At around 300,000 km, one owner's serpentine belt was still holding up and only the tensioner/idler pulley (~36,000 RUB, RU market, Oct 2025) and the alternator needed replacement (cost not specified, October 2025). Belt tension should visually sit centered on the tensioner's reference marks — check all rollers and tension settings whenever replacing the belt. A separate owner replaced the belt at ~280,000 km due to noise; the replacement belt was of a different type/construction than the original, possibly reflecting a local sourcing/quality issue rather than a design problem.585960
