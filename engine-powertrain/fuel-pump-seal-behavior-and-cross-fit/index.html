    Fuel pump seal — behavior and cross-fit

    Tags: #fuel-pump · #bosch · #fuel-filter

    Prices and suppliers on this page are Russian-market (RUB, at the dates shown). Verify locally before relying on them in Syria.

    Seal ring is critical for pressure delivery. A missing or improperly installed O-ring seal on the fuel pump module (located on the pump housing threads) prevents the fuel system from building pressure — the pump itself runs and pumps fine, but no fuel reaches the injectors and the spark plugs stay dry1. Diagnosing this specific case took some searching before the owner realized they'd forgotten to install the seal ring; once installed, the car started immediately234. Lesson: be careful when disassembling the pump module, and check reference photos/diagrams before reassembly5.

    Activation timing — momentary vs. continuous. The X80's fuel pump activates only momentarily during an engine-start attempt, rather than running continuously whenever the key is on (as it does in some other vehicles)16. This is reported to match the behavior of Toyota Corolla, Mazda, and other Japanese-market vehicles using Bosch pumps, several of which use this same start-only activation pattern; the original X80 pump is a Bosch design dating to ~2014178910. The reporting owner notes this is also documented elsewhere online for Japanese vehicles, and states two other members separately confirmed the same behavior on their own cars11. In short: at the moment of start, the pump builds pressure and injectors begin working6. Note: per the candidate analysis of this thread, this "start-only" behavior was itself debated — one view holds the pump runs only on engine start (as described above, matching Japanese-market cars), while another view in the community holds the pump should run on ignition-on for pressure buildup, matching the behavior of typical fuel-injected vehicles generally. The reporting owner's own experience, corroborated by two other members, matched the start-only pattern.

    Cross-brand fuel pump compatibility. - A VAZ 2108 fuel pump was asked about as a possible substitute; one reply confirms it matches by geometry, though performance/output specifics weren't confirmed in this thread121314. - Separately, after the factory (Russian-market) fuel pump failed after about 2 years in service, one owner replaced it with a BOSCH pump originally purchased for a Kia Ceed back in 2020 — reported as a working cross-fit, though long-term durability wasn't yet established at time of posting15.

    Part numbers and a VAZ 2110 cross-fit confirmation. The FAW X80's fuel pump assembly part number is FA031335XAP1; the individual pump-only part number is FA031335XP1. As an alternative, the VAZ 2110 fuel pump (roughly 800 RUB, RU market, Jan 2025) is reported to fit 1:1 and function equivalently. The associated fuel filter part number is FC0160950 (roughly 340 RUB, RU market, Jan 2025). Some owners prefer the Bosch motor found inside the OEM module over aftermarket replacement modules; a Mapco pump with an integral fine filter was also suggested as an option16171819202122.

    Mapco 22760 — a specific fuel pump listing, from the second Telegram group. A separate community (FAW BESTURN X80 group) points to a specific fuel pump product: Mapco 22760, available from the EMEX e-commerce retailer — consistent with the Mapco-brand fuel pump already mentioned as an option on this card, giving it a concrete part number23.

    Fuel filter and pump listings, seals (Sep-Oct 2026). A Kittank fuel filter FC0160950 is labelled on the box for the B50 and X80 (photo, Sep 2026) 24. When refitting the fuel pump or filter fitting, fit new rubber seals and make sure they are fuel-resistant (бензостойкие) before assembling the pump 25; Serega's kit also contained a thick seal in addition to the thin one (photo: a small dark rubber O-ring) 26. Arсhi 77 gives the pump article FA031335XAP1 labelled "for B50" and sees it at already "20" at Kitzap (unit not stated, presumably thousand RUB, Oct 2026) 27. Руслан Кот saw this pump at 3,000-5,000 RUB on Yandex (RU market, Oct 2026) and Arсhi 77 says Ozon is about the same (RU market, Oct 2026), noting that all sellers use one article number; fitment on the X80 was not confirmed 2829.

    Where the community disagrees

    • Community disagreement on fuel pump activation: one view holds the pump operates only briefly on engine-start attempt (matching Japanese-market cars like Corolla/Mazda); another view holds it should run on ignition-on for pressure buildup, matching typical fuel-injected vehicles. The reporting owner's own car, and two others who confirmed, matched the start-only pattern — not a fully resolved community consensus.
